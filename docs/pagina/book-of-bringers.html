Book of bringers

Itens fonte original

Description

The book of bringers is a lore book which is 4 chapters long and is meant to be a research book about fullbringers.

Small summary of each chapter

  • Chapter 1 is about humans with powers but with extremely limited potential.
  • Chapter 2 is about finding ancient readings talking about Fullbringers.
  • Chapter 3 is about encountering someone using Clock and talks about their ability to slightly manipulate the environment.
  • Chapter 4 talks about human souls and how they interact with hollow souls.

Location

On the counter for ASR's Cafe.

Chapter 1

I've been doing some research on humans with abnormal abilities, they seem to get this strength through raw determination and will to get stronger, however their potential is extremely limited... Maybe there is a way to surpass this limitation?

Chapter 2

I found ancient readings in the regards to those who are called 'Fullbringers', humans who were attacked by hollows during birth, they grew to inherit powerful abilities to draw out the souls of objects and transforming them... How interesting.

Chapter 3

I managed to find a human with the ability of Fullbring, though it was through a dangerous confrontation from a distant side of Karakura, I saw him turn his watch into a lethal object when he felt my presence. it is odd though, when we fought, it seemed he could really only do this to his watch, he can alter the environment ever so slightly however not to the level that he is able to with his watch, I tried to question him but he escaped, how unfortunate, however I now know they may exist in our current generation.

Chapter 4

Based of my findings from that encounter, I studied more on the connection between human souls and hollow souls, it seems that when a human encounters a one on one confrontation with a hollow, their soul starts to grow, just enough to allow the individual to manipulate objects with his soul, however the occurrence is so rare that it has yet to be seen by Shinigami's, Quincies, or Arrancars, that is why there is no information on them.
