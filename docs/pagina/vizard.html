Vizard

Vizard


Vizard is an end-game mode obtained by using an Impure Hogyoku. The mode allows the Shinigami to put on a Hollow mask, which buffs the user while it is worn. Becoming a Vizard also allows you to obtain some usually Hollow exclusive moves. Vizard is a pre-requisite for Vasto Rage and Horns of salvation.

Description

You activate your Vizard Mask by using your Skill by the same name (pictured to the right), instantly acquired upon using an Impure Hogyoku and becoming a Vizard. You cannot use your Vizard Mask with 0% Vizard Bar. You charge it by dealing damage and you get passive charge by being In Danger. When in Vizard Mode your charge will passively drop but you can still charge it by dealing damage. Your Vizard Bar depletes passively while out of combat as well. As a Vizard you gain the ability to use a select few Arrancar skills, such as Kick Flurry, Agarre Cero and Cero. (Vastocar skills acquired as a Vasto Lorde, such as Cero Oscuras, as well as Arrancar exclusive Legendary Skills like Gran Rey Cero and Ruthless Fatality CANNOT be used and will not appear when your Soul Shell is opened, also known as choosing an ability after leveling up. Also, you do not gain the ability to use Garganta as a Vizard. Your portal ability will remain Senkaimon.)

Vizard mode gives a big boost to all your stats. In exchange for that however, you get debuffed when you're not using the mask. 15% stat debuff, to be precise. The boosts while wearing the mask are listed below.

  • Mastery 0: Returns the buffs taken as a Vizard, plus a 6% boost normally, 12.5% boost when the bar is above 50%.
  • Mastery 1: Returns the buffs taken as a Vizard, plus a 9% boost normally, 15.5% boost when the bar is above 50%.
  • Mastery 2: Returns the buffs taken as a Vizard, plus a 12% boost normally, 18.5% boost when the bar is above 50%.
  • Mastery 3: Returns the buffs taken as a Vizard, plus a 15% boost normally, 21.5% boost when the bar is above 50%
  • Prodigy is unable to be used with the Vizard Mask ability.

If the mask is used when the Vizard bar is below 50% you get an extra 3% buff. If the mask is used when the Vizard bar is above 50% you get an extra 6.5% buff.

Obtainment

Use Impure Hogyoku as a Soul Reaper at B rank and above

The level requirement is 165.

Mastering

Your Vizard Mask can be trained to last longer by fighting Raian Hirako, the Vizard Trainer. Fighting him costs 200 Yen, and like fighting any other trainer, your Yen is not returned if you are defeated, nor when you win. When It is fully mastered, the outline on it's mode bar will turn purple. You can see how far you are by the Mask Skill Mastery, 3 Bars means you are really close, or fully mastered.(note: you can fight Raian Hirako first a few times prefer 15 times, then when you use Impure Hogyoku you will have max Vizard, I tested it trust)

Fighting your inner spirit on strength testing mode is the best way of grinding vizard mastery. If you defeat your spirit, even if you grip it, it will get back up. There is no way to stop the inner spirit from rising after being knocked. However, if you just spam M1s or abilities that deal fast damage, like glacier swords, you can immediately hit them as they get back up. (not patched mb all)

You can also use a pure hogyoku to max out your vizard.

If you are unhappy with your mask (or Vasto Face) you can reroll it for 30 Robux or with Product Essence from Kisuke.

To change your Mask Color you need to reroll your Shikai Weapon, also known as Shikai Cosmetic (when you reroll your Shikai Weapon you also reroll your Shikai Hilt/Vizard Color), you can buy a reroll for 30 Robux or with Product Essence from Kisuke.

Trivia

  • When Vizard Mask is used the bar will not go up even when in combat, it will only go up purely based on damage (even when its used). Meaning if you hit a player and it damages them (ex. 1000 DMG) then your bar will instantly be full or it will go up very high.
  • There used to be an way to max your Vizard Mastery, which was to go to your Spirit World and ask to spar with your Zanpakuto Spirit to test your strength. The method is to defeat your Spirit and down them, then repeatedly almost-grip it by pressing the grip button then pressing it again to stop it.
  • Another method is that you can fight Raian Hirako before getting Vizard so you can fight him without the 15% debuffs

Pros

  • Has a very good use in PvP if used correctly, for example putting the Aegis Reiatsu Crystal on the Vizard Mask move would give you a shield for a moment, and putting Wind Crystal on the Vizard Mask will boost you forward, letting you escape attacks
  • Gives a considerably large boost in stats, especially if your Vizard Mask mastery is completed.
  • Full Mastery Vizard Mask depletes slowly. (180 seconds)
  • Easy to increase the bar.
  • The Bar can be filled easily by using barrage-type skills that hit multiple times within the move (such as Aerial Assault, Outspeed, Kick Flurry, etc.)

Cons

  • When used, it uses a large amount of Reiatsu.
  • The Vizard Bar depletes faster when you are being hit.
  • When your Vizard Bar fully depletes and your mask crumbles, a large amount of Reiatsu is taken.
  • Getting Impure Hogyokus is a very tedious task.
  • Just being a Vizard decreases all of your stats by 15%, which is why it is recommended to not use any Impure Hogyokus until you have obtained all three that you need. This debuffs does not apply if you have Horn of Salvation.
