Explosion Bankai

"The whole point of fighting is that you don't want to die! That you'll be killed if you lose! If you throw your life away in battle, where's the point in winning?!"

Description

Explosion is one of the common Shikai elements that Soul Reapers can utilize in Peroxide. Explosion's Bankai passive leaves behind 5 falling detonation wisps during the beginning and end of a Flashstep, turns the player's counter into an uptilt, and creates an explosion after a wallbang for extra damage.
