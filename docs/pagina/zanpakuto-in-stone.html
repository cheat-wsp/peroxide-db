Zanpakuto In Stone

Shinigami fonte original

On top of the tallest building in Karakura there's an interactable yellow glowing Zanpakutō In Stone.

When interacted with, it only says
"Don't even try, only a worthy soul can pull me"

Choosing the "I am worthy" dialogue option will teleport you to the popular Roblox Game Adopt Me!


‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎Theory

It's speculated that the Zanpakutō has something to do with Beyond Bankai, which is a Transformation from the game Bleach Brave Souls.
