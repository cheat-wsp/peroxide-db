Gym Bouncer

Outros fonte original

Description

The Gym Bouncer is a NPC located in Karakura Town next to Urahara's Shop thats allows the player to go into the Karakura Gym to increase your stats with the price of 50 each time.

Features

The Gym Bouncer runs the Karakura Gym, which doesn't allow weapons drawn inside. If the player tries to draw their weapon inside the Gym, they will kicked out and told to not draw their weapon out. The Gym Bouncer sells Gym Memberships that allows you to stay inside the gym for 200 seconds or 3 minutes and 20 seconds. The player can increase their stats to a max cap of 30 points, and they can train stat stars, which provide an extra 33% stat exp gain for each one. The maximum amount of stat stars a player can gain from this Gym is 3, while Advanced Dojos in other worlds provide 5 stars maximum.

A membership in advanced dojos costs 250 instead of the regular 50.

The Gym Bouncer's counterparts are as follows:

The Soul Society Dojo Vendor in Soul Society, the Quincy Dojo Vendor in Wandenreich, and the Arrancar Dojo Vendor in The Alley in Hueco Mundo.
