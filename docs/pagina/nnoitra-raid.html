Nnoitra raid

Bosses fonte original

Nnoitra is one of the possible Raid Bosses in the game.

It can be spawned using Time Splitter/Charged Time Splitter in Hueco Mundo.

He sometimes spawns in -S Ranks within Las Noches with a 20% chance, or has a 50% chance to be any Hueco Mundo Time Splitter raid boss.

Nnoitra has unique sword-style moves and a built-in passive of **infinite Hierro**. His outfit adds a new passive to the game that makes Blut Vene and Hierro have a cooldown on taking Reiatsu, making them more effective against multi-hit abilities.

Nnoitra also has a passive where he guard breaks on the final M1 (same guard break the old combat system had).
