Tohjin

Outros fonte original

Tohjin is an NPC that is located in Las Noches, Hueco Mundo. He resides on top of the stairs near The Monolith, and he is essential for players who are doing E to B rank Invasions. If an Arrancar interacts with him, he will tell the player that they do not need his services. If you are a Non-Arrancer race and give him a Storm Arrancar Guard, he will proceed to give you a disguise, telling you that you do not need to know where they placed the body.

The disguise consists of a fake Menos Nose that is placed on your character's face, which makes all Storm Arrancar Guards passive to you, until you attack them. You cannot remove this disguise unless you rejoin or leave the server, and this disguise will not work on any Hollows or the Arrancars that spawn from The Storm. The disguise will not work for the Espada NPC that spawns from B-rank Invasions either.
