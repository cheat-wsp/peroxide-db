Quincycar

Quincy fonte original

A Quincycar is a Hybrid Race obtained by Arrancars.

Description



Quincycar are Arrancars that became Quincy. As Quincycar you lose EVERYTHING Hollow related, even your respective Arrancar Buffs but not Arrancar Mask and Hollow Clan (You cannot use Hollow Clan Skills, to get a Quincy Clan you need to Clan reroll),

As this race is cosmetic, it is recommended to use either a Menoscar or an Adjuchar instead of a Vastocar to save time.

You will keep

  • 5-10+ Max Level
  • 20-80% Exp Boost
  • Your Mask (You can reroll this)
  • Your Clan and it's respective boosts until you reroll your clan. (NOT MOVES)
  • Your Segunda "points"

YOU WILL NOT KEEP

  • Any Arrancar/Vasto Exclusive moves (INCLUDING ANY EXCLUSIVE LEGENDARY SKILLS AND CLAN MOVES)
  • Any Arrancar benefits (e.g The stat and health boost)
  • Any Mode progress (If you were max Segunda you need to re-max Vollstandig)
  • Hakuda


You will be just like a normal Quincy, able to obtain:

  • Schrift
  • Vollständig
  • Cybernetics
  • Schatten (Teleport to Wandenreich)
  • Quincy Skills
  • Spirit Weapons and Schrift Weapon
  • Quincy Names and Clans
  • Etc.

Cosmetic

You keep your Arrancar Mask (and Menos Nose if you are a Menoscar-Quincy) on top of usual Quincy Cosmetics.

Obtainment

Prerequisite: Level 100 and +100 Reputation with Wandenreich.

  1. Become a level 100 (or higher) Arrancar
  2. Get +100 (or more) Reputation with Wandenreich (You can get Positive Reputation by giving Full Soul Gourds to Gunther)
  3. Finish a Quincy Incursion to open the schatten portal to teleport to Wandenreich because of this, it is vital to turn in extra soul gourds so your Reputation doesn't go under 100 (500 is more preferable due to you being able to get your Segunda straight away). You could also pay Gunther to open a portal to Wandenreich.
  4. Go to Yhwach by following the path and going up the stairs
  5. Talk to him, if you meet the requirements, he will turn you into a Quincycar

Additional Notes

  • Going this route for Quincy is faster than grinding Human-Quincy (due to the 20-80% exp boost).
  • You can get your Segunda and then turn to a Quincycar, skipping the parrying part of the Automaton fight.

Tip: If your going for the Quincycar route, progress as a Arrancar first. Get to max level then get 100+ rep with Wandereich. I suggest getting Segunda Etapa first (not full mastery) so you keep your points.
