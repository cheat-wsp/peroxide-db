Shady Arrancar

"The Sasuke Of Hueco"

The Shady Arrancar is a npc that you can find in the hollow base (invasion place in hueco mundo) and in the alley next to the principal road for the bridge. You can rejoin an organisation by the group where you are or create one in the end.

Clicking "Can You Clear My Past?" and paying ¥500 resets your reputation.

You can check your Vasto Lorde progress by speaking to the Shady Arrancar. By speaking to him he’ll tell you specific phrases based on your progress:

  • ”Not a single bit of progress.” 0-100 kills
  • “Not close, not close at all…” 100-250 kills
  • “You have a really long road ahead of you… kid.” 250-500 kills
  • “You have a long road ahead of you… kid.” 500-1000 kills
  • “You’re getting close, that’s for sure” 1000-1250 kills
  • “You’re making some progress, keep going.” 1250-1600 kills
  • “Almost there, just a little closer.” 1600 – 1700 kills

(Once you reach over 1250 kills, you will stop losing all kill points up to that point. (Do note you can still lose kill points, just that the number cannot go under 1250.)

  • Normal hollow counts as 1 kill
  • Red Eye counts as 3 kills
  • Menos/GIllian counts as 5 kills (12 if it is Ancient)
  • Adjuchas counts as 10 kills (25 if Ancient)
  • Vasto lorde counts as 50 kills (75 if Ancient)
  • Storm Arrancars count as 100
  • Eating dead hollows counts as 1 kill
  • Using an Impure Hogyoku or pure hogyoku gives you 1000 kills.
  • Incursion counts as 100 kills (any race)
  • Dying makes you lose 60 kills
  • Combat logging makes you lose 120 kills

Addon/Extra: Storm Arrancars only count after 55% Damage | Vasto Lordes only count after 30% Damage (This may not be true, as there have been experiences where the counter increased without the percentages listed. Further testing required.)
