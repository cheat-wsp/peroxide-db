Mode Abilities

Mecânicas fonte original

Mode Abilities, otherwise known as Shikai Elements, Resureccions, Schrifts, and Fullbrings are abilities that a player can spawn with upon creating their character or reaching a certain stage of evolution, most notably evolving from a hollow into an Arrancar. Players, depending on their race, can use product essence or robux to reroll their Shikai Element, Resureccion, or Schrift. Codes are recommended if you want more spins.
