THE (unofficial) LORE OF PEROXIDE

maybeic4y

Long ago, the world was connected by the flow of Peroxide, a mysterious and powerful energy that shaped realms, spirits, and the very laws of existence. Over time, this energy fractured the world into distinct territories, each governed by its own rules and inhabited by warriors, monsters, and spirits who could channel its power. Among these fractured lands, humans, Soul Reapers, Quincies, and Hollows all carved out their own domains, but the balance of power remained fragile.

In the central city of the Shattered Realms, everyday life is punctuated by the rhythm of portals, bus stations, and marketplaces where energy and information flow constantly. The bus stations serve as hubs for travelers, though their reach is limited: portals exist only for areas permitted to a player’s race. Humans, Hollows, and Soul Reapers can travel freely to most common locations, but Hueco Mundo, Soul Society, and the Wandenreich remain restricted. Only Quincies can enter the Wandenreich freely, unless others perform a rare and dangerous ritual to breach its boundaries. The bus stops include a Hospital for the wounded, a Bridge Entrance connecting districts, Sickles Chicken, which is always mysteriously closed, a Gym for training, a Urahara Shop stocked with essential tools, and the primary Bus Terminal, where paths to other realms converge.

The Shattered Realms are alive with strange phenomena. Every thirty minutes, Terumi from BlazBlue manifests somewhere in the world, bringing chaos and danger to any who cross his path. During the holiday season, the city transforms under the influence of Peroxide itself, raining presents from the skies and bringing brief joy to all races before the constant struggle resumes. The flow of energy also manifests in abilities and transformations: Schrifts, Resurrections, Bankai, Voltstanding, Segunda, and Shikai are the pillars of power, defining every encounter and rivalry. Despite constant player demand, the developers have never added new Shikais, making mastery of the existing arsenal a lifelong pursuit.

The world is divided among multiple factions and guilds. Guilds serve as social and combat hubs, where alliances are forged and rivalries erupt. Hueco Mundo, home to the most dangerous Hollows, remains a forbidden territory for most, while the Soul Society maintains its structured order, training new Reapers and defending the balance of the realms. The Wandenreich, exclusive to Quincies, is a realm of secretive power, with portals locked to outsiders and filled with deadly trials for anyone attempting entry.

The city itself is a maze of activity. The Gym echoes with training strikes, bursts of energy, and the clash of weapons. The Urahara Shop is always crowded with adventurers seeking rare items or tools to enhance their Peroxide abilities. Meanwhile, the bus stations act as central veins of travel, but even here, mysteries abound: Sickles Chicken never opens, though rumors say the owner experiments with strange energies late at night. Portals to other realms glow faintly, each keyed to the soul of its traveler, and only the worthy may pass.

Legends tell of the Peroxide Core, a hidden source of unimaginable power at the heart of the Shattered Realms. Only the most skilled Reapers, Hollows, and Quincies can even hope to approach it, and its presence explains the chaotic patterns of energy that sweep the world. Guilds, factions, and lone warriors alike vie for fragments of this power, unlocking Bankai, Resurrections, and rare Schrifts, each adding to the delicate balance—or tipping it into destruction. Even transformations such as Voltstanding and Segunda are manifestations of raw Peroxide energy, allowing their wielders to challenge entire factions single-handedly.

Despite the constant struggle, life persists. Festivals appear during holidays, rare encounters like Terumi’s spawn shake the world, and guilds maintain social order, offering quests and challenges. Every bus station, shop, and portal tells a story, from the quiet halls of the Hospital to the echoing steps in the Gym. Every race has its privileges and restrictions, and the restricted realms of Hueco Mundo, Soul Society, and Wandenreich create tension, mystery, and constant conflict. In the world of Peroxide, mastery is not just about power—it’s about knowing the rules, the gates, and the rare moments when the world itself bends to your will.

Here, alliances can crumble, rivals can betray, and the rain of presents can be followed by the deadly spawn of Terumi within minutes. Guilds fight to control key locations, players train tirelessly in Gyms or experiment with Schrifts in secret, and every portal carries the potential for glory or disaster. In a world where new Shikais never appear and the established powers rule supreme, the true test is endurance, cunning, and the ability to survive the chaos of Peroxide.

The Shattered Realms never rest, and neither do its adventurers. Beyond the living chaos of portals and recurring threats like Terumi’s sudden manifestations, there exists a network of trade and turmoil that keeps every player on edge. In the heart of Karakura Town stands the Trade Hub, a bustling battlefield of bartering and deal‑making where warriors exchange rare gear, skill reset items, pure Hogyokus, and even Terumi’s Laugh Essence — all while keeping a wary eye on rival guild members who might snatch a deal or ambush unsuspecting traders.

Trade here isn’t a simple click‑and‑sell; you must negotiate face‑to‑face, often in the middle of a crowded plaza or near the Bus Terminal, where everyone gathers after their daily quests and invasions. Valuable items like storm delvers cloaks and pure Hogyokus fetch steep prices, and experienced traders can often be found haggling over a late‑night rain of Christmas presents — Peroxide’s quirky event where seasonal joy floods the city streets with wrapped rewards, even as guild ambitions spark war.

Far from the relative safety of trade routes, deep in the wilds of Hueco Mundo lies Storm Delving — a treacherous gauntlet where only the daring and prepared survive. To enter, players must seek out the Storm Delve NPC and pay a fee of 500 Yen before being flung into a raging tempest with other combatants. Here, Arrancars lurk in packs, awaiting intruders with their chilling Ceros and lightning‑fast strikes.

Storm Delving is both PvE and PvP in nature: waves of Arrancar enemies are unleashed one after another, each stronger than the last, while other players hunt you for your hard‑earned treasure. Victors can extract with chests full of Perk Stones, Product Essence, accessories, and rare relics — but only if they outlast the storm and its dangers.

Product Essence is itself a prize coveted by warriors of all paths. It allows players to reroll aspects of their character and abilities through Kisuke Urahara, the enigmatic shopkeeper whose wisdom guides many toward their destiny. With Product Essence, a Soul Reaper might reroll their Shikai’s element or cosmetic look, an Arrancar might reshuffle their mask or Resurreccion, and even Quincy can reroll their Schrift or Vollständig wings.

These rerolls are precious because, despite the endless grind, the developers do not add new Shikais, making every existing one cherished and long‑mastered by veterans. As a result, much of the meta revolves around obtaining the best possible variant of a Shikai, Resurreccion, Schrift, or Segunda Etapa you can, then enhancing it through battle experience and storm rewards.

The city’s bus stations — Hospital, Bridge Entrance, Sickles Chicken (forever closed), Urahara Shop, Gym, Bus Station 1, and Bus Terminal — serve as daily crossroads where players rest, trade, train, and prepare for their next dive into danger. Each has its role: the Hospital patches up the defeated, the Gym forges strength and mastery of Bankai and Segunda, and the Urahara Shop stands as a shrine of progression, knowledge, and cosmetic change. Portals at these stops link to Karakura Town and other major hubs, but Hueco Mundo, Soul Society, and the Wandenreich require exclusive access — either by race or by rare rituals only whispered about in guild halls.

Guilds remain the heartbeat of this world. They organize Storm Delve raids, control trade routes, start invasions into enemy territory, and compete for notoriety in Organization Wars, where factions fight under banners to claim leaderboards and reputation rewards. These guild conflicts shape the very politics of the Shattered Realms, influencing where portals open, who controls the Trade Hubs, and even which storms bring the deadliest Arrancar elites.

All the while, adventurers chase the horizon: mastering Bankai timers, perfecting Schrifts and Resurreccions, gathering Product Essence from invasions and storms, and dreaming of one day unlocking that elusive, perfect loadout. They trade, fight, bargain, and survive — for in Peroxide’s ever‑shifting shadow, only the strongest, wisest, and most daring endure.

The Shattered Realms were never just about paths and portals — they became arenas of legend and combat, where stories were made in Storm Delves, Time Raids, and hidden corners filled with danger. Word of the Raid Bosses spread like wildfire. Not merely figures of lore, these towering foes manifest when adventurers trigger Time Splitter events or distorted time bubbles. The most feared among them was Coyote Starrk, an Arrancar whose strength scaled with anyone bold enough to challenge him. Starrk did not simply fight — he unleashed three distinct phases of relentless power in battle, forcing players to adapt or be overwhelmed. Those who survived could receive Starrk’s Outfit, Pure Hogyokus, or even Essence of Starrk — rare rewards coveted for their immense stat bonuses and crafting potential.

But Starrk was not alone. Other raid bosses like Ulquiorra and Byakuya stalked the fields, remnants of past eras brought through fractures in time. Their defeats granted accessories imbued with power — items that conferred stat boosts, elemental affinities, enhanced reiatsu regeneration, and other battle advantages. These loot drops were so sought after that guilds would schedule raids weeks in advance, coordinating teams of Soul Reapers, Hollows, Quincies, and Fullbringers to take turns striking blindsided blows.

Amid this chaos stood the many NPCs who shaped the world’s story and progression. Kisuke was the first mentor for aspiring Soul Reapers, initiating adventurers into the path of reiatsu and zanpakutō mastery. Yhwach awaited in the distant halls of the Wandenreich Castle, a Quincy monarch whose powers were whispered in guild halls — if a Vastocar reached level 170+ and spoke with him, an ancient ritual could transform that warrior into a Quincycar, unlocking forbidden Quincy potential and granting access to unique abilities. Gunther, stationed near the fountain and General’s shop, sold the building blocks of power: cybernetics parts, Time Relics, Strange Ore, and keys to further progression. Herupa stood by the quest boards, granting stat increases to those who proved themselves worthy in tasks across realms. Nurse Ari tended to those wounded in storm and raid, wiping flaws, removing unwanted transformations, and altering appearances for a new path. Gargan could open portals to treacherous lands like Hueco Mundo and the Menos Forest, where Arrancar spawn in feral packs. Shady Arrancar whispered your kill count as your Adjuchas form grew stronger, marking your notoriety in the world. Hobo Monk offered stat resets for those seeking a different build, and The Trimigami at Shini Cuts was the unlikely stylist of the battlefield, changing looks with haircuts that carried lore secrets of their own. Laqura and Ken tested warriors in training alleys, sparring partners for those seeking raw supremacy. And Shukuro Tsukishima, hidden behind the Rabbit Cafe, was the gateway for Fullbringer obtainment, bringing an entirely new path into the war of abilities.

As if storms and raids were not enough, a new force emerged to challenge the balance — the Fullbringers. Once dormant echoes of ancient power, they awakened through the cracks in reality. These warriors harnessed the essence of the world itself, unlocking abilities that let them distort matter, manipulate energy, and bend the battlefield to their will. X‑Cution, once relegated to whispers in outlying alleys, became a faction of its own, with leaders and proxies guiding Fullbringers into conflicts once dominated by Soul Reapers and Quincies.

Trade hubs across the realms grew more vital with the influx of raid loot and rare accessories. Merchants at Karakura Town’s central market and near the Hospital exchanged accessory components, Pure Hogyokus, and Essence drops for high prices, and guilds brokered deals under the watchful eyes of rival factions. Newcomers learned quickly: a raid victory was only the beginning — how you invested those gains defined your path to dominance.

In this transformed world, every NPC interaction, every raid victory, and every purchase at Urahara’s Shop carved another legend into Peroxide’s fractured histories. Reality itself seemed to respond to conflict, bending around the ambitions of those brave enough to challenge time, endure storms, and carve their legacy among the stars of these timeless, violent realms.

Among the most feared was Coyote Starrk, an Arrancar whose passive presence warped the very wind around him. When triggered in special Time Gate or Storm Delve events, Starrk emerged with devastating strength and multiple phases of combat, forcing guilds to coordinate tanking, positioning, and special abilities just to survive. Survivors of his onslaught could earn Starrk’s Outfit, rare accessories, and stat‑boosting drops that permanently increased strength or spirit — gear so valuable it became a cornerstone of elite builds. Similar legends arose around Ulquiorra, whose ice‑cold attacks and shadow affinity made him a boss feared by all but the most experienced players. Winning these battles often rewarded unique accessories, each with bespoke stat bonuses that reshaped builds and meta playstyles.
