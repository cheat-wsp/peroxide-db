Vollständig

Quincy fonte original


Vollständig is the 2nd mode exclusive to Quincies and Quincycars.

The mode consists of the player obtaining a halo and wings, along with a new Vollständig move. You also keep your Schrift weapon.

How to Obtain Vollständig

To unlock your Vollständig form in Peroxide, you must defeat your Vollständig boss. Here are the prerequisites for engaging in this battle and attaining Vollständig:

  • 1. Reach Level 90.
  • 2. Accumulate 420 “Points,” which become available only after reaching level 85.
  • 3. Obtain points through different means: Completing a Time Gates awards 75 points. Time Bubbles (Bosses) grant 1 point for each percentage of boss health you damage. Defeating a Storm Vastocar rewards 10 points if you contribute the majority of the damage (at least 60%) Invasions (only if you are level 80+) – 5 points for stealing an object, 12 points for assassinate guards, 22 points for killing a leader. You must complete the invasions to obtain the points.

Once you meet all these prerequisites, initiate the process by talking to Yhwach. He will tell you to go to the mountain lab in Wandenreich, upon entering you’ll engage in a battle. If you are victorious against your Vollständig Boss, you may speak to Yhwach to obtain your Vollständig.

How To Activate Vollständig

Upon equipping your Schrift, activate your Vollständig by pressing the ‘G’ key once the gauge on the left side is entirely filled. Vollständig mode comes with a time limit, starting at 33 seconds and extending up to 190 seconds at its maximum. You can enhance this mode’s duration by diligently training it, which involves utilizing it frequently or engaging in sparring sessions with your Vollständig boss.

Upon defeating the Vollständig Boss, you’ll immediately gain access to Vollständig moves. Furthermore, you can upgrade these moves by employing your Vollständig extensively or practicing with your Vollständig through sparring sessions. To be able to activate your Vollständig, after defeating the Vollständig Boss, talk to Yhwach to obtain your Vollständig.

Benefits

When in Vollständig mode, your character’s base stats receive significant enhancements: Note: this changes for each Vollständig.

– Strength is boosted by 45% (1.7).

– Spirit is amplified by 50% (1.8).

– Vitality receives a 35% increase (1.50).

Vollständig Boss (Samuel Sam)

Upon meeting the point requirement, you must speak to Yhwach. He will tell you to enter a building on the outskirts of Wandenreich.

When you enter, you will be teleported to a room, initiating the fight against the first adversary, the Ancient Automaton.

Ancient Automaton (Samuel Sam)

The Ancient Automaton boss starts off with using Quincy moves, alongside some exclusive ones. The Automaton can only be damaged by either stunning it via Perfect Blocking or M2’ing. Defeating it will open a door to the next opponent.

If you are S- Rank or higher, there will be a checkpoint after defeating it. (If you are a Quincycar, you will skip this phase and immediately move on to phase 2 whenever you begin the fight)

TIP: If you are taking too much damage, reset your stats at Hobo Monk you'll need 800 yen and put at least 160 on Vitality.

Staying in the corner slows down the speed at which you can beat him, but prevents him from spamming flashstep as much. Makes it easier to see attacks coming.

2nd Ancient Automaton (Der Stahlstache)

The 2nd Ancient Automaton, unlike the last phase, can be damaged normally like any other boss; however, getting it to halfway will cause the three immobilized Shinigami in the background to die, transferring their Reishi to the automaton. The boss will then proceed to activate its Vollständig along with healing a decent amount of its health. Winning against the automaton will gift you your respective Vollständig.

TIP: If you are taking too much damage, reset your stats at Hobo Monk you'll need 800 yen and put at least 245 on Vitality.

(If you want to beat it fast i suggest using Apocalypse or Odyssey and 490 strength. The second you enter the boss, dash up 3 times and uppercut. This practically times the robot getting up animation perfectly and allows you to combo first.)

You can use the startup of the robot to get damage in, also at the end of the vollstandig animations. They will have short i-frames so wait until they are almost over before attacking.

Peroxide Vollständig: Vollständig Mastery

Like all other 2nd modes, your Vollständig can be strengthened by sparring with the automaton, or you can use it a lot although it is quite slow, best way to master it, is to try to beat the automaton while using Vollständig. If you fully mastered your Vollständig the bar will turn purple.

You must defeat the 2nd phase boss 13 times to fully upgrade Vollständig. Be careful of players who might grip you if you die against the boss, It is recommended to have a friend around to protect you.

Trivia

  • In the Bleach community, and Peroxide fandom, this form's name is commonly misheard as "Volt Standing".
  • This boss is widely considered to be the second hardest Progression boss in the game, but difficuly varies depending on your combat abilities and Schrift used.
  • Der Stahlstache uses your equipped moves, and will still use Schrift abilities even if you don't have them equipped.
  • the Ancient Automaton mimics your stats based on the Visor Lens ability, but will never improve in power.
  • Der Stahlstache doesn't scale off of accessories.
