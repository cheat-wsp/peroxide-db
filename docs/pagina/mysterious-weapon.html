Mysterious Weapon

Itens fonte original

Description

The Mysterious Weapon is located in the River at Karakura Town. It lies in the middle of the end of the river left of where the The Big Rat is. It resembles the heavy sword that Kūgo Ginjō, a Fullbringer, uses in the anime Bleach.
