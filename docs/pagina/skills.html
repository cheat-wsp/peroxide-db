Skills

Mecânicas fonte original

Description

Abilities are called "Skills" in Peroxide. All races share skills and a list of Obtainable Skills while having their own exclusive set of skills. There are also obtainable skills exclusive to Arrancar and Soul Reaper races. Hakuda can be obtained from Yoruichi and there is an exclusive Vastocar Hakuda obtainable in Hueco Mundo from Orochi The Shura

Obtainment

Every three levels, skill cards are able to be obtained. This shows up as an orange icon above the health bar. Once pressed, it will display up to three skill cards. If not enough skills are available, it can display two, or even only one skill card.

Re-rolling Skills

Re-rolling skills requires an item called SkillShop Reset Essence. These are obtained from Invasions, Time Gates, and NPCs (non playable characters). Some skills such as High Speed Regeneration and Cero (for Adjuchas), may seem impossible to re-roll. But their chances are lower than 1%. Some skills, like High Speed Regeneration, require at least one point in spirit to obtain. The more you add, the higher the chances are to get that move and other spirit moves. It's not guaranteed, but the chances increase the more you add. The stats you need for that skill are tied to the stats that strengthen it. (Resurrección and Shikai move probabilities are not affected by stats.)

Mastery

Upgrading a skill's mastery decreases it's cooldowns, as well as lowering the reiatsu cost and sometimes bringing more unique changes.

Classification

Skills are divided into:

  • Combat
  • Mobility (Evasives)
  • Support (Modes/Heals)

You only have a limited amount of skills that you can bind to your hotbar. You start with 3 Wildcard Slots (any class/type), 1 Mobility Slot and 1 Support Slot. 2nd Mode Skill don't take up slots. You can only bind one Mode Type Skill at one time. To get more Wildcard Slots you have to unlock your Shikai/Resurrection/Schrift/Fullbring (+1) and your Bankai/Segunda Etapa/Vollständig/Twin Soul (+1). Players can change their active abilities depending on their races :

  • Soul Reaper & Arrancar : press N (pc) Left D-Pad (Xbox) and talk to your inner spirit, (for soul reaper you will have to defeat your shikai boss first).
  • Fullbringers : use the Cellphone, and then enter your appartment and use the computer to change your abilities.
  • Quincy : Press N (pc) Left D-Pad (Xbox).
  • Hollows : talk to Hurak at Hueco Mundo, who is located near the orochi cave; this does not apply to Vasto Lordes, who can also access their moves by simply pressing N (pc) Left D-Pad (Xbox).
Vitality (Increases health Points, and in very rare instances, increases damage or mastery of certain moves, ec: showstopper and flag of resolve)
Spirit (Strengthens non-physical attacks, this is essentially the magic stat of peroxide)
Agility (Slightly Increases speed and the amount of times you can Flashstep.)

Dual Scaling

Skills that scale off of two stats are a special case. If you split your stats properly (within a 40 stat variance between Strength and Spirit), a move with dual scaling will do more damage than if you had solely put points into one stat. Using Counter Strike as an example, with 340 Strength, the user will do 101 damage upon successfully using Counter Strike. However, with 170 Spirit and 170 Strength, the user will do 120 damage upon successfully using Counter Strike.
