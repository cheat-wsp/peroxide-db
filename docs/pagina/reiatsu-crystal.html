Crystals

Itens fonte original

Sought-after gems that boost the strength of a user's capabilities.

Description

Crystals are Items that can be use on Skills (excluding unbindable skills), granting them unique effects but gives a drawback of +50% reiatsu cost. Only one can be used per Skill. Crystals can be replaced by using a new crystal on the same skill, voiding the previous crystal. If you use a crystal on an element skill and reroll your element, your crystals on all element skills will be instantly refunded.

Ultimate Crystals are Special Crystals that can be applied to Ultimate skills, but they bear a major drawback.

Obtainment

You can buy a random Crystal for 50¥ and 10 Time Relic from the Shady Shingami located in the café at ASRs Cafe.
