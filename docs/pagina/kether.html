Kether

Outros fonte original

CampoValor
Twin soul

"That's that and this is this"

Description

Kether is one of the Rare Fullbring elements that you can roll as a Fullbringer in Peroxide. It is shown as gloves on your person and when transformed turns into a sword. In the twin soul you gain a passive, a custom M2, and a custom perfect block,Not to mention an annoying custom counter passive (And yes, it's a project moon reference)

Perception Blackening: Don a perception blocking black mask, allowing you to sense the opponents moves and ignore dodges.

Extra Notes: As a Kether(Specially Only Kether) All Your moves will Chain once you have used them, Simply Use one of the Kether Skills and use another one and it'll automatically lock on the enemy similarly like Uproaring Phalant to Bite(Vessel)
