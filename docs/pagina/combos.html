Soul reaper combos

Fire build: 320 strength, 250 vitality, 0 spirit

Fire is the Nº1 shikai for combos, even tho it is rare, it has a single moveset which enables an incredbly OP combo that can even 1-hit-KO your opponents when in Bankai. Incredbly high damage, hard to combo with.

RENGOKU!

Combo 1: The will of fire never burns away

Flash step into your opponent, then use 4 m1s and "Aerial Tandem" followed up by "Will Of Fire". After these 2 attacks, you will have to spam "Fleeting Whirlwind" to use a tech where instead of working as a combo finisher, "Fleeting Whirlwind" actually grounds the opponent right next to you, allowing you to use more 4 m1s and use "Jolting Kick", then you will have to predict where they will fall and uppercut then while still in the air, using the last 4 m1s and a combo finisher like "Aerial Assault" or best option "Ocean Of Fire" (so much damage...)

Combo difficulty: ★★★★★ Combo damage: ★★★★★

Combo 2: Second Form: Rising Scorching Sun

An uppercut, 4 m1s, "Fire Slash" to send your opponent up, and then use more m1s and "Will Of Fire" as the combo finisher.

Combo difficulty: ★☆☆☆☆ Combo damage: ★★☆☆☆

Combo 3: By Novohs2

Get to your opponent, use your m1s, and then use fire slash, then do a full m1 combo and after the down slam use outspeed into areial assault.

Combo difficulty: ★★☆☆☆

Combo damage: ★★★☆☆

Combo 1: Hero's up B

The best way to execute wind combos is baiting you opponent into your "Tail Wind" counter, getting a headstart in damage and using the stun to use your m1s, followed up by the "Tornado Stomp", 4 m1s and "Triple Gale" to finish the combo off.

Combo difficulty: ★☆☆☆☆ Combo damage: ★☆☆☆☆

Combo 2: Aerial bending

Again, You should begin the combo with the "Tailwind" counter bait, followed up by an uppercut, "Tornado Stomp" then use "Double Roundabout" and a ranged move like "Shinten Raiho" or "Heavenly Wind Combo" (trash ult fr).

Combo difficulty: ★☆☆☆☆ Combo damage: ★★☆☆☆

Combo 1: No crystal?

For this combo, you'll need a Crystal of Terra on your "Byakurai". Firstly, use "Petal Slash" to stun your opponent, then flash step into him, use 4 m1s and a follow-up

"byakurai" with a 3/4 mastery "Tenren", then use "Flower Tornado", 4 m1s then finish off with a down slam and an "Shinten Raiho".

(This combo uses a very specific set, so dont use it unless youre 100% sure youre going to use flower for a long time)

Combo difficulty: ★★★☆☆ Combo damage: ★★★★☆

Combo 2: Muryo Kusho

Simple: Activate bankai, use your ult, land 1 m1. thats it. ult opponents wasted. You can just keep doing m1 combos and bouncing him off the wall as long as the m1s hit instantly. (You should use "Battle Instinct" with this combo for maxium damage).

Combo difficulty: ★☆☆☆☆

Combo damage: ★★★☆☆
