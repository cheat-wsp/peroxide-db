Charged Time Splitter

Outros fonte original

The Charged Time Splitter is an Item purchasable from Dr. Hydrogen from his pocket realm for 5,500, as well as a reward from the Weekly Quests.

This Item is a more powerful counterpart to the Time Splitter and can be used to summon Distorted Raid Bosses, which are significantly stronger than normal raids and have higher drop rates than their regular counterparts. It can summon the following raids: Aizen Raid, Ulquiorra Raid, Starrk Raid, Nnoitra raid, Byakuya Raid and Yamamoto Raid, depending on the current world. For more information on raids, see Time Bubbles. Beating the Distorted Raids guarantees you 19 Time Relics.
