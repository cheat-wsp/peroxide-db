Yamamoto Raid

Bosses fonte original

Yamamoto is one of the five raid bosses in the game. He scales with your level and also scales with the amount of members joining a raid. He has 3 phases. He is guaranteed to drop Time relics and has 2% chance to drop his outfit as well as his beard and his cape. When killed he can also drop Soukotsu a Powerful Skill that can be used by all races in game. He can also drop his cape which is valuable.

(This raid boss can only be spawned with a Time Splitter while being in Soul Society bought from Dr. Hydrogen or a charge time splitter from Dr. Hydrogen/Weekly Quests )

Phases

Phase 1: Yamamoto starts the fight using basic moves along with some Legendary Skills. He stays this way until you manage to deal enough damage to anger him.

Phase 2: Yamamoto activates his Shikai “Ryūjin Jakka” and starts being able to use Fire Shikai moves and his knowledge move Soukotsu. While his Shikai is active he also sets up a ring of fire and bursts flaming pillars out of the ground to distract you from your goal.

Phase 3: He activates his Bankai “Zanka no Tachi” where he unlocks the ability to use his ultimate, he passively summons skeletons and lights the player on fire with every hit from his moves, this fight is meant to be hard so good luck.
