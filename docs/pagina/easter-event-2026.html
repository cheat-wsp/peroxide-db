Easter Event 2026

Eventos fonte original

Peroxide Easter 2026 Event have added 22 limited times egg accessories to the game. You can find them wandering the map rarely. You can also find them attached to any kind of NPC, giving that NPC unique buffs (Even stronger buffs if the egg they're wearing is rarer, giving rarer items based on the egg as well)
