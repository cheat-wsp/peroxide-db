‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎Description

Accessory Crates are Lootboxes obtained from the Battle Tower in Ranked PVP.
All Accessories from the Crates ae cosmetic only, meaning they have no Attributes nor Traits.

Whenever a new season begins, the accessories of the previous season become unobtainable and get replaced by new accessories.

‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎Rarity

Both Accessory Crates and the Accessories within come in 4 rarities.

  • Common - 20 BTP
  • Rare - 25 BTP
  • Legendary - 25 BTP
  • Godly - 100 BTP

All accessories from Accessory Crates can be Exotic.


‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎‎Exotics

Exotic is like a secondary rarity on-top of the original rarity. You can identify it by the purple fade.







When you get an Exotic it will roll for one of the nine effects that will be on the accessory.
