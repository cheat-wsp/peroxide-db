Hollow Prince

Bosses fonte original

To find the Hollow Prince, you must talk to Hueco Oscura in the Ancient Tome in the Wrecked building, found in the Menos Forest

The requirements for fighting the Boss are,

  • Level 90+
  • 420 points

Time Gates reward 75 points if your team win and 25 points if your team loses. Time Bubbles (Bosses) grant 1 point for each percentage of the boss' health you damage. Defeating a Storm Vastocar rewards 10 points if you contribute the majority of the damage (at least 60%).

“You are strong, but, your soul lacks.. something… vital. You must fight those beyond the hourglass, or within the gates of war. I refuse to prey upon anyone else…“ Less than 200 points.

“Your soul is finally gaining that spark, continue your training. Return to me once you’re finally a worthy prey.“ More than 200 points.

After beating the Hollow Prince, you are rewarded with the second release of your Resurrección, Segunda Etapa.

Battle

Phase 1:

The Hollow Prince jumps at the Player and starts attacking them. After the Player deals around 20% of his health, the Hollow Prince backs down and sends his minions to fight him.

Phase 2:

The Hollow Prince sends out two ancient Adjuchas. These Adjuchas are slightly stronger than the ones in Hueco Mundo. The player will not get any sort of Health pack until both Adjuchas are dead.

Note: Passing Phase 2 once will set up a checkpoint and you will not have to repeat it even if you fail and die during the next phases. However this checkpoint only appears if you're rank S- or above.

Phase 3:

The Hollow Prince makes a return, this time he will have more skills to his kit. The player has to deal around 5%-7% of his Health and when they do so, the Hollow Prince will once again disappear and hide while his minions fight for him.

Phase 4:

The Hollow Prince sends in an Arrancar to fight the player. This Arrancar will be using the Resurrección “Manifest” when fighting against the player. When the Arrancar reaches half their health they will activate their Segunda and heal back to near max health. After a long and hard battle, another foe appears.

Phase 5:

The Hollow Prince sends in another Arrancar to fight the player. This arrancar will be using the Resurrección “Acceleration” this time. The player has to repeat the same thing they did in the previous phase, because this arrancar also has Segunda. After the Player has defeated the Arrancar, the Hollow Prince decides to come back to finish him off.

Phase 6:

The Hollow Prince makes his final return, and this time it is the Player’s goal to kill him, he will have even more moves that the Player must look out for. When the player has successfully killed the Hollow Prince they will be sent out of the Abyss... with the Power..

Hollow Prince

The hollow prince has several moves you have to worry about, they are as follow: Flash Stamp (Avoid), Aggare Cero (Perfect Block/Parry or Avoid), an unknown beam move (Block), Scatter Image (Watch), Flash Mirage (Run), and Bala (Block) Second Phase: Kick Flurry (Avoid).

Attack Loop

The trick to beating the enemy is forcing a foreseeable outcome. To do this we will almost constantly Hold Block (F). The enemy will always start with either flash stepping close to you and using a ranged attack like Bala or the Unknown Beam Move. Or try to go around you and Shield Break (M2) you, we can use this to our advantage to constantly catch them in a M1 or use our 4th M1 to stun them. If you hit the 4th M1 their are most likely going to use an evasive move, if they use one your best option is to run or continue to M1.

What to do if you are stuck in an M1

If you are stuck in their M1 use Push Counter (F + M2) if possible. If you have most of your HP (above 50%) just take it and run.
