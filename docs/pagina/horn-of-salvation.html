Horn of Salvation

"For all your work, you've gained SALVATION"

Description

Horn of Salvation (short: horn, HoS) is a Soul Reaper exclusive transformation obtained by using a Pure Hogyoku with Max Vizard and completing a pvp-focused questline.

Horn of Salvation replaces your Vizard mask when in Bankai. On use, the user regains around 60-75% of their hp, gains a better vizard buff, and 2 exclusive moves. Horn of Salvation lasts for the entirety of Bankai, regardless of when it was used.

Obtainment

Guide for Horn of Salvation Quest-line

To obtain Horn of Salvation you need to complete 2 stages that are extremely hard.

Stage 1:

  1. Have Vizard Mask Mastery Maxed Out
  2. Consume a Pure Hogyoku
    • Obtainable from Starrk Raid and Storm Delve.
    • When consuming the Pure Hogyoku and the text: "Your mastery of hollow powers is insufficient" that means that your Vizard Mastery isn't maxed. But there is currently a bug that even if you max out your vizard, Pure Hogyoku will still refuse and say: "Your mastery of hollow powers is insufficient". To solve this, keep clicking the Pure Hogyoku until it’s consumed, the server may take a time to load in.
  3. Kill 50 S+ players in Hueco Mundo without dying (death resets progress)
    • You must grip 50 different players
    • They need to be S+ rank
    • They have to be combat tagged (do not say mb before grip or it wont count)
  4. Kill 5 players in Time Gate (dying before achieving the 5 grips will reset your progress); you can grip players from your own team, and the same player counts.
    (If someone combat logs after they die to you, you get another kill)
  5. Once you have completed 5 Time Gate Grips, the game will say “advanced to the next stage”, meaning you can be combat logged/dying anywhere without getting the grip reset, failing the obby in Stage 2 also won't reset your progress.

Stage 2:

  1. Find ゴン npc (ゴン translate to Gon) that is located at the end of "The Pit" that can be found in the right wall of the Gourge in Hueco Mundo, speak to ゴン, he will fling you saying “ピトー“, speak to him again and he will send you to the obby.
    If you have not gotten the 55 kills (50 unique Hueco Mundo grips and 5 Time Gate grips) he will send you to the menos forest and reset your progress.
  2. Complete the super hard obby, if you fail the obby, you can re-try after the cooldown of 15 minute ends, you will not lose your progress.
    (you have infinite tries)    .

Stage 3:

  1. Have a Time Gate win streak of 5 (winning 5 Time Gates without losing a single one)
    (Leaving a Time Gate will reset your win streak progress back to 0.)

Stage 4:

  1. Go to the bell in Hueco Mundo and kill Terumi (Dying to Terumi won't reset your time gate streak so you can die as many times until you beat him)
  2. Congratulations, you have unlocked Horn of salvation!

Note

  • If you have Vastorage and use the Pure Hogyoku, it will destroy itself, since you CANNOT have both at the same time.
  • All attacks and abilities color change depending on your Reiatsu Color.
  • Horn can only be used when in Bankai.
  • Horn lasts as long as your Bankai.
  • When active your left eye's sclera (white outer coating of the eye) turns black and your iris turns yellow.
  • Vizard debuffs do not apply during Horn of Salvation.
  • Once you start the quest, you are able to get out of it by talking to Nurse Ari.
  • However, Vastorage’s quest and HoS quest can be co-existed, meaning you can do either one of them and obtain the mode you wish for.
  • Trivia: The text that plays on the screen upon activation, after translation says: "What’s your Salvation?"(お前の救いは 一体なんだ?[Omae no Sukui wa i ttai nan da?])
  • "ピトー" is the Japanese nickname of Neferpitou, meaning "Pitot." It is also a fluid viscosity measuring device.
