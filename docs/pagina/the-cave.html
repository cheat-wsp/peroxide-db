The Cave

Locais fonte original

The Cave is a location is the Menos Forest, where only Adjuchas spawn. They attack anyone on sight and are able to team up against the player. It is recommended that you are a high level if you want to grind in the cave. Due to The Cave also being part of the Menos Forest, your EXP is still buffed by 1.75x. It is one of the ways an Adjuchas can grind to become a Vasto Lorde instead of beating Storm Arrancars and Vasto Lorde in the caves and The Gourge, as long as you do not get teamed by the NPCs.
