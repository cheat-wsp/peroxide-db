Fullbring

Fullbringer fonte original

Desription

Fullbrings are the Element Abilities that you can get as the Fullbringer race in Peroxide. So far there are 6 of them.

How do I get my Peroxide Fullbring?

In order to achieve your Fullbring you must make sure that you have completed the things necessary in order to obtain it. First you must talk to Shūkurō Tsukishima in order to become the Fullbringer race. Then you must get to level 15 and head to the graveyard at night to talk to Michibane Aura. She will give you a Cellphone which can be used to get in and out of the Fullbringer HQ. Then inside you must talk to her to get and need to complete 3 different quests that she gives you. Once these are done she will put you in a match with endlessly spawning agents who you must kill. this is time based about 1:30 to 4 minutes time based. Once you have completed it, your screen with go grey and have an effect on it, with your Fullbring Ability manifesting a few seconds later you will not instantly teleport out.

How do I use my Peroxide Fullbring?

In order to use your Fullbring you must fill up the bar on the left side of your screen to at least 19% and then press G to manifest it. Getting to B rank will allow you to manifest your Fullbringer without needing to fill up the bar at all and infinitely sustain it.
