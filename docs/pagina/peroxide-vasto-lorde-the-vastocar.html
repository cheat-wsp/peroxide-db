Vasto Lorde

Hollow fonte original

CampoValor
Vasto Lorde
Found atHueco Mundo Menos Forest

Not to be confused with Vastorage.

Vasto Lorde is the first evolution of a Hollow before turning into an Arrancar.

While this race is usable in PvP and PvE, it is not recommended due to the lack of a Second Mode and the abysmally bad stats on Strength and Spirit. It's still semi-viable due to the Lord's Roar and the summon.

Description

Vasto Lorde have 3 exclusive Skills, Lord's Roar, Secuases and Cero Oscuras. Non-player Vasto Lorde leave behind Remains when they die. They spawn in Hueco Mundo and Menos Forest.

They are able to use clan skills such as Cero Metralleta.

Vasto Lorde get stats every 10 Levels (starting at Level 5), they get a 1.15x stat multiplier and their stat scaling gets buffed once they reach level 100.

Vasto Lorde can become Vastocar by pulling their mask off. When you become an Arrancar you lose all your levels and you don't keep any Vasto Lorde exclusive Skills (except Cero Oscuras, Hierro, and High-Speed Regeneration). It's recommended to use a skill reset before becoming an Arrancar and getting all the Skills you want at full mastery, as you cannot receive them for some time after becoming an Arrancar. Do not use a skill reset, as you will lose them.

Obtainment

For players, Vasto Lorde is obtained by getting 1700 "Evolution Exp" as Adjuchas. Exp is gained through killing or eating different types of Hollows, some give more exp than the others. You lose exp upon death. If you are curious on how to keep track of your points, talk to Shady Arrancar in the Alley.

    Trivia

    • Due to it's near unviability in PvP and over reliance on Prodigy Secuases, it has been referred to as "boyfriend race" as it is otherwise near unusable without only engaging if Secuases is active.
    • Vastolorde have a max level of 201 and have a max rank of S until they press G, which makes them glow purple, and causes them to instantly become S rank after rejoining, (D rank before leaving the game.) Pressing G again will activate the mask rip minigame. After pressing G, the new max level will be 201.
    • Vastolorde variants 3 and 4 now have their eye color scale off your secondary reiatsu color (t'was me yw).
    • Vastolorde's Lord's Roar can extend off of any combo,(except things like counters if you count that as a starter) making it one of the most usable moves in things like ranked.
