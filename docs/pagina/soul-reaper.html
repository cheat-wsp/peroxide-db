Description

Soul Reapers are souls that have the ability to fight and see Hollows with their Zanpakutō and most other souls. A Soul Reapers job is to fight and purify hollows and other lost souls, But for that same reason 200 years ago they erased the Quincy who were over-hunting the Hollows almost being the cause of the collapses of the worlds making the Soul reapers the total opposite of Quincy.

Check out the Soul Reaper Progression Guide in order to get started as a Soul Reaper.
