Hurak

Outros fonte original

Hurak is a NPC located in Hueco Mundo next to the Vastocar Cave near The Alley.

Hurak allows Hollows and Adjuchas players to rebind their skills. Non-Hollow races such as Arrancars and Soul Reapers cannot use this function. If the player who is a Non-Hollow race tries to talk to him, he will only have one option, which is as follows:

"What do you need?"

"What's going on here?"

"Oh, this place is starting to shift, can't you feel it too? HE's coming, and when HE arrives, we may not survive"
