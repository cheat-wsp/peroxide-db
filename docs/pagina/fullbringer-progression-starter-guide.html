Fullbringer Progression/Starter Guide

Part 1: Becoming a Fullbringer

As a human or soul, talk to your best friend, Shukuro Tsukishima, to become a Fullbringer. He is in a room behind Asr's Cafe, the building directly to the right of the Gym. Talking to him and choosing the first dialogue option will immediately make you a Fullbringer.

Part 2: Early Fullbringer Progression

After becoming a Fullbringer, all you have to focus on now is reach level 15 and increasing your stats. There's several ways you can go about doing this.

  1. Head to the gym and pay the gym owner a fee of 50 yen for entry. (Recommend doing some jobs for money first.) From there you can use the training machines found in the dojo to start increasing your stats.
  2. You can take on jobs from the Job Billboard. They'll grant you experience so that you can level up. You can also get stat increases upon completing jobs if you talk to Herupa and pay her to boost your chosen stat whenever you complete a job.
  3. For the sake of grinding levels, you could take the bandit beater route and just kill things for experience, ignoring the Job Billboard. (not really efficient/or just not ask Herupa to not attune a stat so you get extra xp) When increasing your stats you will have to limit break at several points of your progression. Limit-breaking requires you to max out 3 areas of your stats for each rank ranging from Rank F to S+.
  • Combat Stats
  • Reiatsu
  • Agility

Once you max out these 3 areas of your stats, you'll need to gain limit break experience in order to ascend to your next Rank. This can be done by talking to NPCs you haven't spoken to before, taking on jobs, or just slaying enemies.(P.S: Invasions also helps limit break. It helped me a whole bunch when there were no good jobs and/or normal Adjuchas.)

Part 3: Obtaining Fullbring

Once you reach Level 15, at night time, travel to the graveyard on the hill to the left of Urahara's shop, and speak with Aura Michibane. Upon closing the dialogue box, you will be given a Cellphone that you may use outside of combat to travel to the Fullbringer/XCution HQ. Speaking with Aura Michibane in the XCution HQ will allow you to take on the three missions that you must complete before you are given your Fullbring ability. If you are experienced, you will already know what the ability is by looking at the accessory you were given when you spoke with Shukuro Tsukishima and became a Fullbringer (see Fullbrings). The three missions can vary from four randomly selected tasks, all intended to be moderate in difficulty.

  • Escort - You must escort an XCution agent to the designated area, while keeping them alive and safe from Hollows, which will spawn directly next to the Agent in an interval and begin attacking immediately. It is intended to be medium in difficulty, but because of the way the game is designed, it is the most difficult task Aura Michibane can give you, other than reaching Level 90 and defeating your Twin Soul. Once the escort walks near the designated destination, it will despawn and you will be teleported back to HQ, the mission completed. It is possible to utilize the Escort mission in order to farm Hollow kills, but it is not reccomended as it only spawns fishbones (the lowest hollow class), which are not worth much to either Hollows or Shinigami. (Tip: don't worry about the Hollow spawns and continue running to the designated area)
  • Slay an Adjuchas - You will be spawned in an area nearby an Adjuchas Hollow which you must simply defeat, and you will be teleported back to HQ, the mission completed.
  • Slay an Arrancar - Similar to the Slay an Adjuchas task, you will be spawned in an area nearby an Arrancar which you must defeat, and upon gripping them you will be teleported back to HQ, the mission completed.
  • Defeat the Hollow Horde - Same as the last two tasks, but instead you will be placed in an area nearby a handful of Hollows, varying in amount. You must simply kill five of them, and you will be teleported back to HQ upon killing the fifth one, the mission completed. Normal Hollow kills will add to the count, even if they were not marked by the quest.

After completing the three missions assigned to you by Aura Michibane, you will be tasked with defeating Agent Harlock, who has the ability to seamlessly clone themselves. It is a difficult boss if you don't know what you're doing, but it isn't easy either way, and will most definitely take you a few tries. The objective is to survive the onslaught of Harlock's clones for three minutes. Once you choose the left most choice after Aura Michibane gives you the offer, you will be teleported into a white block arena, and a few moments after you are teleported, Agent Harlock clones will begin to spawn. They do not have much health, however they are given every Fullbringer signature move, except for the actual Fullbring moves. Agent Harlock does not have a Fullbring ability themselves. Once you have successfully survived for three minutes in the arena, you screen will darken and blur for just a moment, then your character will automatically enter your first mode, summoning your Fullbring ability, its name and shape predetermined. You are given a few moments inside of the arena to test it out, after of which you will be teleported back to XCution HQ, and Aura Michibane will have no more missions for you. From here on, you must reach Level 90.

Part 4: Twin Soul

In order to begin working towards your Twin Soul you must meet the following requirements:

  • Obtain your Fullbring.
  • Reach level 90.
  • Acquire 420 points from the following:
    • Successfully completing a Time Gate gives 75 points.
      • If you lose, you still get 25 points.
    • Time Bubbles (Bosses) give 1 point per % of boss HP dealt.
    • Each Storm Vastocar kill gives 10 points (majority damage required).
    • Invasions give points only if you are above level 80:
      • 5 points — Steal an Object
      • 12 points — Slaughter Guards
      • 22 points — Kill a Leader
    • Each enemy grip during an Incursion rewards 6 points.

After fulfilling all requirements you need to enter your apartment and you talk to your apartment computer and execute the command ¨beckon¨ reality shifts and you will have to go the other room now available and talk to your reflection and fight them. The fight will begin with you facing off your past deaths (If you have died during the progression). You won't have to do this again if you are S- Rank or higher. Then you are tasked with the job of defeating your twin soul. Beware, as he has a second mode and will use it once he gets to half health. Tip: Go 250 vitality and 120 strength or spirit depending on what your fullbring scales off Different tip: Be rank S, and put 405 into str/spirit (Depending what your fb scales off) and put 95 into vit Build and Strategy:

  1. Stats Allocation: Focus on building 100 Vitality (for survival) and 390 Spirit (to maximize damage output).
  2. Positioning: As soon as you spawn, move away from the center to create space between you and the boss. Keeping your distance is key to survival.
  3. Main Combo:
    • Snare (1)
    • Snare (2)
    • Lace Compass or Opening Thread Keep running and spamming this combo to maintain pressure while keeping your distance. (A faster combo is snare, lace compress into terraform, (can add Ryodan at end) Its because the boss doesn't have access to snare and doesn't block snare) (tip: before doing Snare(2), wait a bit to see if boss uses evasive otherwise you may waste lace compress or opening thread)
  4. Survival Tools: Use Scatter Image to create more distance between you and the boss if needed. I use opening thread to either when the boss dashes at me to guarantee a hit or when lace compress is on cd from the main combo. i use counter as a way to get hits in for my battle instinct to get reiatsu back.
  5. Gear:
    1. Helm of Miracles
    2. Ancient Cloak
    3. Glacial Destroyer
    4. King’s Wristband
    5. Traitor Shades My accessories are mainly focused on boosting Vitality for extra survivability.
  6. Skills
    1. Lace Compress
    2. opening thread
    3. Snare
    4. exploitation (dmg is good but can use terraform if you don't have it)
    5. counter(for when the boss dashes at me as i either cant dash away or use scatter image. can use if you feel confident you can land it and goes well with battle instinct to get reiatsu back)
    6. scatter image (run away when dash isn't available
    7. battle instinct (This skill is essential. you don't have a good amount of reiatsu even when S rank and you WILL run out of reiatsu very quickly)
