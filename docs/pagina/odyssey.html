Description

Odyssey is one of the Strength-based common Schrifts that Quincys can roll in Peroxide.

Odyssey is based around low but unpunishable damage along with heavy amounts of Invincibility frames, while in Vollstandig, gain a custom counter and complete I-Frames during your flashsteps.

This Schrift is notorious within Ranked PVP due to its ability to stall and having the inability to be punished.
