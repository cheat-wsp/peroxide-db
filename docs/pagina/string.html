String

Outros fonte original

CampoValor
Twin soul

"With great power, there must also come great responsibility."

Description

String is the second common and the second Spirit-scaling Fullbring Element that you can roll as a Fullbringer. It is shown as a white headband on your character, and in its Fullbring, a gray kunai. In its Twin Soul form it has three passives, and a custom M2 counter.

Passive 1: Laced Composure: After being guard broken, give yourself a string shield reducing damage by 50% until you're able to move. [Cooldown: 60s]

Passive 2: Razor Strings: All string skills will now proc bleed.

Passive 3: Threads of Fate: Threads randomly spawn only you can see, when touched they will make your ultimate deal more damage. (Max stack of 30)
