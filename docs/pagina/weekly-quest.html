Weekly Quest

Outros fonte original

Added with the Valentine Event update, weekly quest gives the player different reward when you finished the 6 quest

Your rewards for beating a Weekly can range from 3000 Yen, 8 PE, A Charged Time Splitter, or a "Quality Reiatsu Crystal"

Quests

S-RANK INVASION QUESTS

  • Invisible : Complete a S-Rank Invasion while only killing 13 or less guards
  • Five for Five : Achieve a score of 5 or higher on 5 S-Rank.
  • Hoarder : Collect 40 Sub Objectives in S-Rank Invasion.
  • Perfectionist : Blitz a S-Rank, getting score of 8 or higher.

TIME GATES QUESTS

  • Massacre : Get 30 Player kills while within the Time Gate.
  • Rampage : Win 6 Time Gate Matches.
  • Untouchable : Win 2 Time Gates without dying.
  • Brutalize% Speedrun : Get a kill within the first 6 minutes, 10 times, within any Time Gates.

OVERWORLD QUESTS

  • Banisher : Partake in and defeat 2 Distorded Time Raid.
  • Unbleached : Defeat a Time Raid in under 7 minutes, 3 times.
  • Slayah : Slay 15 Chained Vastocars during the Storm in Hueco Mundo.

Upon completing any of the quests, you will get one of the 4 rewards below.

Quality Reiatsu Crystal, 8 PE, 3000 Yen, Charged Time splitter

Grand Prizes

By beating all 6/6 of your allotted weeklys, you can get one of the three below.

Time Remnant, Unstable Time Remnant, or 25 PE.
