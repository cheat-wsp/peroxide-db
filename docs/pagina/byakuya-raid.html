Byakuya Raid

Bosses fonte original

Byakuya Kuchiki is one of the five Raid Bosses in the game. He scales with your level and also scales with the amount of members joining a raid. He has 3 phases.

(This raid boss can only be spawned with a Time Splitter while being in Soul Society bought from the Dr. Hydrogen NPC)

Drops

time relics - 100%

Hado 33 Soukatsui Knowledge - 25%

Flower Kenseikan (Rare) Stats: 10 Vitality, 20 Spirit (Hat) - 15%

Flower Kenseikan (TYBW) (Rare) Stats: 25 Spirit, 0.1x Reiatsu Regen (Hat) - 10%

Sky Cloak(Legendary) Stats: 40 agility, +0.1x heal regen, +0.1x reiatsu regen | +0.15x pve damage (Collar) - 10%

Flower Cloak (Legendary) Stats: 20 Vitality, 30 Spirit, 0.2x Reiatsu Regen. (Collar) - 5%

These chances can be doubled if the time bubble is distorted or you have a fortune field generator active.

Phases

Phase 1: he start the fight with his Zanpakuto and fights with Kido skills.

Phase 2: he activates his Shikai and uses Flower Shikai and Kido skills.

Phase 3: he activates bankai and starts to use spirit pressure, use flower Bankai skills and Kido skills.
