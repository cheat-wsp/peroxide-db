General Alberich

Outros fonte original

• General Alberich

"GOOD SOLDIERS FOLLOW ORDERS." - General Alberich when questioned why he is on top of the roof.

General Alberich is an NPC that serves as the Invasions NPC for Quincy and Quincycar.

He can be found at Wandenreich and is located inside the General store, in the ceiling.

If you have negative reputation with the Quincies, Invasions cannot be done from this NPC even as a Quincy. However, if you have positive Reputation, you can do Invasions from this NPC even if you aren't a Quincy.

• Trivia

• The reason he is on the ceiling, is because they gave him Cybernetically Enhanced Legs for jumping power... But not feet for escaping power.
