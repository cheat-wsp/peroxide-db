Buarbiette The Bullet

Outros fonte original

Buarbiette is a NPC that is located near the Quincy Dojo Vendor. Soul Reapers, Arrancars and Quincy players can fight Buarbiette for the cost of 100 yen and if they are over level 50. If you are below level 50, she will tell you to get stronger. If you win, she will upgrade one of your stats. You can previously choose what stat do you want to upgrade before getting into the actual fight. The Buarbiette NPC has a 30 minute timer everytime you defeat her. She gives 5 stat stars max, each star make +33% experience in the stats you chose beforehand, which is better than Kisuke. However, Buarbiette, unlike other Duelers such as Ken and Laqura, does not use Vollständig.

Her counterparts in each map are as follows:

Kisuke in Karakura, Laqura in Soul Society, and Ken in The Alley in Heuco Mundo.

Note: Kisuke only provides 3 stat stars while other duelers provide 5 stat stars due to how hard they are compared to Kisuke. You can also only fight 1 dueler every 30 minutes, and this is effected for every map worldwide for the player. This means that if you beat Kisuke and try to fight Buarbiette, you will be told you are too tired and that you will need to come back after a set amount of time.
