Segunda Etapa

Astra

420 Spirit, 69 Vitality Wait for the boss to spawn the m1 4 times, after that follow up with Rising Star then 4 more m1s to then use Agarre Cero.

The boss should be at Segunda phase already, that's when you hit him with a normal Cero then founding rain of stars to finish him off hit him with a Cero Oscuras

Accessories: Demonic Hood, Flower Cloak, King's Wristband, Scientist Glasses and Starrk's Outfit

Vessel

Stats: 490 Strength, 0 Vitality. Block the boss’s combo starter, use 4 M1s and use bite. This should get your inner spirit to below half, making him pop segunda. Move near the boss, and activate its ultimate, and run towards one of the walls during the charge up. This should finish the boss by itself without you having to fight its Segunda .

Accessories: Candy Horns, The Kenpachi’s Eyepatch, Starrk’s Outfit, and Storm Torn Cape

the first phase is really the hardest for vessel, at the last phase, have PATIENCE. do the move "AHAHAHAHA" as a combo starter, then do your combo, run, repeat.

Hope this helps :)

Bankai

Pyro cheese: MAX Strength, 0 Vitality and 0 Spirit

Equip Tsuzuri Raiden , a combo extender (cyclone, Fire Slash etc...) and a strong combo finisher ( Will of Fire , slash barrage etc...), DONT equip any evasive moves.

You should try to only use Tsuzuri Raiden and your shikais moves, since if you equip anything else, you bankai boss will have these moves as well.

Talk to the Bankai NPC and go into a corner, wait for him to use flash step once and begin to spam m1s, he will flash step into your m1s, so you just need to use a

simple combo extender, 4 more m1s and then the combo finisher, as soon as both of you fall into the ground hell flash step again, and then you just repeat this

process. Try to only use Strength acessories and simple shikais.

The fight should be quite easy this way, so unless your doing the glacier bankai (If u need help with the ice bankai click HERE), u should be able to defeat the boss 13

times in a row no problem!

Voltstanding

(Stage 1) No strat, pure instinct.

(Stage 2) Voltstanding Obtainment: 490 Strength, pop Voltstanding when ur low.

(Mastery) Use 490 Strength and Apocalypse . Use crystal of artificiality on Conquest. Skills: Cyclone and Conquest (Do NOT Equip Evasive). Use Medium weapon (Gun, Claw or Sword). Do 4 m1s into cyclone, another 3-4 m1s into Conquest. The conquest bypasses his Voltstanding pop allowing for a fast Cheese . (If this doesn't kill him you might need PVE accessories)
