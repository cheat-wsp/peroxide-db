Races

Outros fonte original

There are four races in Peroxide: Hollow, Soul Reaper, Quincy and Fullbringer. All races possess different skills and abilities that differentiate them from each other.

There are also three hybrid races: Arrancar, Vizard and Quincycar


The Races

You can become part of any race upon spawning into the game on a brand new slot depending on what you do first.

Soul Reaper

To become a soul reaper, you could find Kisuke and choose the soul reaper or "die" option while talking to him or get killed by a hollow to become a lost soul and find Kisuke and select the option to become a soul reaper. You'll then be reborn as a Soul Reaper. Kisuke is located at the Urahara shop, you can find him with your Soul Wayfinder.

Hollow

To become a Hollow, you must die to turn into a Lost Soul and then press "G" key 5 times. You will pull your chain and become a normal Hollow. As a Hollow you progress by killing and eating the remains of other Hollows.

Quincy

To become a Quincy, you need to talk to Gunther, this can be done as a human, or a lost soul. You can find him with your Soul Wayfinder.

Fullbringer

You must go to the back of the Cafe next to the Gym to meet Shukuro who will ask if you want to join him. Say yes and you'll be converted into a fullbringer and gain the ability to use fullbringer exclusive moves. At level 15, talk to Aura Michibane in the graveyard at nighttime for a phone to acess the fullbringer's HQ.
