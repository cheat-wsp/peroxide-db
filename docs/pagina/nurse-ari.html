Nurse Ari


Description

Nursi Ari is an NPC that can be used to reset/change a multitude of things.

Usage

  • Resetting your progress/race. (100 yen)
  • Changing your gender. (1000 yen)
  • Giving you a random name. (250 yen)
  • Removing Hos/Vrage or their questline.
  • removing Curses.

Location

Nurse Ari NPC is found at the Karakura hospital, Which is located Near Hair Rat And it has it's own Bus Stop.


(This marker shows where the Hospital/Nurse Ari is)


Trivia & Tips

  • The progress/race reset won't remove your accessories.
  • The progress/race reset cannot be done twice in a day with yen and requires Robux to do it again on the same day.
