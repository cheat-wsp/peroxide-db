Clan Faces

Currently, only Cifer, Zaraki, Aizen and Jaegerjaquez clan make changes to your face.

Cifer clan

With Cifer clan, you get Ulquiorra's eyes which have small slit-shaped pupils, distinctively thick eyebrows And the exclusive Cifer clan face marking has teal lines which descend from the lower part of your eyes.

While you use Segunda Etapa, the sclera of your eye turn green while in that form, like how Ulquiorra's turn green in the anime

The pupil changes based on your reiatsu color.

Jaegerjaquez clan

The Jaegerjaquez clan has has a pair of narrowed, angry eyes with green lines below them, similar to the eye markings on the Panthera genus of cats.

Aizen clan

Aizen clan === Aizen clan grants you purple sclera and white irises. This is taken inspiration from Aizen's fused with the hogyoku form.


Zaraki clan

It gives you a face with high cheekbones, enlarged canine teeth, and pronounced and hairless brow ridges. You cannot use a face reroll for this. This is Kenpachi Zaraki's Face. For females, only your eyes and mouth change. You get a eyes become aggressive along with raised eyebrowns. The female face is most similar to Kūkaku Shiba.
