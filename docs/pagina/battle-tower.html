Battle Tower

Outros fonte original

You get Ranked PVP rewards from the Battle Tower.

Description

The Battle Tower is similar to a Battle Pass but it's completely free. It has 50 Tiers that you can buy for 20 Battle Tower Points each that have 3 Cards each. The cards work similar to Invasion Loot Chest cards being revealed by clicking them but to claim them you have to spend even more Battle Tower Points. If you don't like what you got you can reroll all unclaimed cards in a Tier with 1 Shuffle Points or 100 Battle Tower Points per card. Every 10 Tiers you are rewarded with an emote and on the last Tier you get an accessory without spending additional Battle Tower Points.

Rewards

There are two types of rewards, randomized and set rewards.

Randomized

Every Tier you get 3 cards. They can be Common, Rare, Legendary or Godly.

Rarity is based on the current tier you are on. With higher tiers having higher chances for Godlies.

  • Common/Rare/Legendary/Godly Accessory Crates
  • 50/300/1000 Yen
  • 1/6/20 Shuffle Point(s)
  • 3/5 Hair Color Rerolls
  • 3/5 Eye Color Rerolls
  • 3/5 Name Rerolls
  • 3/5 Reiatsu Color Rerolls
  • 5/8/15 Clan Rerolls
  • 30 Awakening Element Rerolls
  • Time Remnant
  • Legendary Skills
  • 5/15/30/40 product essence
  • Legendary/Godly emote
