Apartment

Locais fonte original

Description

The Apartment is a location that you can access as a Fullbringer when talking to your best friend Shūkurō Tsukishima in the XCution Headquarters. The room is mainly for Fullbringers to rebind skills and to begin their Twin Soul Fullbring obtainment.

Features

In the Apartment, you can find your Apartment Computer, which has 2 options:

  1. Can you modify my skill? (Skill Bind)
  2. Execute command "Beckon"

Your Apartment Computer will only have the command "Beckon" when you reach level 90, but you can only use that command when you have a total of 420 progression points. For more information on progression points, check out the Fullbringer Progression/Starter Guide.

When you use the command "Beckon" successfully, you will be teleported to your Reality Shift Apartment, where the room appears deserted and the Apartment Computer is the only thing left. If you interect with it, it will reply saying that you are not their master. You will not be able to return to your original apartment after using this command.

You will be able to walk out of the doors that once appeared closed and walk down a decorated hotel hallway where at the end you will find the Reality Shift Apartment Room, where a shadowing figure waits for you. Talking to the NPC will allow you to fight your Twin Soul Fullbring Boss. For more information on progression points, check out the Fullbringer Progression/Starter Guide.

The Apartment will change its appearance after beating your Fullbring Boss, becoming the Reality Shift Apartment Room from then on. You will not be able to reverse this change.

Also you can use the apartment as a sort of bank as, dropping yen in your apartment won't disappear if u die or leave the apartment. But if u leave the server or rejoin the yen will disappear.
