Shini Cuts

Shinigami fonte original

Description

Shini Cuts is a location that is found in Karakura, located down the road near the Kindergarten Play Area and Urahara's Shop. In there, it is a barber's shop with 2 entrances and holds none other than The Trimigami, who gives the player a new or their original hair cut for ¥50. You are able to change your hair by using 5 hair IDs, and you can choose any hair ID as long as it is hair. If it is not hair, The Trimigami will be unable to change your hairstyle.
