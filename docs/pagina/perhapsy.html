Perhapsy

Outros fonte original

Perhapsy is a NPC that is located in a shop located outside the walls of the Soul Society. His shop is placed up a hill where you can walk up a ramp to get to. He is hidden behind curtains and you are able to talk to him, but he will only respond with the following text:

Hello, welcome to my town, but... How did you get in my shop?

This NPC serves no purpose in the game.
