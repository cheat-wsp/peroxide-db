Wungus

Eventos fonte original

Wungus is the currency for the Night of the Wungus Halloween Event that can be used to trade with The Harvester. It can also be eaten but it doesn't do anything.

Wungus can be obtained by:

  • Sacrificing a player in the cauldron - 10 Wungus (12 minute cooldown, if you sacrifice during cooldown you only get 1 Wungus)
  • Collecting it around Karakura Town - 1/2 Wungus
