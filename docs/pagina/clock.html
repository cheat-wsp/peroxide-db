Clock

Itens fonte original

CampoValor
Twin soul


"It’s Dead O’clock Somewhere"

Description

Clock is one of the rare Fullbring Elements that you can roll as a Fullbringer in Peroxide. It is shown as a watch on the left hand of your character, and upon manifest it turns into a revolver. In it´s twin soul your revolver becomes a duel revolver you also gain a custom M2 counter, Custom Sheath attack, a custom perfect block, and one passive

The Three Chronicles: Activating a chronicle´s passive will decrease the cooldown off their respective skill by five seconds if the skill is equipped and is on cooldown.
