Ultimate Crystals

Itens fonte original

Description

Ultimate Crystals are godly items, and are exclusively applicable to ultimate moves (flower domain, starrk metralleta, etc).

They give unique effects similar to reiatsu crystals, but unlike reiatsu crystals, they have downsides (other than inferno).

Obtainment

  1. Combining 10 godly perk stones in a Storm Delve
  2. Using an Unstable Time Remnant

Trivia:

  • Shady Shinigami can remove your ultimate crystals
  • They are among the most valuable items to get from an unstable time remnant, with an ultimate crystal of prodigy often going for around 7 hogyokus.
  • Aegis ult gem's downside can be somewhat of an upside, allowing you to mode-cycle to get your mode back faster for the healthpack (although this is quite unreliable, as you would have to pop twice to get the healthpack, as every other mode pop in combat doesnt give a healthpack).
  • Ultimate Crystals can effect people in your party.
  • You can use Ultimate of Prodigy to make explosion ultimate have scaling.
  • You can bypass Ultimate of Spirit's drawback by using Music segunda's blue tempo passive
