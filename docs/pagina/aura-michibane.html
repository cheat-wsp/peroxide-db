Aura Michibane

Outros fonte original

Description

Aura Michibane is a NPC that spawns in the Graveyard in Karakura Town at night time as well as behind the counter in the XCution Headquarters. Fullbringers will be told by Shūkurō Tsukishima after they turn level 15 to seek her out. If the player interacts with Shūkurō Tsukishima again, he will give clues to her location and spawn time.

Features

Once the player finds Aura Michibane at the Graveyard and talks to her as a level 15 Fullbringer, she gives the player some dialogue and then hands over a Cellphone, which is used to get in and out of the XCution Headquarters. The player will be able to find her as well behind the counter, and when interacted, will give the player 2 options:

  1. Allow me to prove myself! (Begin Mission)
  2. Can you open a portal?

If the player selects the first option, they will be given a random task. The player must do 3 of these tasks to be able to fight Agent Harlock and obtain their Fullbring. For more information, check out the Fullbringer Progression/Starter Guide. If the player selects the second option, they will be given the option to open either a Senkaimon or a Garganta, or sometimes both, for free, if the player has equal Reputation for Soul Society and Hueco Mundo. You may change your skills by going to bestfriend and saying [ENTER APARTMENT] then go to the computer.

Notes

  • Aura Michibane can also tell the player if they are over level 90 and accumulated 420 points that they can fight their Twin Souls Boss.
