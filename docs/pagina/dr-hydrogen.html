Dr. Hydrogen

Outros fonte original

Dr. Hydrogen is a wandering trader who offers various items from his shop as well as an item scrapper.

The portal changes location at 8:00 PM Eastern time

Search for gabi_nekoo or SheepyTG on Youtube, he releases a video of Dr.Hydrogen’s location everyday

Item Shop Rotation

He will sell a random combination of 3 of these items.

  • Fungus - 300 (Halloween Event)
  • Peroxide Mixture - 350
  • Skill Focus - 600
  • Skill Obliterator - 800
  • Fortune Field Generator - 850
  • Time Splitter - 2,200
  • Charged Time Splitter - 5,500

Counter Items

Wandering Radar - Helps find Dr. Hydrogen. Drops on death. ( 10,000)
Hair Dye - Colors your hair. ( 5,000)
Dye Remover - Removs dye off of clothes. ( 100)

Scrapper Machine

For 3 Common Accessories and 150 or 3 Rare Accessories and 300 you can roll like on a Slot Machine to get one of these rewards.

  • Yen 70 - 3,500
  • Accessories
  • Explosion (you get damaged by Lanza del Relámpago)
  • Product Essence
  • Legendary Accessory
  • Time Remnant
  • Impure Hogyoku
  • Unstable Time Remnant
