Ulquiorra Raid

Bosses fonte original

Ulquiorra is one of the five Raid Bosses in the game. This boss scales with the player´s level and the amount of player´s fighting him. He has 3 phases he is guaranteed to drop Time Relics, he also has a 2% chance to drop his outfit that gives boost to your stats, And finally he can drop the Volcan Cero Skill Knowledge.


Phases

Phase 1: Ulquiorra starts the fight in his base form and doesn't even bother until he sees how annoying you can be.

Phase 2: Ulquiorra, after getting damaged enough will awaken into his Ressurreción mode. In this stage Ulquiorra still isn't going all out and only attacks you with Vessel abilities as well as Lanzas del Relampago. He has ichor crystal on all of his moves; meaning he will heal some hp after hitting you with them.

Phase 3: Wanting to show you what true despair feels like, Ulquiorra enables his Segunda Etapa mode which buffs him immensely. This is where things get serious as he lets many Lanzas del Relampago fall from the sky and uses his special move, the Volcan Cero. Beware as in this stage he's almost impossible to beat without the help of your friends. However he is easier to beat if you have a good spirit build or have a high damage AOE skills.
