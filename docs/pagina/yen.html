Yen

Outros fonte original

Yen is the main currency in peroxide, denoted by .

You get from quests, invasions, Gunther, Weekly Quests, Battle Tower, or by killing players .

Yen is used to buy items and more.

Early game, yen is used for various services such as training, going to the gym, or other things. Late game, yen is used to rearrange combat stats or buying items from Dr. Hydrogen, making yen useful at any stage of the game.

You drop 20% of your upon death, with a minimum of 10 and a maximum of 500 or 1000 if you leave while in combat.

  • You do not lose your money by resetting your character or dying while not in combat.
  • The best way to farm is to sell items at Gunther, or by killing other players.
