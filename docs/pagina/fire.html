Description

Fire is one of the Rare Shikai elements that can be acquired by Soul Reapers in Peroxide. In its Bankai form, Fire bestows the user with 3 passives.

East: a special M2 attack that shatters the ground beneath the opponent,

Fire servants: causes exploding skeletons to passively spawn around the place with every 7-8 seconds, detonating upon contact with an enemy.

Inferno: each M1 in Bankai attack triggers burn damage,push countering summons a exploding skeleton. And all sources in air trigger burn damage. Burn damage automatically grips enemy's after a few seconds of burning.

Trivia

  • Will of Fire have two media references in one move. The naming came from the Naruto series, while the move itself took inspiration from Demon Slayer's Flame Breathing, as it is a combination of Ninth Form: Rengoku (the fire dragon) and Fifth Form: Flame Tiger (the relentless slashing animation done by the player's avatar).
