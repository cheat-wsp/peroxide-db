Yoruichi

NPCs fonte original

Description

Yoruichi is a NPC located in Urahara's Shop in Karakura Town. She leans on the table in the room where the Soul Gourd is located and teaches the player Hakuda for free, as long as they are a Soul Reaper or an Arrancar.

Hakuda Obtainment

When the player talks to Yoruichi as a Soul Reaper or Arrancar, she will tell the player that she will be able to teach the player Hakuda. However if they are not either of these races, she will state that only Soul Reapers and Arrancars can learn Hakuda, due to them being stronger. When the player continues to speak to her, she will tell the player that they need to prove themselves against her with a "Lesson of Strength", and when the player confirms the fight, they will be immediently be placed against her, much like a fight with Kisuke, except she uses Hakuda moves instead of Soul Reaper moves. When you defeat her, a text will appear onto your screen, stating this:

"You felt a newfound ability awaken within your being, more skills have emerged on the skill shop..."

This confirms you have learnt Hakuda. You must level up or use a Skill Shop Reset in order to obtain the moves. After you complete her "Lesson of Strength" you can complete her "Lesson of Agility" and "Lesson of Vitality" for the other 6 moves of Hakuda. To complete these lessons you just need to talk to and fight Yoruichi.

You also have a small chance to obtain Yoruichi's Outfit.
