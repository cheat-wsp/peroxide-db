Gotei 13

The Gotei 13 faction is based off of a group of skilled Shinigami in Bleach

This faction is very popular, having over 19K members in their Roblox group and over 12K members in their discord group.

Similar to other Factions attaining a certain rank allows the usage of special titles in your ingame name, this only applies for captains roles and the kenpachi role.

The way to get a higher seat is to defeat someone who is one rank above your seat.

Like many other factions in peroxide Gotei 13 has many leaders/captains for their divisions,

They get their own ingame title as Captain.

Divisions info

There are 13 divisions in the entirety of Gotei 13, each of these divisions has a set of roles, each role fufills a specific set of requirements for the division to operate effectively.

Captain

The Captain of a division is the leader and the head of the division they show the upmost integrity and lead their division into battle with great leadership and forges their divisions path to reach their goals.

Sub-Captain

The the expectations of a Sub-Captain align the same as a captain due to the fact that they are expected to be able to operate their division on their own if for any reason a captain isnt availible.

Head Lieutenant

Similar to Sub-Captain the head Lieutenant is expected to be able to fulfill the role of Sub-Captain if required

Lieutenant

The Lieutenant assists in the division to keep it running and alive they could also be promoted incase of sudden departure of any higher commands.

Sub Lieutenant

This role assists the Lieutenant with their duties

Seated Officer

These are the best players in PvP in any division, there are about 3-5 of these members per Division
