Storm Boss/Hero of the Storm

The Storm Boss spawns 19-6 minutes shortly before the end of a Storm Delve.
It uses a random Resurrección and Hollow Clan, and also uses the Ultimate Crystal of Aegis, which grants 9.5 seconds of damage negation after its ultimate move.
You can easily find the boss by using the afterlife gps as it has a skull icon while using it.

Drops

Titles

A random title can drop for the player who dealt the most damage (45%+ damage dealt) to the Storm Boss.
Titles are purely cosmetic, although higher tier titles are generally considered more valuable.
