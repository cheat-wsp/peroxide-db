Yhwach

Quincy fonte original

CampoValor
Yhwach
location Wandenreich

Can be found sitting on his throne in the Castle of the Wandenreich.

Schrift

Talk to him when you reach Lvl.15+ as a Quincy, in order to unlock a Schrift by doing the following things:

  • You have to complete a Hunt by using the Bounty Medallion, and then talk to him. That's when you will receive your Schrift. There's a chance he's disappointed in your services and wants you to do it again. In that case, he will say "Pathetic" .
    • The chance of obtaining a Schrift is 1/3 (25 - 30%) every time you don't receive a Schrift the chances of getting one gets higher by 10%.
    • instead of doing Bounty Medallions, you can do any invasions instead and Yhwach will react the same way.

Vollständig

The next time you should talk with Yhwach is when you reach Lvl.90+ as a Quincy.

  • He will give you instructions on how to prove yourself, You have to go up a large hill in the Wandenreich and enter the House on the top. There, you will be surprised to meet great Danger.
  • Vollständig Boss.
    • If you end up victorious, he will grant you the ultimate power, Vollständig.

Quincycar

Talking to Yhwach as an Arrancar (Who is also level 100+) will allow you to become a Quincy with an Arrancar clan.

  • (For this you will need positive Wandenreich reputation.)
  • NOTE: YOU DO NOT KEEP THE ARRANCAR PASSIVES WHEN YOU BECOME A QUINCYCAR EXCEPT FOR THE RESPECTIVE EXP AND LEVEL BONUSES!

Notes

  • If you provoke Yhwach through one of the responses, he will kill you for your impudence.
  • Yhwach has many lines that when you talk to him, can reference memes and jokes placed by the devs.
  • Its a very popular belief by many players that you don't receive arrancarr buffs however you do
