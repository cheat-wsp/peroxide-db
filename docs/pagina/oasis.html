Description

Oasis is one of the Legendary Schrifts that Quincy can roll in Peroxide.

While in Vollstandig, gain a water hop that acts as a double jump, and while in waterpark, gain a combo-starting pushcounter. You are also immune to burn effects while in this mode.

Uniquely, this Vollstandig is the only one with a true combo starting pushcounter, but it is locked behind your domain.

Oasis has 1% chance of obtainment every time you reroll your Schrift
