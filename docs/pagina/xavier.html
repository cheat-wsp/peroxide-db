Xavier

Outros fonte original

Xavier is an npc like Captain Tojo or General Alberich which give you the oportunity to make Invasions.

It also has the function of delivering soul gourd to him (Rewards: 140 yen, increase your stats or lose Reputation with Soul Society/Increase with Las Noches).

You can find him in The Alley of Hueco Mundo.

IF YOU HAVE POSITIVE SOUL REAPER REPUTATION, YOU ARE UNABLE TO DO INVASIONS WITH THIS NPC.
