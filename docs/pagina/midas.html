Midas

"Stay away!!! Stay away!! I'm cursed! Stay away! Don't you see? Everything I touch...gold."

Description

Midas is one of 2 common Fullbring elemens that you can roll as a Fullbringer. It is shown as orange glasses on your person, and activating it will turn the glasses into a pair of golden caestus. In its twin soul form it has three passives, A custom M2, and another passive activated by pressing G.

Midas touch: Dealing damage while having the golden fist passive on landing Midas moves will give you gold. (While having golden fist, gain gold based on your damage or by landing Midas moves)

Gold Borner: While possessing gold, reduce damage dealt to yourself by 30% (Getting hit causes you to drop gold)

24-Karat Retaliation: While possessing atleast 10 gold, Successfully perfect dodging attacks will convert the attacker into solid gold and briefly break them
