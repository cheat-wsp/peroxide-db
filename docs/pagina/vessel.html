Description

Vessel is one of the Rare Resurreccions that Arrancar can roll in Peroxide. Vessel has 4 moves and mostly scales with strength. Players receive a passive buff that gives the player a custom dash that deals damage, A full custom M1 combo, A custom M2, A custom uppercut, And a custom counter.

While this resurreccion has many passives, most are mainly reskins of the base kit and has little impact.
