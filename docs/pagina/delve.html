Storm Delve

Bosses fonte original

Storm Delve is a special game mode that requires you to be within 25 levels of your maximum level and is mainly used to obtain perk stones and Product Essences.

Overview

Storm Delve is a timed PVP/PVE mode where players fight through waves of enemies to collect loot. Players can queue solo, in duos, or in trios (duos/trios require a party). Queueing costs 500.

Players can kill eachother for loot

Total of 12 players are required to start, and players are teleported to the map after the queue fills.

Locations

Storm Delve can be accessed by talking to Erelander Lathrix at multiple locations:

  • NPC near the sakura tree in Karakura
  • Colosseum in Hueco Mundo (near clothing store in Wandenreich)
  • Segunda location in Menos Forest
  • House near the entrance of Soul Society
  • Tree at the side of the Trade Hub

Bases & Storm Powers

  • Arrancar bases marked on mini map by purple dots
  • Clearing a base spawns a Storm Power (interact with E)
  • Storm Powers grant buffs: Blessed, Cursed, Trackstar, Marauder, Pilgrim, Corrupt, Conduit, Omniscient.
  • You can consume multiple Storm Powers but only gain their corruption points instead of powers.
  • Every 25 levels of corruption you now gain a Divine perk. (Either a completely unique perk or a super buffed version of an existing perk)
  • Every 5 corruption levels you gain 2 perks rather than 1.
List of Storm Powers
Storm Power Effect

Cursed Prevents other players' Blessed from activating
Trackstar Increases Flash Step speed and halves its reiatsu cost

Conduit Gripping the hero of the storm (Arrancar boss) with Conduit power steals all kill credit from the lobby and gives you the supercharged effect (Like V - The Vanguard)

This power will also give you immunity to the delve collapse (boss grip isn't needed) and all the open chests within the delve will fill back up when the delve collapses.


Chests

  • Rare: 2 PE + basic items
  • Legendary: 5 PE + better items
  • Godly: 20 PE + top-tier items

Golden chests are very rare and spawn an additional chest at extraction.

Corruption

Every corruption level increases the user spirit and strength stat by 5%, and also gives a random perk that stays until death or delve collapse.

Corruption can be obtained in various ways, including:

  • Corruption orbs(+1)
  • Storm Current(+2)
  • Storm Power(+3)
  • Espada Kill(+2)(those that spawns in bases not the titled one)
  • Vastolorde Kill(+2)
  • Killing a player(you steal the amount of corruption they had)

The amount of "Corruption points" you need to level up is L*2, ex: to level up from level 4 to 5, I need to get 8 Corruption points.

Bounties & Healing

  • Orange dots on minimap mark NPCs for bounty hunting
  • Killing bounty targets rewards Time Relics and Perk Stones
  • No passive healing—healing must come from skills or Storm Powers

Bosses

Storm Delve features two main bosses:

  • V - The Vanguard – See page for strategies and loot
  • Hero of the Storm – See page for strategies and loot

  • Use hit-and-run tactics if hunted while carrying valuable loot
