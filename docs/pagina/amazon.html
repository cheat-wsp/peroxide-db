Twin soul

"I am Amazon!"

Description

Amazon is the second legendary Fullbringer that you can roll in Peroxide. It appears on your character as a belt, and transforms into an outfit covering your character which inflicts small damage and flame damage, the colour of your character changes based on your reiatsu colour and sub-colour. In the Twin Soul form you gain 5 passives and 1 debuff. Do note that this secondary has incredibly low damage numbers, and uses fist m1s.

New Amazonz Driver: Play Entrance Animation (Will also do a small amount of damage and inflict flame debuff.)

Echolocation Difaction: Immunity to Shadow and most forms of screen-obscuring effects

Insanity: Violent Slash will now cleanly decapitate an opponent’s head off on knock

Armour Zone: Custom Grip. Grip now heals additional 10%

EAT, KILL ALL: Less health = more damage plus bleed.

DIE SET DOWN: All damage done to Party Members is doubled. Self damage is doubled
