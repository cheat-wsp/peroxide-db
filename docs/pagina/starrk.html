Starrk

Bosses fonte original

CampoValor
Starrk Segunda

"I so envied the weak. The weak can always find others to be around. I want to be weak. And if that's impossible, I at least want to find companions who are as strong as I."

Description

Starrk is the very first and only Godly Resurrección that Arrancar can roll for a 0.25 percent chance. It can also be obtained by using Starrk essence which is a 1% chance drop rate on a Starrk Boss Raid or 2% on a Distorted Starrk Raid.

Most Starrk's skills scale on both Strength and Spirit. When Players pop their Segunda Etapa, their first passive will be automatically activated. This Segunda has four passives, an uppercut, sheathe, and counter-block move which each of them are listed below.

Appearance

Starrk has some unique appearance trait:

  • All the moves color changes based on what your Reiatsu Color is.
  • When you use Starrk, your Resurrección Weapon will automatically be switched to gun, your Resurrección Outfit will be set on outfit #4 (Starrk's) and your Segunda Outfit will be set to a custom one.
    • Note: you cannot change these through rerolls, however note that your old weapon and outfits will still appear if you switch to another Resurrección.
  • Starrk uses a different Resurrección transformation animation than the regular one.
