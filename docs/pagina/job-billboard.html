Job Billboard

Outros fonte original

Job Billboards in Peroxide are used to get quick quests.

Function

Job Billboards in Peroxide are a feature that lets players pick jobs (quests) that upon completing, the player will receive Yen and EXP (or stat upgrades, if enhanced by Herupa). Billboard jobs have a timer that's different for every quest, since not all of them are equally fast to complete.

Jobs

Jobs are classified by difficulty/completion time by stars. The more stars, the more time consuming or harder (yet rewarding) the quest is. These stars are ranged from 1 to 4.

1 star jobs:

-Kill 3 Hollows (30 EXP 15 Yen)

-Clean up plastic near Kisuke's house (8 EXP 15 Yen)

-Deliver the package (15 EXP 20 Yen)

2 star jobs:

-Kill a Menos (375 EXP 60 Yen)

-Kill 15 Hollows (265 EXP 90 Yen)

-Deliver the far-off package (60 EXP 40 Yen)

-Slay an Adjuchas (285 EXP 105 Yen)

3 star jobs (Hueco Mundo exclusive):

-Exterminate 3 Adjuchas (420 EXP 145 Yen)

-Slaughter 3 Menos (500 EXP 90 Yen)

-Slay a VastoLorde (620 EXP 160 Yen)

4 star jobs:

-Defend against a Time Bubble (1125 EXP 150 Yen)

-Eradicate 10 Menos (825 EXP 900 Yen)

Requisites

To pick up quests from the Job Billboard, you need to have a humanoid form, thus fishbone, red eyes, menos and adjuchas players cannot pick up any of the previously mentioned quests. Another important thing to know is that these quests show up different for every player, which means noob players can only get 1 star and 2 star jobs.

Locations

There are several locations at Karakura, but also two in Hueco Mundo. Note: Hueco Mundo's Job Billboards have different quests than the ones at Karakura.

Tips

  • When delivering packages, use your knowledge of bus stops.
  • Killing a menos is probably the best quest if you are grinding in Karakura.
