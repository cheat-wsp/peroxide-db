Body Modifications

Outros fonte original

Quincy have the ability to modify their body with various cybernetics.

Description

Body modifications allow Quincy and Quincycar to get 2 new Skills. All modifications have 1 Combat Skill and a Utility Skill that doesn't take up a Skill Binding Slot. In order to modify your body you need a Special Part. There are currently 4 parts and all are legendary items that can be obtained from Invasions and Time Gates. You can only have one modification at once.

Obtainment

Prerequisite: Level 60 and a Special Part of choice

You have to talk to Johan who is located in a house between the General Shop and Outfit Store in Wandenreich. Choose the option "I have a special part of interest." while holding the item in your hand.
