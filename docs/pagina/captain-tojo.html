Captain Tojo

Captain Tojo is an NPC like Xavier or General Alberich which give you the oportunity to make Invasion.

He also allows you to exchange Rogue Shinigamis badges for 45 yen each or to improve a stat from your choice.

He's located inside Soul Society.

IF YOU HAVE POSITIVE ARRANCAR REPUTATION, YOU ARE UNABLE TO DO INVASIONS FROM THIS NPC.

• Trivia

• When exchanging Rogue Shinigami Badges, he says the following line. "Thanks to you, these criminals have less of a chance of being Vi- I mean, Dangerous Traitors." Presumably, the word he cut off was Vizard.
