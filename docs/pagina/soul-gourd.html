Soul Gourd

Itens fonte original

A Soul Gourd is an item that can be bought for 25¥ from either Kisuke’s Shop or the Hueco Mundo Alleyway. It is utilized to gain reputation/rewards with in-game organizations.

NOTE: This is a multiple use item, meaning it won't disappear from your inventory after you turn it in to Kisuke/Xavier/Gunther, it is also impossible to drop Soul Gourds. This item also cannot be bought multiple times, so you can't have more than one soul gourd per slot.

How to Use

Collect a certain amount of hollow kills and the Gourd will become bright green, then you can go to either Urahara's Shop, The Alley, or the garage by the bus stop and give the Gourd to Kisuke, Xavier, or Gunther respectively. You can get ONE of these rewards for doing so:

  • +140¥.
  • +40 Soul Society/+45 Wandenreich Reputation, lower negative Reputation with Las Noches by an unknown amount.
  • Exp towards a certain stat of your choice.

NOTE: Higher evolutions of hollows take less kills to fill the gourd. Ex: Killing 1 Storm Vastocar will completely fill the gourd whilst Adjuchas will fill it by around 1/3.
