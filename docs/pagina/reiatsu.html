Reiatsu

Outros fonte original

Description

You need Reiatsu to use Skills and to Flashstep. The maximal stat points in Reiatsu are 180. Some Accessories give you additional Reiatsu. There are Crystals that interact with Reiatsu in different ways, the Crystal of Spirit reduces the Reiatsu cost of the move or the Crystal of Creep that makes you steal the Reiatsu of the person you hit.

Colors

These Colors of Reiatsu can affect many aspects of the game such as skills and accessories.

Custom Colors

Custom Reiatsu (Crei) can be any color. It can be earned through various methods such as: devs, faction tournaments, moderators, or giveaways in offical factions / offical server.

However, depending on the method used to earn custom reiatsu, the hex range will be capped at certain values, meaning that you will not always be able to choose pure white / black.
