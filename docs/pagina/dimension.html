Dimension

Locais fonte original

CampoValor
Twin soul


"It's just Janemba, isn't it?"

Description

Dimension is the one of two legendary Fullbring Element that you can roll as a Fullbringer in Peroxide, the other being Amazon. It appears on your character as a golden ring, and in-mode transforms into a purple sword. In the twin soul form you gain three passives and a custom M2 counter.
