Orochi The Shura

Outros fonte original

Description

Orochi is an NPC located in a cave in Hueco Mundo near the Hurak NPC (the cave is also a Vasto Lorde spawn). He teaches hollow players (Vastocar and Vasto Lordes only) the Arrancar Hakuda skill set right after being beaten. He has 20,000 HP and scales off your strength(?) and spirit stats. Using spirit stats against this boss the damage will depend on your vitality, and his health will be doubled (possibly depending on your spirit).

Requirements

  • MUST be a Vastocar or Vasto Lorde, otherwise you won't be able to fight him.
  • As a Vastocar, you are NOT required to unlock the base Hakuda from Yoruichi to fight Orochi and unlock the Arrancar Hakuda.

Vastokuda obtainment

When the player talks to Orochi he will say "I've seen fishbones with more grit than you." if you are not a Vastocar/Vasto Lorde player. If you are a Vastocar/Vasto Lorde player and talk to Orochi he will give you the chance to challenge him. If you accept you will be teleported to a dojo similar to the one you are given the tutorial by Laqura when you first play the game. After spawning in Orochi "The Shura" will have a short cutscene before starting the boss fight, and a timer of 240 seconds (4 minutes) will be set (with voice lines from BlazBlue). The fight against Orochi is very hard, but once you beat him you will be given access to all 9 Arrancar Hakuda moves (you must use a skill reset to select, upgrade and use them).

His moveset is unique in a few ways. It consists of a combo starting critical, a combo starting M2-Counter, the Arrancar Hakuda moveset (frequently using Banishing Hunt, Ferocity, Tatsumaki, Lightlike, and Flying Fist), Ruthless Fatality, and Dark Beast transformation (healing 33% of all damage dealt, he is stunned during the animation allowing you to waste one evasive from him), his boss exclusive move. In addition, he also has multiple evasives, keep this in mind for high damage combos. Waste evasives with M1s. On rare occasions, he won't use Dark Beast transformation (probably a bug). On these tries, he will NOT heal off his moves nor M1s. Maximize your damage as much as possible, save your evasive and mode, and make a good use of Segunda passives.

On high ping, Tatsumaki will true combo into Lightlike. You can perfect block or counter Banishing Hunt. You can block Flying Fist and Tatsumaki, however, to avoid the guardbreak at the end of Tatsumaki, M2-Counter immediately before it happens. Same can be said for Ferocity, block and M2-Counter before the uppercut guardbreak happens and you can get a free combo off of both.

Unrivaled and Unforeseen are rarely used, but should still be noted. Unrivaled, while having no counter, gives you iframes at the end, allowing you to block his M1s or chained moves. Unforseen can be blocked. Expression (Earth Crouching Dragon but fancier) should just be dashed away, as it also has endlag.

He will dash around you frequently, wait them out and use M1s when he gets close. Heavy weapons (greatsword, axe, guns) are recommended, but not necessarily needed. Pay close attention to the animations, if he catches you off guard when he uses an evasive, he'll use a combo. Occasionally, he will hold block. Back away and don't use any moves. He will M2-Counter, and because of his moveset, he'll be able to combo start off of it. He'll often use Ruthless Fatality when nearby and not blocking as well, make sure to predict and block it for a free m1 combo.

Recommended crystals: Ichor (healing), Artificiality (increases PvE damage), Aegis (one hit protection).

Not recommended crystals: Terra (no real function), Inferno (next to useless between his constant dodges and i-frames), Exponentiality (high risk, high reward. He is able to both perfect dodge and perfect block these sometimes, and his i-frame moves will flat out nullify all damage, as well as your i-frame moves, including Outspeed and Aerial Assault), Chains, Creep, Degression, Shadow (do next to nothing aside from depression and shadow applying a minor damage debuff), Despair (does nothing for NPCs).

Recommended moves: Aerial Assault (only on low ping, high ping makes the animation waste a lot of time), Outspeed (good for catching him while dashing and bypassing his block phase, true combos into either Aerial Assault or Dispersing Barrage), Earth Crouching Dragon (pseudo-evasive without sharing CD, let's you deal extra damage), Dispersing Barrage (movestacking at the end of an m1 chain, catching him from a distance or after Outspeed), Counter Strike (only if you know how to use it properly), Showstopper (does good damage and potentially might give you a free m1 combo if he uses his flip move).

Recommended moves (as a Vasto Lorde): Bala, As many Ceros that can be aimed downwards with Ichor or Artificiality as possible, Secuases only use when you need to heal since the Adjucha will take boss on him self and you wil be able to heal 10-25% of your health, and Lanza del Ralanpago since it also can be aimed down words.

This trick is crucial to landing hits make sure to combo as much as possible its going to take time but is possible

Trivia & Tips

  • This is one of the hardest bosses in-game atm, meaning it might take you like 5 gorgillion years to beat him, so good luck!! This is currently the only boss that has a visible timer, and a cutscene before and after it's boss fight.
  • His appearance resembles that of Ryomen Sukuna's while possessing the body of Megumi Fushiguro from the popular anime/manga Jujutsu Kaisen. As he has an extra pair of eyes, besides the crimson eyed iris (the Grimmjow's mask could as well make allusion to Sukuna's true form bone placed on the right side of his face).
  • If you use Vessel's Bite move, he can Evasive (but will still take damage), forcing him into the ground. Free combo.
  • Don't put ichor on your evasive because it can cause stunts which give him a free combo.
  • In the event you manage to teleport him out of the arena with Aerial Assault, if he evasives, he will either be right behind you or to the diagonal left of you.
  • It is recommended to find low-player count servers and lowering the graphics quality for better ping and overall performance.
  • Recommend to use either beast(for bleed) or vessel(for the lock animation)
  • Make sure you have at least 0.50 pve damage from accessories as without it you will probably do no damage, the time will run out and he will use you as a toothbrush.
  • The only thing you can do if you are a Vasto Lorde is to pray for the devs to make the boss as hard as to normal players or buff Vasto Lorde.
  • If you time Dispersing Barrage right while getting teleported you can get someone else in Orochi's arena with you so you can get help with fighting him (possibly needs testing).
