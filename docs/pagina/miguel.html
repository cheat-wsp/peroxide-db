Miguel

Miguel is an Npc in Soul Society that can disguise you if you are not a Soul Reaper.

[During Invasions only] If you are carrying a dead Soul Reaper Npc and talk to him, he will disguise you.

If you are a NOT in an invasion he will say "Trust me ... You have no need for my service ..."

If you turn in a guard as the guard gets knocked out your hand, he says "want a sprite cranbarry?"
