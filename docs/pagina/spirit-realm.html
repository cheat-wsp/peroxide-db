Spirit Realm

Outros fonte original

A Spirit Realm is a place where a Zanpakuto spirit resides, created within the minds of the Soul Reapers and Arrancar who wield them. Players can access their spirit realm by pressing the N key.

Soul Reaper Spirit Realms

A Spirit Realm depicting a burned down Soul Society housing district.
A Spirit Realm depicting a world on its side with towering skyscrapers.
A Spirit Realm depicting a rocky world with various pipes above the ground.
A Spirit Realm depicting a rocky, grassy world with gohei streamed along the sides of your world, companied by two Torii gates on opposite sides.
A Spirit Realm depicting a grassy floating island littered with stone pillars and pieces of floating rock surrounding the island, held together by chains.
