Grafitti At Karakura

In Karakura, Theres a lot of Graffiti in various parts of the city, Sometimes containng messages and art, Most of the times containing Characters from bleach. In addition to being an interesting visual element, They are an essential part of the urban landscape. These drawings can be found in various areas of the city, spread across buildings, walls and even in isolated streets. Although of them have no "meaning", they are known for their funny messages and jokes, A cool way to personalize and make the city interesting to explore.
