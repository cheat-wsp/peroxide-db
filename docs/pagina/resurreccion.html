Resurrección

Hollow fonte original

Description

A Resurrección is a special ability that is given to you once you become an Arrancar during the Hollow progression process. You also need to be level 15 to find out what your Resurreccción is. If you obtain a Resurrección that you don’t want, you can reroll the Resurrección by spending 100 OR by spending Product Essences. Additionally, there are some redeemable codes that reward you with PE that you can use to reroll your Resurrección.

Press G to activate your Resurrección when your bar has reached a minimum of 20%, which is filled by dealing damage. Each ranks slows down the drain rate of the bar. Reaching B rank allows you to use your Resurrección when the bar is at 0% infinitely. From this point on, the bar itself will have no use until you obtain your Segunda Etapa.

Each Resurrección has 3 skills associated to it and when it becomes a Segunda Etapa, you get an Ultimate ability depending on what Resurrección (commonly referred to as res) you have.

Also, when you use your Resurrección, you take on a new form that will be randomly picked for you, this is purely cosmetic however, and you may reroll them for 1 PE per 4 rerolls.

Keep in mind that Resurrección has different activating style.
