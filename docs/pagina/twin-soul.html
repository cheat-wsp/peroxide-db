Twin soul

Outros fonte original

Twin Soul is the end-game second mode for Fullbringers

How to get Twin Soul

To unlock your Twin Soul in Peroxide, you must beat your inner reflection. Here are the prerequisites for engaging in this battle and attaining Twin Soul:

  1. Attain Level 90.
  2. Accumulate 420 “Points"
  3. Obtain points through different means: Completing a Time Gate awards 75 points if your team wins, and 25 points if your team lose. Time Bubbles grant 1 point for each % of boss health you deal. Defeating a Storm Vastocar awards 10 points if you contribute the majority of the damage (at least 60%). Invasions give points only if you are above level 80. 5 Points for "Steal an Object", 12 for "Slaughter Guards" and 22 for "Kill a Leader". Each Npc grip you get during an Incursion rewards you with 6 Points.

Once you meet all these prerequisite initiate the process by using the ¨beckon¨ command on the apartment computer.

Peroxide Twin Soul Guide: How To Activate Twin Soul

Upon equipping your Fullbring, activate your Twin Soul by pressing the ‘G’ key once the gauge on the left side is entirely filled. Twin Soul mode has a time limit, starting at 33 seconds with no mastery and extending up to 190 seconds at full mastery. They can enhance this mode’s duration by diligently training it, which involves utilizing it frequently or engaging in sparring sessions with their reflection within their altered reality world.

Upon defeating your reflection, you’ll immediately gain access to Twin Soul. Furthermore, a Fullbringer can upgrade these moves by employing their Twin Soul extensively or practicing with their Reflection through sparring sessions.

When in Twin Soul mode, your character’s base stats receive significant enhancements. Note: this changes somewhat for each one

Twin Soul Mode Benefits:

– Strength is boosted by 20% (1.2x).

– Spirit is amplified by 30% (1.3x).

– Vitality receives a 25% increase (1.25x).

Phase 1:

The Player gets teleported in a room where they have to defeat their past failures. Every time they have died to thus far will come back to haunt them. It is now their job to best the failures and move on. These reflections will be using whatever Fullbring and accessory the player has at that time.

Phase 2:

Phase two is when the Player’s reflection appears. With it’s goal being to prepare them for the King’s return, it will definitely not go easy on them. After getting it to around 40% HP, the reflection will activate Twin Soul Fullbring and heal to max HP. You will also be healed along with your reflection. it will grant the Player Twin Soul Fullbring after the fight.

Note: Passing Phase 1 once will set up a checkpoint and you will not have to repeat it even if you fail or die during Phase 2. However this checkpoint only appears if you're rank S- or above.

Peroxide Twin Soul: Twin Soul Mastery

To be in Twin Soul longer, a Fullbringer will need to beat their Reflections multiple times (13 times to full mastery). To fight the boss so one can increase their mastery, one needs to go to their apartment, and click on "Let's duel again. (Train Mastery)". One will know when one has mastered their Twin Soul when the outline of their bar has turned purple. Reflections will use your currently equipped skills, but will also use all of your Fullbring skills regardless of if you have them unlocked or equipped. Using a Fullbring that scales off of spirit while running a strength build can make the fight much easier, as the AI doesn't take into account move scaling. Your mastery also passively increases by using the mode, but this takes an extremely long time (over 1000 uses) and is not recommended.

DO NOT PUT ALL OF YOUR STATS INTO STRENGTH/SPIRIT

IT DOES NOT WORK WITH THE TWIN SOUL BOSS

Go for an even mix of Spirit/Strength and Vitality

This is because of the twin souls boss copying you stats reversed that means 300 vitality=300 strength/spirit etc.

The best build I can suggest is 230 vitality and 260 strength/spirit

Build and Strategy:

  1. Stats Allocation: Focus on building 100 Vitality (for survival) and 390 Spirit (to maximize damage output).
  2. Positioning: As soon as you spawn, move away from the center to create space between you and the boss. Keeping your distance is key to survival.
  3. Main Combo:
    • Snare (1)
    • Snare (2)
    • Lace Compass or Opening Thread Keep running and spamming this combo to maintain pressure while keeping your distance.
  4. Survival Tools: Use Scatter Image to create more distance between you and the boss if needed.
  5. Gear:
    • Helm of Miracles
    • Ancient Cloak
    • Glacial Destroyer
    • King’s Wristband
    • Traitor Shades My accessories are mainly focused on boosting Vitality for extra survivability.


Note: For accessories you can also go for the full PvE set for maximum damage:

Hat: Real Chicken Egg or Destiny Egg- 0.25 PvE Dmg

Face: Yamato Beard or Scientist Glasses - 0.2/0.25 PvE Dmg

Collar: Winter Cloak or Ancient Cloak - 0.2 PvE Dmg

Outfit: Starrk outfit - 0.25 PvE Dmg

Wrist: Any wrist accessories.

Extra Note: For some reason, with moves that do about the same knockback as an m1, the twin souls boss will commonly dash directly towards you, leading to a very long, but inconsistent m1 chain, this has not been tested on spirit builds.

The AI has two different modes that it swaps between, a more aggressive style, and a more passive one, it's recommended to attempt to perfect block and generally defend when he is in passive mode, and to go all in on offence when he is in aggressive mode.
