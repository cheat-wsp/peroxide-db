Laqura The Lightning

Outros fonte original

Laqura is a NPC that is located in Soul Society next to the Soul Reaper Clothes Store. Soul Reapers, Arrancars and Quincy players can fight Laqura for the cost of 100 yen and if they are over level 50. If you are below level 50, he will tell you to get stronger. If you win, he will upgrade one of your stats. You can previously choose what stat do you want to upgrade before getting into the actual fight. The Laqura NPC has a 30 minute timer everytime you defeat him. He gives 5 stat stars max, each star make +33% experience in the stats you chose beforehand, which is better than Kisuke. However, Laqura is much stronger than Kisuke, being able to use Lightning Bankai moves against you.

His counterparts in each map are as follows:

Kisuke in Karakura, Ken in The Alley in Heuco Mundo, and Buarbiette in Wandenreich.

Note: Kisuke only provides 3 stat stars while other duelers provide 5 stat stars due to how hard they are compared to Kisuke. You can also only fight 1 dueler every 30 minutes, and this is effected for every map worldwide for the player. This means that if you beat Kisuke and try to fight Laqura, you will be told you are too tired and that you will need to come back after a set amount of time. Has a time limit of 5 minutes.
