Oni White

Outros fonte original

Description

Oni White is an event raid boss with 2 stages added in the Halloween event 2025. He can be spawned with the thread of fate. He is incredibly hard to fight so it is advised to fight him with multiple people.

Cheesing the boss no longer work.

Drops:

Oni white drops multiple rewards for defeating him, to increase the chance of receiving them deal more damage to the boss.
