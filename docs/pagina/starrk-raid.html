Starrk Raid

Bosses fonte original

Coyote Starrk is one of the 6 Raid Bosses in the game. He scales with your level and also scales with the amount of members joining a raid. He has 3 phases.

(This raid boss can only be spawned with a Time Splitter while being in Hueco Mundo bought from the Dr. Hydrogen NPC)

Drops

Starrk's Outfit - 10%

Pure Hogyoku - 5%

Essence of Starrk - 1%

Beyond Patch - 1%

Unstable Time Remnant - ???%

Impure Hogyoku - ???%

These chances can be doubled if the time bubble is distorted or you have a fortune field generator active.

Phases

Phase 1: Starrk starts the fight using some basic moves along with some Vastocar and Arrancar Hakuda Skills such as: Cero Oscuras, Expression and Unforeseen. He stays this way until you manage to deal enough damage to tick him.

Phase 2: Starrk, after getting to half health takes it slightly more seriously, and goes into his Resurrección (Once activated heals up to 75% of his max health) while calling out “Kick About, Los Lobos” and getting full access to Starrk Resurrección moves along with his Ultimate move Starrk's Metralleta, unlike us players, he can use it whenever without needing to land the skill Renunciar to fill up the meter.

Phase 3: After getting tired enough he's forced into his Segunda Etapa, in which, he begins with his first passive Revolver de Los Lobos summoning two big revolvers to fire a giant cero afterwards, and of course being able to use all of his passives as well. When in this state he will begin firing his Ultimate more constantly so you all better be ready(he uses his ultimate more when the targeted player runs).
