Obtainment

As it is a Godly Drop, the chances of obtainment are:

  • Invasions (F~B rank) – 0.1% (Unobtainable from S- rank, gives Unstable Time Remnant instead.)
  • Incursions – 0.1%
  • Time Gates – (0.6% If your team wins, 0.3% If your team loses)
    • Note: After 45 Wins in Time Gates, you are guaranteed a Time Remnant.
  • Weekly Quests - 1 in 3 chance per weekly quest
  • Dr. Hydrogen's Scrapper Machine

Obtaining it from Incursions and Time Gates will have it go directly to your inventory. But for Invasions you will have to take it from your loot chest.
