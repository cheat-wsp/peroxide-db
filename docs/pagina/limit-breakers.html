The Ultimate Guide to Limitbreaking in Peroxide

At first your stats are capped in Peroxide, and to increase your stats further, you have to Limitbreak. There are eight ranks in Peroxide, and each rank has a stat cap. A Limitbreak is essentially an ascension to the next rank.

But before you do, you need to max out your stats for the rank you’re on. In your Stats menu, on the right side will say Limitbreak, and underneath this should be some text. It should look something like this:

  • Combat Stats Unmaxed
  • Reiatsu Stat Unmaxed
  • Agility Stat Unmaxed

You need to max all of these stats for your current rank. Once you do, the text will disappear, and in its place will be a yellow/golden bar. This bar is your Limitbreak gauge. You now need to max the bar until it’s at maximum.

You’ll know when the Limitbreak bar is maxed out because it will start to animate like a liquid, so when it’s full, you can then press G without your weapon out to rank up. To complete/access some of the harder content in Peroxide, you must Limitbreak to get better stats and a higher level cap.

How to Charge Limitbreak in Peroxide

To charge the Limitbreak, you have the choice of a wide variety of mostly combat-focused content, however there are a few exceptions to this. Your options include the following:

  • Invasions
  • Incursions
  • Time Gates
  • Defeating NPC hollows
  • Defeating NPC guards
  • Performing a Konso on a lost soul (Soul Reaper exclusive)
  • Talking to NPC's in all worlds (this only works once per npc)

It should be noted that generally, the tougher challenges grant more limitbreak progress. For example, completing a S- Rank Invasion will grant more limitbreak progress compared to a B rank invasion. Additionally, limitbreak bars become tougher to fill up as you rise through the ranks, but at the same time you unlock better sources of limitbreak progress.

Total stat points every rank

Combat stat points:

  • F: 70 combat stat points
  • D: 140 combat stat points
  • C: 210 combat stat points
  • B: 280 combat stat points
  • A: 350 combat stat points
  • S-: 420 combat stat points
  • S: 490 combat stat points
  • S+: 570 combat stat points

Reiatsu and Agility stat points (each):

  • F: 22 stat points
  • D: 44 stat points
  • C: 66 stat points
  • B: 88 stat points
  • A: 110 stat points
  • S-: 132 stat points
  • S: 154 stat points
  • S+: 180 stat points

Other information

As well as combat/support stat requirements, a few extra requirements must be filled to go past certain ranks.

-To go beyond C rank, you must unlock your Shikai/Schrift/Fullbring. Arrancars are the exception to this, as they don't need to do a quest in order to unlock their Resurreccion.

-To be able to rank up to S+, you must have your Bankai/Vollstandig/Segunda Etapa/Twin Soul completely mastered. More information can be found on their respective pages on how to unlock and master these modes.

You also unlock a few bonuses at certain ranks:

  • At B rank, you can stay in your first mode indefinitely, without needing to charge up 20% of the bar beforehand.
  • At S rank, once you reach 150 Agility you can flashstep while carrying objects and players/NPC's
  • At S- rank, you unlock a checkpoint for your 2nd mode boss that allows you to skip the first parts of the fight and directly challenge the boss, making unlocking your 2nd mode much easier.
  • You also unlock new ranks of invasions with better loot chances and higher difficulty.
