Hellverse Ichigo

Bosses fonte original

Night of the Wungus Event Boss.

Description

You can summon Hellverse Ichigo at The Harvester for 666 Wungus.

alternatively Hellverse Ichigo spawns every 4 hours in a server.

He spawns in the river of Karakura, has only one life but is very tanky and deals alot of damage using Vastorage Skills, Getsuga Tenshou and Ryodan.

Rewards

Hellverse Ichigo has multiple rare drops. You need 4%-9% damage (depending on server size) to get a reward.
