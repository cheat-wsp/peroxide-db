"If you wield your blade without regard for your own life, you will find yourself unable to protect anything."

Description

Flower is one of the Rare Shikai elements that Soul Reapers can obtain in the game. In its Bankai state you get a a custom M2 counter, M2, uppercut, and a passive that spawns a sword that deal 50 percent of the damage of every third hit.
