Hollow

Hollow fonte original

Hollows are a type of monstrous lost soul who can harm ghosts as well as humans.

Description

Hollow is the only one among all Races with multiple Evolution Stages. Hollow NPCs can spawn in Karakura, Hueco Mundo and in Menos Forest. When non-player Hollows die they leave behind remains that can be eaten by Player Hollows for Exp.

Check out the Hollow Starter Guide in order to get started as a Hollow.
