Reputation

Mecânicas fonte original

The Factions

  • Las Noches - Hueco Mundo.
  • Soul Society - Soul Society.
  • Wandenreich - Wandenreich.

Description

Reputation is how much a Faction Agrees with what your doing.

  • If you have a red bar, it means you have Negative Reputation.
  • If you see The primary color of that Faction, it's Positive Reputation.

Reputation controls Enemies NPCs and Ally NPCs, also is what determines if they are ally or foe.

  • If you have High Positive Reputation with a Faction they will no longer target you if you are the opposing Faction, that Faction will also allow you to do their invasions.
  • If you have Low or Negative Reputation with your own Faction, they will not longer treat you as a ally, and will attack you, will not allow you to do invasions.
  • If you have total neutral will all Reputation, your Faction will treat you as ally but not others.

Arrancar Into Quincycar

  • You need High or Medium Positive Reputation With Wandenreich to be a Quincycar.

You can reset your reputation at the Shady Arrancar.

Other information

  • The shinigami and hollows are in a relationship where killing shinigami will bring you to the positive for hollows. Therefore a negative bar means you are technically an ally with Las Noches/Hueco Mundo.
  • Ways to get + or - reputation include killing a faction's npc, doing invasions and giving soul gourds to the respective npcs.
  • Killing a Faction's Base Guard earns you -5 Reputation for the Killed Faction.
