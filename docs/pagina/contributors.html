Become a Wiki Supporter

Want to help make the Peroxide Wiki better and get recognized on this page? Join our Peroxide Wiki Discord and contribute by:

  • Sharing game resources: GIFs, screenshots, and recordings of abilities, bosses, or events
  • Reporting updates: Game changelogs, new content, and balance changes
  • Providing data: Item stats, drop rates, quest information, NPC dialogue
  • Testing features: Verify wiki accuracy and help catch errors
  • Giving feedback: Suggest improvements and report missing or outdated information

Active and reliable contributors can be promoted to Wiki Supporter status, which includes special recognition both in Discord and on this page!
