Johan is a npc in Wandenreich who can attach your cybernetics and give you a special Schrift Weapon. He is located in the laboratory behind General Alberich’s shop.

Cybernetics

If the player presents Johan with a Body Modification part and asks for him to install a cybernetic onto their body, he will give them the part’s respective cybernetic and it’s Utility Skill (the Combat Skill must be unlocked via leveling up). The player can also ask him to remove their cybernetic, so that they may get another one. In order to install cybernetics, you must be level 60+.

For more information, visit the Body Modifications page.

Schrift Weapons

The player can ask Johan to fix them up a special schrift by giving him a Strange Ore. Doing so will change the player’s schrift bow into a more traditional weapon. If you’re unhappy with the schrift weapon you get, you can use another Strange Ore to reroll it. Once you have a schrift weapon, you can freely change between the weapons and the bow by asking Johan. To get a shrift weapon, you obviously need to have your schrift unlocked.

The weapons themselves can be seen on the Quincy page.
