Update Log

Meta fonte original

Description

This update log keeps tracks of new content, bug fixes, and balance changes that are released. The timeline for the tracking of updates starts at the Jester Update which was released on September 1, 2026. For a detailed overview events such as the Halloween event 2025, or Easter Event, please refer to their respective pages. The exact times of the updates are not the time the game was updated per say, but rather it is the time the update log was released on the peroxide discord server. The format for the dates are dd/mm/yy.


Balance Log #2.5 | 9/9/2026

+ Acceleration's Segunda M2 counter passive Lightspeed Reflexes now goes through block. Reduced self-stun/end-lag a bit

+ Removed Music's 20% Agility Segunda buff. Tempo and Muse Beat remain the same

+ Amazon's Twin Souls' Violent Execute will now auto execute opponents below the final hit's damage

+ Explosion Bankai's dash passive can now hit the same target multiple times but they will only explode on contact once, meaning the explosions themself have to hit the person.

+ Explosion Bankai's Blast Overdrive can now hit the same target multiple times but they will only explode on contact once, meaning the explosions themself have to hit the person.

Balance Log #2 | 8/9/2026

+ Reiatsu Ball now has 10% increased spirit scaling

+ Ryodan will now stop when perfect blocked

+ Flawless Pierce endlag when missed decreased to 1s, dash distance increased, speed increased, hitbox increased, sends enemies flying with first tick of damage, and is now true of your last m1. Endlag when landed reduced to 0.45s, and time to next flashstep reduced to 2s.

+ Heilig Schlag will now detonate when it hits a player

+ Self damage no longer procs Apocalypse's Vollstandig passive Deaths Toll.

+ Tenran now has 0.8s of endlag if completely missed

+ Danku's cooldown increased to 50s. Attacks that Danku blocks now count as your perfect block if you are near the Danku AND said attack.

+ Horin's hit damage increased to 8, and pull damage increased to 5

+ Explosion Bankai's dashes and Blast Overdrive can now only hit once

+ Amazon's Violent Slash damage decreased to 6

+ Amazon's Violent Punish damage increased by 33%

+ Dimension's Lightning Shower Rain can no longer be cancelled once they're flying in the air (Only before).

Balance Log #1 | 5/9/2026

+ The following skills no longer hit through walls and need line of sight: Soukotsu, Lacerate, Dispersing Barrage, Flash Draw, Counter Strike, Orbital Pull (V variant), Ultimate Crystal of Conduction, Renunciar, Storm Blitz and Cosmic Illusion.

+ Bow M1s now have an extra 12.5% spirit scaling

+ Increased Bow M2 spirit scaling to 150% + 25% extra spirit scaling

+ Daggers and Savage Claws now have 15% chip damage

+ One Handed Claws now have 20% chip damage

+ Spears now has a much longer and further M2

+ Fists now has a 10% faster M2. This does not stack with the Acceeleration Segunda passive

+ Increased Gun's M2 hitbox s a bit

+ Greatswords now block break on last M1, however, the last M1 is perfect blockable and perfect dashable, but perfect blocking it will not stun the user

+ Acceleration's Time Acceleration no longer deals self damage if Crystal of Prodigy is equipped

+ Manifest's created weapons M2's now have 50% extra spirit scaling in their realm.

+ Manifest's Curtain Call now has a V variant where it always uses the sniper

+ Manifest's Spiraling Abyss of Brilliance arena will now use your spirit realm when using Ultimate Crystal of Prodigy or Ultimate Crystal of Spirit + Manifest's Realm anvil summoned on downslam damage decreased to 3

+ Added a new passive to Manifest's Segunda that works both outside and inside the domain

+ Buffed Dimension's Twin Souls passive for chip block damage (Chip % of non-scaled damage -> Chip % of scaled damage) P.S I'll find out what this means later

+ Jester's Taunt healing amount increased to 25 + 50% spirit efficiency, and lowered it's cooldown to 29s

+ Jester's Wrath evasive requirement increased to 1.5s

+ Jester's Greed now grants HardArmor during the transformation instead of HyperArmor

+ Rachepfeil: Now explodes on contact or damaging someone, more mastery now increases the minimum low health damage increase scaling, bigger and more accurate hitbox, and added an extra 5 damage on hit

+ Geki is now non-evasiveable

Jester Update | 1/9/2026

Content Changes

+ Added a new fullbring element, Jester

+ Added a new Godly perk, Sponge

+ Added a new Ultimate Crystal, Ultimate Crystal of Ichor

+ Added a new skill that is obtainable from the Nnoitra raid, Vertical Slash

+ Remade one of the Arrancar Resurrección outfits

Balance Changes

+ Missing Soukotsu entirely now has increased end-lag (0.3s -> 0.6s)

+ DuoScale moves scaling only off Strength now scale at 90% efficiency (100% -> 90%)

+ Ultimate Crystal of Spirit longer drains your Reiatsu but still stops you from regenerating Reiatsu for 10 seconds

+ Buffed Kugo's spirit multiplier (12% -> 15%)

+ Lowered the start-up of Lumen Zip (0.5s -> 0.3s)

+ Savage Skewer, Illusion Smash (Last hit is still only strength scaling), Cosmic Illusion (First hit is only spirit damage, rest is strength), and Lightning Shower Rain all now DuoScale

+ Cosmic Illusion's first hit damage increased. (0 -> 3)

+ String's Lacerate now has a max distance for the auto target that scales with mastery

+ Arrow Barrage: Wind-up decreased by 0.1s, end-lag if landed decreased by 0.25s, end-lag if completely missed increased. (0.55s -> 0.65s), and stun time per tick reduced. (0.7s -> 0.6s)

+ Blut Arterie now takes 16% increased damage instead of 20%

+ Speed of Duality's White Singularity projectile now scales with mastery

+ Duality's Empryal Assualt is now 360° blockable

+ Duality's Dark Sun can now absorb more ink to reduce charge time by up to 2 seconds

+ Vermin's Black Pestilence is now DuoScaling

+ Odyssey's Wormhole Burrow and Dispersing Arrow are now DuoScaling

+ Apocalypse's Volstanding dash passive hands are now 360 blockable

+ Lanza del Relampago is now move-stackable

+ Dessgarron: Wind up lowered to 1.25s, wind up with crystal of prodigy lowered to 1s, 100% extra spirit scaling, Increased base speed to 250m/s

+ Lightlike now hits all targets instantly

+ Volan Cero: Stun time reduced to 0.075s, damage reduced to 0.5, wind up per cero lowered to 0.8s, is now 360° blockable, and fires an extra cero

+ Music's Sonic Boombox will not proc crystal effects if the target is stunned (not including by the move itself)

+ Vessel's SELF-HATRED now DuoScales

+ Joy of Battle: Has HyperArmour instead of i-frames, wind up lowered to 1s, lowered cooldown to 300s, and 20 second cooldown if cancelled in wind up.

+ Kurohitsugi: Hado 91 no longer procs crystals when dealing damage

+ Perfect blocking Sokatsui now stuns the caster

+ Damage for touching Gravity's rocks in field reduced to 2

+ Gravity's rocks in their field damage reduced when falling (6 + 100% scaling to 5 + 75% scaling)

+ Perfect Blocking Gravity's Field will now stun the caster

+ Increased the speed of Gravity Bankai's push counter

+ Wind's Tailwind no longer gives mode bar upon reflecting damage

+ Flower Tornado: Stun time lowered to 0.3s, and will now have knockback when used in succession with Aerial Tandem

+ Flower's Slaughter Scape now has less i-frames

+ Flower's Bankai M2 is now a block breaker, and has increased damage scaling by 25%

+ Lightning's Falling Thunder is now DuoScaling

+ End-lag when Lightning's Bankai passive EX ADVANCING STORM is missed completely is increased to 0.65s

+ Glacier's Cry of a Thousand Moons now has 0.8s of end-lag when completely missed

+ Glacier's Bankai passive Raimei Slamdown cooldown lowered to 2s

+ Chaos' blaster now has less end-lag if cancelled during wind up

+ Chaos' Segunda passive Cero Eviscerate cooldown lowered to 16s

+ Jester's wrath is now evasiveable after 1s

+ Jester's Greed damage nullification cool-down increased to 16s, and M2 passive cool-down decreased to 8s
