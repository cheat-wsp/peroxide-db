Halloween event 2025

Eventos fonte original

Added the ability of Summon Cauldron to all non-Hollow players, throwing a player inside will reward with 5 Halloween Candy, as well as, being able to steal up to 30 Halloween Candy from another player(30 steal+5 for sacrifice). Cooldown on the same victim is 5 minutes, furthermore they must be around your level and rank or higher(DONT KILL NEWBIES).

Every 5 sacrifices you're given a Thread of Fate that summons the Oni White boss.

You may now go to the menos Forest or Storm delve to turn in Halloween Candy for Halloween Essence or Ingus potion. After obtaining Ingus you can upgrade your Ingus to Legendary Ingus.

The Cauldron can also be used to grip NPC's without losing reputation (like the Shinigami guards)
