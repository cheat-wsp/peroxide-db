Spirit Names

Outros fonte original

In Peroxide there are many names your Shikai can have once you unlock your Spirit Realm, this goes for both Soul Reapers and Arrancars. You are able to change the name of your Shikai through using Product Essences by talking to Kisuke at Karakura, Or by clicking the Robux icon on the top of your screen .
