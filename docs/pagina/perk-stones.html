Perk Stones

Itens fonte original

  • Perk Stones are items that can be applied to your Accessories to give special buffs to them

Description

Perk stones are items that can be dropped from Arrancars, Vastolordes, and Hollows in storm delving. These stones can be applied to your accessories that give significant buffs. Perk stones are obtained in four different rarities. Those rarities are common, rare, legendary, and godly. Legendary stones and godly stones are only dropped by espadas.

Perk Stones

These Accessories are obtainable from Storm Delving .

Perk stone Attributes Obtainment
Common perk stone A common variant of the Perk Stones, gives a random Common perk to an accessory of choice. (You have to be wearing it)
  • Strengthened:+4 Strength
  • Hardened: +4 Vitality
  • Spiritual: +4 Spirit
  • Agile: +4 Agility
  • Resevoir: +4 Reiatsu
  • Artificial: 5% more damage against NPCs
  • Parkour: 20% faster climbing speed and slightly higher if stacked
  • Storm Delve
Rare perk stone A Rare variant of the Perk Stones, gives a random Rare perk to an accessory of choice. (You have to be wearing it)
  • Aerial: Take 5% less damage during air combos
  • Proficient: Skills take 10% less reiatsu
  • Shielded: The first attack to hit you outside of combat is voided and does not damage you (this does NOT stack)
  • All-Round: +2 to all stats
  • Swift: 10% extra flashstep speed out of combat
  • Speedster: +1 out of combat flashstep
  • Slayer: 8% more damage against NPCs
  • Storm Delve
Legendary perk stone A Legendary variant of the Perk Stones, gives a random Legendary perk to an accessory of choice. (You have to be wearing it)
  • Boxer: M1s deal 3% more damage
  • Strength Resistant: Strength related damage deals 3% less damage
  • Spirit Resistance: Spirit related damage deals 3% less damage
  • Doubled Edge: Deal 4% more damage but also receive 8% more damage
  • Dexterious: Second form lasts 10% longer
  • Underdog: Enemies that have higher health deal 3.5% less damage to you
  • Sniper: Deal 5% more damage when hitting enemies that are atleast 50 studs away from yourself
  • Conqueror: Deal 10% more damage against NPCs
  • Storm Delve
Godly perk stone A Godly variant of the Perk Stones, gives a random Godly perk to an accessory of choice. (You have to be wearing it)
  • Respiration: Perfect dodging an attack will heal you for 1.5% of your max hp
  • Reaper: Deal 15% more damage to NPCs
  • Engager: Lower all cooldowns by 2 seconds upon block breaking an opponent, does not work on evasives and ultimates
  • Sponge: Increases all incoming healing by 5%
  • Storm Delve
