Easter Event

Eventos fonte original

Description

The Easter Event was added on the 9th of March and is an event that is about collecting 6 different eggs, all with different rarities. They are droppable from hollows who have the respective egg and can be found in different maps told by Fluffy Chad in the ASR Cafe in Karakura Town.

Eggs

There are 6 different eggs, all with rarities. The eggs and their rarities are as listed:

  • Kon Egg (Common)
  • Hollow Egg (Common)
  • Grimmjow Egg (Rare)
  • Vasto Egg (Rare)
  • Vizard Egg (Legendary)
  • Hogyoku Egg (Legendary)
