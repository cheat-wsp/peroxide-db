Aizen Raid

Bosses fonte original

Aizen is one of the five Raid Bosses in the game. This boss scales with the player´s level and the amount of player´s fighting him. He has 3 phases he is guaranteed to drop time relics, he also has a 2% chance to drop his outfit that gives boost to your stats.


Phases

Phase 1: Aizen starts off normal. He uses a few abilities and goes easy on the player. That is, until you anger him.

Phase 2: Aizen decides to finally get rid of you. He enables his Shikai "Kyōka Suigetsu" and starts summoning tons of clones to help him fight the player. If all fails, he has a plan.

Phase 3: Enough games. Aizen uses the power of the Hogyoku to power himself up and attack the player with all he's got. Using unique abilities like the Final Kurohitsugi (Kurohitsugi but bigger and stronger, not obtainable for players) and a Purple Lanza del Relampago (Not obtainable for players) he deals tons of damage. It is possible to solo with good aoe attacks or a good build. It is recommended you do it with friends but it isn’t too hard to solo. The more people you do it with the more stronger and more health the boss has.
