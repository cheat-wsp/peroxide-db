XCution Headquarters

Organizações fonte original


Description

The XCution Headquarters is the homebase where Fullbringers get teleported to upon use of the Cellphone that Aura Michibane gives them. It holds many NPCs, many which are useless and important, and is essential for Fullbringers to obtain their Fullbring and Twin Soul, as well as travel between Soul Society and Hueco Mundo to be able to do Invasions. To exit the XCution Headquarters, use your Cellphone once again, and you will be teleported back.

Characters

In the XCution Headquarters, there are a total of 4 NPCs you can find in the room:

  • Aura Michibane, who transports the players to Soul Society or Hueco Mundo if their reputation allows them to, and allows the player to obtain their Fullbring after 3 quests she gives out. For more information, check out the Fullbringer Progression/Starter Guide.
  • Shūkurō Tsukishima, who teleports the player to their Apartment.
  • Grey Star, who likes to chill on the couch.
  • Joe, who sleeps at the counter.

Notes

  • The XCution Headquarters is directly based of the Headquarters seen in the anime the game references, "Bleach"
