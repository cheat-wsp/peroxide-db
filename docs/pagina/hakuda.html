Hakuda

Mecânicas fonte original

Description

Hakuda is a fighting style (Soul Reaper and Arrancar Exclusive) with 9 close range moves, and can be learnt by completing "Lesson of Strength" and defeating Yoruichi (Who can be found at Urahara Shop).

Yoruichi's stats scale off yours, so stat reset max strength or low stats can help make the fight more possible.

There are currently 9 abilities in the game right now, and three lessons. Each training gives three skills, first being the basic "Lesson of Strength", second being the "Lesson of Agility" and third being the "Lesson of Vitality". You need a certain amount of stats to do each respective lesson. Shunko is also confirmed to be added soon.

While Vasto Lordes and Quincycars could obtain Hakuda, it has since been removed from them.
Note: Vasto Lorde are able to obtain Arrankuda

Arrancar Hakuda

Arrancar Hakuda is a fighting style exclusive to Vastocar Arrancar's. This fighting style can be obtained by defeating the Orochi boss and unlike Yoruichi after beating him once you will gain all 9 moves of arrancar hakuda.

Note: You do not need to have obtained all of Yoruichi's Hakuda moves before you can fight Orochi. The only requirements to fight orochi is to be out of combat and be a vastocar or vasto lorde.
