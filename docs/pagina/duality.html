Duality

Outros fonte original

CampoValor
Vollständig

"living or dead, all black things that exist in this world... belong to me."

Description

Duality is one of the common Schrift that Quincy can roll in Peroxide. Duality Vollständig passive is whenever you dash or Heavy attack (M2) you Spread more ink around you. The ink buffs your moves damage and when you stand in it you take 25% less damage.
