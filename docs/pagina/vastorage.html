Vastorage

Hollow fonte original

Vastorage is an end-game mode that can be obtained by Shinigami. Although it will not impact your progression up until level 165, it will most likely impact your experience in PvP.

Activation

You activate your Vastorage by getting knocked while in Bankai mode and having your Vizard mask on. When you get knocked, a cutscene will play where you begin to violently smash your head against the floor, before transforming into your Vastorage form, and letting out a powerful roar which knocks back anyone within its radius, slowing them and nullifying their flashstep. The transformation will heal you fully, and lasts until you run out of Vizard bar. When it runs out, you will go back to your Shikai form.

Your stats are multiplied by 1.13x but your max hp is reduced by 0.3x.

Vastorage no longer passively drains but your Bankai bar becomes your hp bar. If you land skills you heal and gain more bar, if you get damaged your Bankai bar goes down. When you have no more bar you'll get knocked and be vulnerable to gripping.

Obtainment

Prerequisite: Level 165 and 3 Impure Hogyokus in total.

  1. Become a Vizard by using your first Impure Hogyoku as a level 165 (and above) Soul Reaper.
  2. Obtain 2 more Impure Hogyokus and use one. (You should see a text saying "The threads of destiny align." after you use it) Keep the third Impure Hogyoku in your inventory and grip 30-50 Vasto Lordes with your Vizard Mask on. (You will receive the confirmation message in red text: "You feel the urge to kill more..." when killing the first Vasto Lorde, and another that says: "The Impure Hogyoku disappeared..." when you have killed the last Vasto Lorde.)
  3. Talk to Ranburā (He's next to Bus Station 1/Fountain behind the stairs/Fusionfriends NPC)
  1. If you get the dialogue option "Him?" click it and read what he says instead, if you don't get it, then end dialogue and talk to him again until it appears. If you skip through the "Him?" dialogue by accident, reattempt interacting with him until you get it again.
  2. Now you have to find a Vasto Lorde with the name he has given you (it's a random spawn but there's a higher chance a Vasto Lorde with that name spawns in your server) A really easy way to find the Vasto Lorde with the name given is to join a small server (5 or less players) and keep checking the Gourge and Vasto cave and kill every Vasto Lorde so they keep respawning.
  3. Grip the Vasto Lorde WHILE YOUR VIZARD MASK IS ON with the name Ranburā gave you. Ensure you are in combat with the Vasto Lorde before executing it to receive credit. If done correctly, "Something doesn't feel right..." will appear in red text at the bottom of your screen above your move binds.
  4. Congratulations, you have unlocked Vastorage. Celebrate by having your friends and/or enemies brutally maul you to death while in Bankai with your Vizard mask on. Your Vastorage will then activate.

Appearance/Note

  • You DO NOT need full Bankai/Vizard Mastery for you to get Vastorage.(But It Is Recommended)
  • During obtainment, you can use condensed hollow bait (the cube with lines) to possibly spawn your Vasto Lorde.
  • Rage and Gigantic Cero colors change depending on your Reiatsu Color (Mode-up animation will always be red).
  • You keep your hair-color.
  • Accessories and Outfits aren't shown.
  • You can reroll your Vastorage Body for 10 or with PE.
  • Your face and head-shape is determined by your Vizard Mask.
