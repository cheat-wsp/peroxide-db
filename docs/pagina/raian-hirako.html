Raian Hirako

Raian Hirako is a npc in Karakura who specializes in vizard training. He is located near the park.

He will offer you to spar as a regular shinigami, but once you are a vizard will offer vizard training for 200 yen per session, which works like second mode training. After beating him with vizard your mastery with vizard will increase causing your vizard mask to last longer

Phases

His fight consists of Three Phases:

Phase One; He will be in his base transformation with the Vizard mask equipped

Phase Two; He will enter his Shikai, which is Glacier

Phase Three; He will enter Bankai

Fighting him will be free if your vizard mask is at maximum mastery.

He is located near the park entrance near the Hobo Monk and Shady Shingami.

Trivia

  • It usually takes 6 to 8 wins to fully master your Vizard.
  • Raian Hirako has Iframes when he pops Shikai.
  • If you cannot find him use the bus to the Hospital then turn left and after reaching a crossing turn left again. Then you should be able to see a npc with blonde hair.
