V - The Vanguard

CampoValor
The Vanguard


V - The Vanguard is a Delve NPC boss that uses the Apocalypse Schrift, he will spawn at the start of the delve ramdomly in one side of it you can hear a dark music when he is close to you. He can also be found in Wandenreich as a NPC that you can talk

Stages

V - The Vanguard has two main stages:

  • Base form
  • Schrift (Stage 1)
  • Volt (Stage 2)
  • Vital Surge (Stage 3)

Even while in his Schrift, he can still use the Apocalypse ultimate, so be careful.

Abilities

True Volt

V - The Vanguard has a unique Vollständig form called True Volt, which only he can use. True Volt heals him 100% of his HP, and he will keep popping it if you do not kill him fast enough.

It is possible to duo or even solo this boss. He has 16,000 HP, but V - The Vanguard also gains a hidden buff if he executes the Storm Server Boss, which increases the chance of his drops. It also make him immune to PvE multiplier damage. This is his Charged State.

Charged State

If V - The Vanguard consumes the Storm Boss, he will gain immunity to PvE damage but still have the same health.
