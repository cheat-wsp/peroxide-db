Herupa

Outros fonte original

Herupa in Peroxide is an NPC whose purpose is to enhance your board jobs.

Function

Herupa can enhance your board jobs, making them so that instead of gaining exp you boost one of your stats. Herupa can do this enhancing process for 50 yen, although the player can cancel this or change it to increase another stat at any time. Changing stat increase will still cost 50 yen.

Note: if you leave the server, you won't have this enhance back when rejoining.

Location

Herupa can be always be found next to a job billboard, which means he is in several locations at Karakura city and at one place in Hueco Mundo.

Trivia

  • Judging from his appearance and mastery in reishi condensation, Herupa is a Quincy.
