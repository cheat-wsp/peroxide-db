Monolith Quest

Outros fonte original

The Monolith Quest is a quest that the Arrancar player must do in order to obtain their Resurrección weapon.

Quest Guide

First, the player must go to the Las Noches Ruins

Directions: Walking straight from the raids/invasions area in hueco mundo take the first right turn and go straight until you see a structure.

Next, speak to the monolith located at the top. An easy way to go up the monolith is to walk up the flight of stairs in the main entrance. An alternate way is to take a left near the main staircase and climb up the slanted rock. While it is faster, this route has more enemies that could ambush you.

From there, the player must interact with the monolith to absorb its energy. When the player does so, there will be purple text appearing on the bottom of the screen, hinting to the player that they must go to Menos Forest.

The player will then be put on a time limit. If time runs out and the player has not reached Menos Forest by then, a message will appear, informing the player that the ancient energy of the monolith has dispersed. If you made it in time, congratulations. You have obtained your very own Resurrección Weapon.

Notes & Trivia

  • There is an NPC known as Vidente who sets you on the path for this quest; however, he is rarely visited due to the fact that speaking to him is optional.
  • Avoid combat with anything in your path. Fighting NPCs/Players will cause you to be combat tagged, which greatly reduces your flashsteps.
  • If you have the Acceleration Resurrección, it is recommended to activate the speed passive move, which allows you to move faster than usual
