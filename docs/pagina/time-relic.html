Time Relic

Itens fonte original

This page is currently being worked on.
Some things may be incomplete.

Time Relics are used to purchase Crystals from the Shady Shinigami NPC for 10 Time Relics and 50 Yen. Alternatively, they can be sold to Gunther for 35 Yen each.

Time Relics are obtained from Time Gates (always 15 but rare drop), Invasions, and Time Bubbles (9 or less | distorted Time Bubbles give 19). They may also drop from Incursions but this has yet to be verified.

You can also get them from killing Storm Vastocars in the The Storm Server Event, which happens in Hueco Mundo. They can drop around 0-6 Time Relics each when you deal around 60% of damage to each Storm Vastocar.

Another way to get time relics is from the hollow egg in the 2024 Easter Update. Upon collecting the egg you have a chance to get 3x time relics
