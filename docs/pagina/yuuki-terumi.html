Yuuki Terumi

"Feel the Wrath! Witness the dance of death! I am the one true Susanoo! DIE!"

Description

Yuuki Terumi "GOD" is a Boss NPC located at the Karakura Town Fountain near the Fusionfriends (Trade Hub) NPC and the Ranburā NPC. He will always spawn at XX:35, (5 minutes after the time gate ends), and despawn after 20 minutes (XX:55). Once he spawns, a green-highlighted message will pop up, saying "The Time Gates manifests a familiar face once again."

He is also located at the top of the tower in Hueco Mundo but this version of Yuuki Terumi can only be fought in the 2nd stage of the Horn of salvation and will say "Got something you need to take care of?" along with a red text reading "You do not qualify for this boss" if you have not started the quest or have not got to this stage of the quest yet.

Yuuki Terumi, just like an Arrancar (even though he isn't due to him lacking a mask), can use Segunda Etapa. He'll use it when he's halfway through his health-bar, and will also have passive regen similar that is greater than the player's.

He uses the Vessel moveset as well as Hakuda, but as other bosses do, he has a few unique skills (some are shared with the Ulquiorra Raid Boss), one of them being a (much) longer-lasting version of the skill called AHAHAHAHAHA, and a move named stinging seal that drains mode and reiatsu as well as allowing him to follow up with Bite.

This boss is even harder than Orochi The Shura. He will Block, P-block, and Parry ALMOST every single skill and is immune to pve accessories.

Using Starrk's Revolver de Los Lobos, Starrk's Metralleta or Flower Ult will make him run out of your range.

He MUST BE KILLED in order to unlock Horn of Salvation (HoS), since he's the final boss of the Quest-line stage 2.

A currently existing cheese allows any domain type move (Oasis' Floodgates of Paradise or Flower Ultimate) to trap him outside, and he can be cheesed with large and ranged AoE (preferably block break) attacks, as he will be too busy trying to break your kneecaps to remember to P-Block, or that he barely has any health left. You could also just run and use moves that bypass block.(Arrankuda moveset). No time limit, go bonkers. This has been patched, however, currently, Odyssey's extenders as well as other moves may still function. He also blocks uptilt moves less often (Odyssey, Wind, Flame). Currently, it's recommended to turtle his ass (hold block and unblock when he uses crit), usually in conjunction with block bypassing moves. Recommended moves for bypassing include Ferocity, Expression, Showstopper, Unrivaled, and Headstrong. It should be advised that he'll evasive a lot, and has a move that drains mode and reiatsu. He will also run from any block bypassing moves (including the above), so try to m1 bait him (m1s into the move). He's also not immune to m2 counters or fire, Vessel's and Explosion's dashes, and he also runs from uncharged Starrk's Metralleta has since been patched. I (Epirus7) will update the changes as they roll out.

Using Ferocity guarantees his evasive will be wasted. Use Counter Strike, wait for him to use AHAHAHAHAHA, walk into it while blocking. M2 counter then IMMEDIATELY use Counter Strike, guaranteed hit. Make sure to waste his evasive before you do this so you can get a full combo in.

Punish endlag on Uoaring Phalant, make use of m2 counters and follow up with frame one moves (dimension extender, dispersing barrage + wind gem, Soukotsu).


PREPARE TO LOSE A LOT OF YEN.

You lose all of your mode when starting the fight.

He currently drops (Overdrived) Ouroboros Cloak, Lesser Terumi's Soul, Terumi's Laugh Essence, and Shadowed Head. All these things are VANITY ONLY, and are extraordinarily rare drops from killing him. Note that these are very valuable in trading, so if you're extremely desperate (and good at the game), go ahead.
