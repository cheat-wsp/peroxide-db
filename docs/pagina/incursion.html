Incursion

Outros fonte original

Arrancar, Soul Reaper and Quincy war parties randomly spawn within Karakura, Soul Society and Hueco Mundo to attack anybody who approaches them (unless you are the same race or have high reputation with the race invading).

Defeating all Npcs from the Incursion opens a portal to their respective dimension, e.g. Soul Reapers will spawn a Senkaimon. This is also one of the only ways to get to Wandenreich without being a Quincy, the other is having positive quincy reputation and buying a portal from gunther, though this is the only way to get it with negative Wandenreich reputation,

Races won’t attack their own land, meaning no Soul Reapers in Soul Society, and no Arrancar in Hueco Mundo.

You can also find out which race it is using the color of the beam, blue being quinces, gray being arrancars and black and grey being soul reapers.

Rewards

  • Defeating an Incursion enables you to bolster one stat of choice.
  • You can get a random Accessory.
  • 0.1% of a Time Remnant.
