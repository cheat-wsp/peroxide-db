Organizations are a mechanic found in Peroxide. Organizations may be created/joined by any group you're in. It is possible to join an Organization by talking to the Shady Arrancar NPC. Members of an organization can be identified via an icon of the organization above their head, as well as next to their username on the leaderboard. Hoving your mouse over said icon on the leaderboard should replace the player's name with a yellow text, showing you the name of said Organization.

While not required, Organizations help you identify allies without the use of a Party, and helps you and other people tell a player from a NPC in certain cases.
