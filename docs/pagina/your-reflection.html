Your Reflection

Outros fonte original

Your Reflection is a Fullbringer exclusive npc in Peroxide which you need to speak to in order to unlock your Twin soul.

Once you speak to the npc Apartment Computer in your quarters and choose the option "Execute command: 'Beckon'" the player will have their first encounter with this npc.

You will need to win a fight against them to unlock twin souls, after you get the twin souls you can talk to them anytime in your apartment to fight them again or to modify your skills.
