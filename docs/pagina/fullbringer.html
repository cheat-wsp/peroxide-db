Fullbringer

View all Fullbringer Clans.

Fullbringer's are humans who absorbed the spiritual energy of a hollow while still in the womb of there mother, they are born with the ability to see and interact with spirit but they only get there combat powers at random. Fullbringers are capable of manipulating the soul in an object they have a close attachment to in order to activate there "Fullbring"

Obtainment

Talk to the NPC that calls you "Best Friend" behind the gym as human to become Fullbringer.

Check out Fullbringer Starter Guide to learn how to progress further.

Appearance

Flashsteps are accompanied by a flickering of a green luminescence called Bringer Light.

Abilities

Fullbringers can use General Skills, Fullbringer Skills and one Fullbring.

Fullbring

Read Fullbringer Starter Guide: Part 3 to learn how to obtain Fullbring.

The First Mode, a Fullbringers Elemental Abilities.

Press G at 20% Mode Charge to release your Fullbring, once you reach B-Rank you can use it anytime indefinitely.

Rarity Chance Redirect
Common 79% Midas, String
Rare 20% Clock, Kether
Legendary 1% Dimension, Amazon
Each Fullbring has its unique weapon and skillset.

Twin Soul

Read Fullbringer Starter Guide: Part 4 to learn how to obtain Twin Soul.

The Second and last Mode of Fullbring.

Press G at 100% Mode Charge to release your Twin Soul.
Mastery
It has a time limit of 33 Seconds at Base and 190 Seconds at Max Mastery.

You get Twin Soul Skills immediately after beating the boss. They can be upgraded by increasing your mastery.
