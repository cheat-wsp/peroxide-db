Time Gate

Outros fonte original

The Time Gates are an event in Peroxide.

The Time Gates can spawn in every World at xx:00 and closes at xx:30 EST. They will teleport players to a lobby where they are put up in a team with other players and sent to a special map where PvP-based game modes play out. There are only three teams, those being Shinigami, Arrancars, and Quincies. The teams don't mean/do anything to individual players. For example, the Quincy team won't only have Quincies, it's all randomized.

Winning these game modes will give the player two random rewards, a decent amount of EXP, and the chance to boost a stat of their choice. For Point progression, winning a time gate rewards the winners with 75 points while losing only rewards 25.

Friendly fire exists within these game modes, so teams will need to plan out how they'll attack without harming their allies.

There is a Level average that's calculated when the teams are full. the game will attempt to pair teams against other teams with a similar average to keep the battles fair.

Gladiator

The Gladiator game mode consists of the three teams being sent to a gladiator arena. 2 players of each team will be sent in to battle each other. If one person on a team dies, another random player from their team is chosen. The goal of this game mode is to be the last team standing, the zone is first visible after 15 minutes, and is fully closed (same size as any other zone) at 17 minutes also a cool thing that after 17 minutes which team have the most people alive that team win.

Elimination

The Elimination game mode is a normal battle against the three teams. each kill rewards the team with 1 point. The first team to get 15 points OR the team that has the most points in the allocated time (10 minutes) will be regarded as the victors.

Strong Foe

In the “Strong Foe” mode, a player is randomly chosen to act as the “Team Leader.” This player stands out due to their enhanced health and increased damage. The main goal of this mode is to defeat the players of the opposing team, with a special emphasis on their leader.

This mode features a designated zone that players must remain within to avoid sustaining damage.

Team Leaders are easily recognizable to other players thanks to a Crown icon.

An additional feature of this mode is that regular players can respawn continuously if their Team Leader is still alive.

Capture The Flag (CTF) (REMOVED)

Capture the flag consists of each team getting a flag in their spawn area. You must steal other flags and return them to your own. This will give you one point. the goal of this game mode is to have the most points under the allocated time. While holding the flag, you cannot use evasive skills, making avoiding attacks more difficult.

Hitting an enemy enough will have them drop their flag. if it is not retrieved within ten seconds, it is instantly sent back to its respective team.

Domination

In the Domination game mode, there are three distinct areas that teams must control to accumulate points. The objective is to secure as many of these areas as possible by positioning team members within them. Each area under a team’s control contributes one point per second to that team’s score.

The current controlling team of each area is indicated, allowing for strategic gameplay as teams can overtake areas controlled by the opposition, and must also defend their own controlled areas from being captured.

The first team to get the most points or the first to get 150 under the allocated time (10 minutes) will be regarded as the victors.

Hard Point

In the “Hard Point” game mode, players are tasked with controlling a specific area that shifts its location approximately every 90 seconds. The objective of this mode is to accumulate as many points as possible by maintaining control over this area.

The match duration is set at 10 minutes, and the team with the highest point total at the end of this period or accumulates 75 points first is declared the winner.

Trivia

  1. When a player’s health points (HP) are fully depleted, they are automatically gripped. However, if they possess abilities such as Vastorage, Blast Overdrive, or Vital Surge, they will only be grounded instead. This rule applies even if these abilities are currently on cooldown. This rule also does not apply in Strong Foe, as Strong Foe already makes the player knocked naturally.
  2. In all game modes except Gladiator, the respawn time after a player’s character dies increases as the duration of the game progresses.
  3. In the Strong Foe mode, the designated zone inflicts damage solely on the team leader. However, regular players also become susceptible to this zone damage if their team leader has been eliminated.
  4. The Hard Point game mode is currently experiencing a known issue. Specifically, after a team achieves 75 points the hard point stops appearing but the game doesn't stop.
  5. In the Gladiator mode, if the game drags on for too long, a zone will start to close in.
  6. If two players in a team are more than 20 levels apart they will not go into a time gate together.
