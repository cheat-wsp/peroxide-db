Description

Gravity is one of the Rare Shikai elements available to Soul Reapers in Peroxide. Gravity's Bankai passive provides the user with an asteroid belt that inflicts passive damage on those in proximity. Additionally, the user gains access to a special heavy attack capable of creating spikes. Furthermore, as the third and final passive effect, the user acquires a special counter move within the gravity field, enabling them to lift and slam rocks into their opponent.
