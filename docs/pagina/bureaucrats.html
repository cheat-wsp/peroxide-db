How to Contact Bureaucrats

For serious issues: Leave a message on a bureaucrat's message wall for matters like vandalism, user disputes, or permission requests

Bureaucrat Responsibilities

Bureaucrats are the highest-level trusted users who oversee wiki operations and manage user rights.
Their responsibilities include assigning and removing user rights and promotions, protecting and deleting pages, blocking rule-violating users, configuring wiki settings and appearance, resolving community disputes, and coordinating with administrators and moderators on improvements and major updates.
