Adjuchas

Hollow fonte original

Description

Adjuchas are the third Evolution Stage of the Race, Hollow. Adjuchas NPCs can spawn in Hueco Mundo. They are the post-evolution of the Hollow Evolution Stage, Menos and the pre-evolution of Vasto Lorde. Non-Player Adjuchas(s) leave behind their body when they are killed. Their bodies take a long time to eat, so if a Hollow wants to eat one, they should make sure no other NPCs are around to interrupt their meal.

Obtainment

Following the Hollow Starter Guide, after being alive as a Gillian for 30 minutes AND getting 30 kills (must have the killing blow to receive kill credit), the Gillian will automatically turn into an Adjuchas.

Progression

There are two different ways to continue from here:

  • Following the Hollow Starter Guide, after surpassing 1700 Evolution EXP, the Adjuchas will automatically turn into a Vasto Lorde.
  • An Adjuchas can bypass the first method by pressing G, which afterwards would give them an Arrow Mini-Game. Should an Adjuchas complete this Mini-Game, they will have their mask taken off. This turns them into the second stage of the Arrancar, the Adjucar. This method is unrecommended as you can't reverse this action, doing this will sacrifice the ability to progress any further to vastocar, which have significantly better buffs.

    Trivia

    Facts

    • NPC Adjuchas have a chance to spawn with the “Ancient” title, which does not change their looks but makes them a lot tougher and stronger. “Ancient” Adjuchas also give more kills towards Vasto Lorde Progression than regular Adjuchas.

    Community Tips

    • After reaching about level 90 or greater, an Adjuchas should be able to solo Storm Vastocars in Hueco Mundo, which is the fastest way to give an Adjuchas Vasto Lorde Progression. If you still fail at this, resort to fighting Adjuchas
    • Find a friend that can help you, and farm either the gourge or the cave.
