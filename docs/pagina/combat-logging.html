Combat Logging

Mecânicas fonte original

This page is currently being worked on.
Some things may be incomplete.

Combat Logging means you left with combat status on,if you do that you get more punishment than when dying

Combat Logging as a menos, will transform you back into a hollow and your level reverts back to 10, instead of level 15.

If you Logged as an Adjuchas, it will remove 120 kills/exp, instead of the usual 60 kills/exp.

In case of logging as an arrancar, quincy, fullbringer, or soul reaper. You will lose 50% of your money unless you have more than 200 then you will lose 1000.

Logging on Horn of salvation Quest will reset the kills to zero
