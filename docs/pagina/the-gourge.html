The Gourge

Outros fonte original

Description

The Gourge is located in Hueco Mundo, where it is one of the only ways to access the Menos Forest for Non-Hollow players without paying Gargan. its holds many dangers, with Vastolordes roaming around the Gourge, waiting for unsuspecting prey to fall among their kind...

Features

The Gourge is a spawn for Vastolordes, the strongest versions of Hollows and are able to use Arrancar moves such as Bala and Cero Oscuras, as well as Volcan Cero and Finger Cero, and Vasto Moves, such as Lord's Roar and Secuases. There are 5 spawn points for the Vastolordes, and they have a chance to become "Ancient" versions of themselves. Players can also travel to the far end of The Gourge, where they can fall down a pit to the Menos Forest, to exit go to the left side and scale up the wall to exit
