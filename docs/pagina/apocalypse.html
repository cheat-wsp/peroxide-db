Description

Apocalypse is one of the Rare Schrift that Quincy can roll in Peroxide. Apocalypse Vollständing passive is once you are in Vollständing all moves will auto execute players, Your dash summons dark hands that attack nearby opponents. Your M2 also gives the opponent a bell with a skull this is a death toll after 20 seconds the bell will ring and when the opponent gets hits during this time they get stunned.
