Description

Gunther is an NPC found in Karakura Town that allows non-Quincies players to access Wandenreich.

He is the npc which you talk to to become a Quincy as a human/lost soul

He can be used to reset your rep with Wandenreich for 460 yen (if negative) or gain rep if you have neutral/positive rep with Wandenreich by turning in a full Soul Gourd

You can sell him Crystals, Strange Ore and Body Modification.

He can also open a portal to Wandenreich if you have positive rep and are not a Quincy. (costs 250 yen)

Sell prices:

Time Relics: 30 yen

Common Crystals: 100 yen

Rare Crystals: 250 yen

Legendary Crystals: 500 yen

Strange Ore: 250 yen

Body Modification: 500 yen

Location

(His soul compass icon only shows if you're a human/lost soul)

Near bus terminal.

Leaning against the wall of the invasion building in Wandenreich.
