Zanpakutō

Shinigami fonte original

A Zanpakutō is the weapon that all Shinigami, Arrancars, and Vizards start out with. In the game, all Zanpakutō take the form of a katana by default. They all are nearly identical, with the only difference being the color of the scabbard and hilt. It functionally acts like a normal katana, but is actually the first weapon a Soul Reaper can get their hands on to hunt and harm Hollows and other spiritual presences.

It not only is the staple weapon of both races, but also houses the Soul Reaper's spirit and power in the form of their Shikai and, eventually, their Bankai power. In the case of Arrancars, it's a vessel for said Arrancar's power before they took off their mask, which is unleashed in an alternative, Shikai-like form upon usage of their Ressurectión and, when they've proven themselves, is improved upon obtainment of Segunda Etapa. Unlike Soul Reapers, they don't unlock their Ressurectión Weapon as soon as they get access to their Zanpakutō; They would need to complete the Monolith quest at The Ruins of Los Noches first. Vizards are a mixed case, where they combine the fighting spirit of their Shikai and Bankai with the power they've gotten through using their Mask to greatly improve the potency of their attacks, similarly to a Hollow.

Trivia

  • In Bleach, the Zanpakutō of Arrancars vary extremely, such as Nnorita Giliga’s Santa Teresa taking the form of a crescent scythe, or Coyote Starrk’s Los Lobos being his subordinate, Lilynette Gingerbuck.
  • According to the official Bleach wiki, Zanpakutōs can break. However, they can easily be repairs through the use of the owner's Reiatsu, the time it would take for the owner to infuse said Reiatsu to their Zanpakutō, and by their sheer will.
    • There are no instances of weapons breaking in the majority of Peroxide's progression as any race, bar the Bankai fight.
  • Zanpakutōs, canonically in Bleach, are made by a blacksmith in Soul Society by the name of Ōetsu Nimaiya. The process of a Zanpakutō's creation, mentioned in the wiki, is through a technique involving the merging of multiple Soul Reaper souls into an "Empty" Zanpakutō known as an 'Asauchi'.
    • Due to Soul Reapers immediately getting their Zanpakutō upon "talking" to Urahara at his shop, instead of interacting with a blacksmith to aquire an Asauchi which they train and improve to become a Zanpakutō, it's strongly implied the blacksmith in question does not exist or that the swords operate and works slightly differently in Peroxide, implying that it might directly be linked with the awakening of a Soul Reaper's yet-to-be-realized potential.
