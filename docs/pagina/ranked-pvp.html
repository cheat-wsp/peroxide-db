Ranked PVP

Outros fonte original

Overview

Ranked is a gamemode accessible through the menu, requiring a minimum level of 166 to enter (S+ rank for non arrancars, and S rank for arrancars). It is a gamemode where players are pitted to fight against players instead of NPCs, and as such, it has a very different "meta" than standard gameplay. Common "PVE Builds" such as Chaos builds that utilize many Ceros can be much weaker than Resureccions such as Beast.

In ranked, you earn "Battle Tower Points" and "ELO" to gain rewards or be ranked respectively. With the battle tower containing rewards such as Legendary Skills or exclusive Emotes. It costs 20 "BTP" to unlock every tier (until after tower level 50 which it costs 50 BTP), and a further amount to unlock or gain items such as Impure Hogyokus, Legendary Skills, Product Essence, Clan Rerolls, Shikai Elements, Resureccion's, Fullbrings, or Schrifts and Accessory Crates.

The modes are: 1v1s, 2v2s, 3v3s, 4v4s, and 5v5s, along with a region selector to gain a better chance of getting a certain region.

The top 50 players get titles in game chat of [#rank] name showing off their rank.
