Bounty Medallion

Itens fonte original

The Bounty Medallion can be bought from the General's Shop for 150¥, and is used for hunts by quincies to kill their enemies. Surviving a hunt will grant you a stat upgrade. Hunts are also used to obtain Schrift.

It's not recommended as most players you hunt when trying to get a scrift (Around lvl 15) will have noob protection.

Even if you are not a Quincy, you can still use this if you manage to sneak into Wandenreich and buy a Medallion.
