Description

Night of the Wungus is the second Halloween Event of Peroxide. In Karakura it turned Autumn and the city is full of Halloween decoration.

Content

The Night of the Wungus brought alot of content with it.

Wungus

Wungus is the currency used to buy from The Harvester.

NPCs

Added The Harvester. You can buy Rerolls, Skill Skins, the Wungus Curse and summon secret boss from his shop.

New boss Hellverse Ichigo that drops many rare items.

Hollows now have Pumpkin heads. Hollows drop the Pumpkin that they are wearing.
