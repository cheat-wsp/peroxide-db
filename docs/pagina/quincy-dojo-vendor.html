Quincy Dojo Vendor

Locais fonte original

The Quincy Dojo Vendor is an NPC located after the first set of stairs to Yhwach, then to the right, where the Quincy Dojo is located along with the Quincy Dueler Buarbiette. The Quincy Dojo Vendor offers a Dojo Membership for his Dojo for the price of ¥250. The Dojo does not allow weapons to be drawn inside the Dojo. If the player tries to draw their weapon inside the Dojo, they will kicked out and told to not draw their weapon out. The Dojo is an advanced Dojo that can give you 5 stat stars for a singular trained stat, and each stat star gives you an extra 33% stat exp when gaining stat experience through quests and dueling.

The Quincy Dojo Vendor's counterparts in each map are as follows:

The Gym Bouncer in Karakura Town, The Soul Society Dojo Vendor in Soul Society, and the Arrancar Dojo Vendor in The Alley in Hueco Mundo.

Note: The Gym Bouncer only provides access to the Dojo in Katakura town, which only provides a max of 3 stat stars for a chosen stat. Advanced Gyms are ones that are located in other maps than Katakura, and all provide a maximum of 5 stat stars for a chosen stat. Advanced Dojos are more expensive but more rewarding at the same time, but a cheaper but harder option with a chance of failure is fighting Duelers in the different maps. They also cannot raise your stats over 30, but still provide star bonuses.
