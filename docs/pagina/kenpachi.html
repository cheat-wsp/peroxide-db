Kenpachi

Shinigami fonte original

Boss that can spawn by chance (30%) in S- rank invasion within soul society. He doesn't scale of players stats and is recommended to take on with 4 or more players. A single Horn of salvation can solo him easily. And gripping him does not give a better chance for him to drop his godly accessory "The Kenpachi's Cloak".


Drops

  • Kenpachi Cloak [Godly] 25% drop rate
  • Kenpachi’s Roar Essence 25%(which you need to be a Vasto Lorde and have Lord’s Roar equipped to use) .
  • 250 yen

Useful information

  • Can only spawn in the Soul Society invasion
  • Very heavy hitter and uses Ryodan and something like Lord's Roar, pretty sure he has a grab move where he leaps to the player, brings them up and slams them into the ground (similar to vastorage m2)
  • Does not take any knockback and stun
  • Only activates Shikai once taking an amount of damage, does not use Bankai (yet)

Cheese Methods

He's a simple boss with only 4 attacks: Ryodan, Lord's Roar, Lunge, exclusive combo extender. He is very easy to infinite combo with Vessel in Segunda but requires a fast attacking weapon.

For killing him I'd recommend comboing the life out of him Kenpachi is immune to stun, running until CD is gone, rinse and repeat. He has short term memory and forgets about you if you are to far away and out of sight. You can spam moves run and heal and repeat until dead. And you can let him fight the reinforcements he will get them down around 20%.

Chaos can get on top of a tall building and spam Ult and chaos blaster.

Flower can use ult and spam ranged moves to kill him while he's outside the barrier.

Gravity can get on top of a tall object and spam 1, 2, 3.

Fire can sit on top of a tall object and let his fire skeletons kill him, this will take forever.

Glacier can just be glacier and infinite stun him forever easily.

Forest can be every forest user and spam ranged moves behind walls and run.

A Horn of salvation even spirit ones can just slap him around till he dies.

Glitch

In Arrancar S- Rank invasion if you die and your teamate(s) kill the bankai and tp in the reward zone you will respawn at the fighting area without stuns or taking damage allowing for an easy kill on Kenpachi
