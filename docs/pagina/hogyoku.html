Impure Hogyoku

Hollow fonte original

"An orb containing a unique substance thought to be capable of dissolving the boundaries between Soul Reaper and Hollow."

Description

The Impure Hogyoku (aka: Hog or Imp Hog) is a rare droppable item obtained from the normal Time Remnant. It allows Soul Reapers and Hollows (mainly Adjuchas and Soul Reapers) to become stronger by speeding up their progress or unlock new forms.

(You can use this as a menos BUT it does NOT speed your progression up. It changes your mask into that of an Ancient Menos' mask, which is purely cosmetic.)

Note: The Pure Hogyoku can also be used to speed up Adjuchas progression into Vasto Lorde. (Gives +1000 progression points on use aswell)

Obtainment

You can get an Impure Hogyoku by using a Time Remnant, a Godly item obtainable in Invasions, Incursions and Time Gates.

The chances of getting a Time Remnant are:

  • Invasions
    • (F/D ranks) – 0.1%
    • (B rank) – 0.2% (Not Recommended)
  • Incursions – 0.1% (Not Recommended)
  • Time Gate – 0.6% (Recommended)
  • Note: After 45 Wins in Time Gates, you are guaranteed to get a Time Remnant.
  • Weekly quests (Recommended)
  • Dr. Hydrogen's Scrapper Machine (you can get a Time Remnant or an Impure Hogyoku) P.S: Not Recommended
  • Trading

Usage

  • Obtaining Vizard
  • Obtaining Vastorage
  • Speeds up Adjuchas progression into Vasto Lorde (Gives +1000 progression points on use).
  • Turning into an Ancient Menos (Cosmetic change)

Tips

  • The most efficient way to get an Impure Hogyoku is to grind Time Gates or Weekly Quests.

Trivia

  • It is one of the hardest items to get.
  • Despite belief it is not one of the items with the most value in trading but it is the item used to measure value in trading.
