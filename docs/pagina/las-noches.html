Las Noches

Locais fonte original

A Land of Legends, Claimed by The Storm...

Las Noches is a area located in Hueco Mundo, which holds many powerful enemies, namely the Storm Arrancars that have taken over The Ruins and used it as their base. It is located next to The Alley and is the area where F to B-Rank Invasions from Soul Society and Wandenreich are located.

There are two NPCs that are located in Las Noches, one being Tohjin, who helps Non-Arrancar players by giving them a disguise during invasions, and The Monolith, which is essential for Arrancars to recieve their Resurrección weapon for their progression.
