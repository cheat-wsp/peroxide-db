Presents 2024

Eventos fonte original

Presents

There are three types of presents in the 2024 peroxide new years event, these been green, blue and red presents. The green presents are the most common and red presents being the rarest and most valuable.

Obtainment

Presents can be obtains through two means these being:

Present drop event

In Karakura town there are 2 drop events every hour on several spots on the map. The drop events happen every XX:25 and XX:55, Currently there are 6 drop spots in karakura town.

Two in inner karakura

The Fountain

The Three-way crossroad between the fountain and the Gunther NPC

Three in outer Karakura

In front of the the gym next to Kisuke.

In front of the Kisuke House.

in the SicklesChicken's parking lot.

Three on karakura lake

The are three drop spots on the lake, two are separated by the two bridges and the third being on the present trad-in island.

Protect Peroximas!

The second method to obtain presents is through "Protecting peroximas". In the karakura lake seperating inner- from outer karakura there is an island with presents and an NPC called Saint Peroxilas. talk with him and you will get the following dialog "Ho ho ho merry Peroximas how may I help you?" you get two options, one being "I'd like to help save Peroximas"

Choose this option and you will be summoned to protect a "wise mythical tree" from waves of Hollows, these hollows range from normal hollows to vasto lordes. The waves will become progressively harder and every 5 waves you will be get a large present drop all around the arena similar to present drops in karakura town. This event is a raid event so it's not made to be a solo venture.

Recommendation

If you want to farm as many presents as possible fast gather about 2 other players and keep playing the raid event to wave 5 then leave and start a new, it should take about 5 min to get to wave 5 and if you are a small party it will be a lot of presents for all of you.

Usage & Rarity

The presents are collected to be opened for limited accessories and skins. There are different rarity presents as well as rewards with each rarity present being worth 10 presents of the lower tier and giving better chances for higher rarity rewards.

Present trade-in

When interacting with Saint Peroxilas you have another option besides saving Peroximas, this being present trade-in. You can trade in 10 gifts of one rarity and get one of a higher rarity. For example trading in 10 Green presents would result in you receiving 1 blue present
