Shūkurō Tsukishima

Outros fonte original

Description

Shūkurō Tsukishima is a npc that is described as the best friend of the player. He spawns behind the Kindergarden Play Area and is located in the XCution Headquarters.

Features

When the player interacts with Shūkurō Tsukishima for the first time as a human or a Lost Soul, he will ask the player to join the Fullbringers. If you talk with him again at level 15 he tells you that you need to meet up with a friend of his, Michibane Aura, who will give you the Cellphone, which will take you to the XCution Headquarters.

When the player interacts with Shūkurō Tsukishima before unlocking their Twin Soul Fullbring in the XCution Headquarters, he will teleport the player to his/her Apartment. When the player interacts with him after unlocking their Twin Soul Fullbring, he will comment on how strong they are, and he will teleport the player to the Reality Shift Apartment.
