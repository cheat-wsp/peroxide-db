Rerolls

Outros fonte original

Description

Rerolls are a mechanic that allows you to change your Shikai/Resurreccion/Fullbring/Schrift Element and some other things.

How to do a Reroll

To perform a reroll, you need Product Essences. Visit Kisuke to use your PE or press the Robux icon at the top of your screen and then pressing the "toggle PE usage". (can be done with robux)

PE spending options at kisuke:
  1. All of Your Product Essences: This option uses all your PE. (Not Recommended)
  2. Half of Your Product Essences: This option uses half your PE.
  3. One Product Essence: This option is the most conservative, using a single PE.

Different types of rerolls

  • Res/Shikai/Schrift/Fullbring Element reroll 1 PE/200 robux
  • Clan reroll 1 PE
  • Weapon colour/style reroll 0.2 PE
  • Shikai/Res weapon/Schrift bow 0.3 PE
  • Main/secondary rei colour reroll 0.3 PE
  • Hair/eye colour reroll 0.2 PE
  • Face/name reroll 0.2 PE
  • Face marking reroll 0.5 PE
  • Spirit name reroll 0.3 PE
  • Spirit realm reroll 0.2 PE
  • Vrage body reroll 0.1 PE
  • Vizard/Arrancar mask reroll 0.3 PE
  • Bankai/Segunda/Res outfit reroll 0.3 PE
  • Bankai/Spirit phrase reroll 0.15 PE
  • Res animation reroll 0.15 PE
  • Volt wings/wings colour reroll 0.3 PE

Trivia

  • Other than element rerolls, all rerolls follow a system of 1 PE = 100 robux.
  • Elements such as Amazon or Glacier have their own reroll page to change their cosmetics.
  • Rerolls can also be obtained through the ranked Battle tower.
  • Element/Clan rerolls obtained through ranked are race locked. (meaning you NEED to buy them on the race you want)
  • Res animation/weapon rerolls cannot change the starrk res animation/weapon.
