How to Get Segunda Weapon

To unlock your Segunda Weapon in Peroxide, follow the steps below:

Requirements

  • You must be a Hollow that has progressed to Arrancar
  • Reach at least Level 90
  • Earn a total of 420 Points by:
    • Winning Time Gates (75 points each)
    • Losing Time Gates (25 points each)
    • Participating in Time Bubbles, Invasions, or Incursions
    • Defeating Storm Vastocars

The Hollow Prince Trial

  1. Go to Menos Forest and find the Hollow Prince in the ruined building
  2. If you meet the requirements, you can challenge him
  3. Defeat him through six phases, facing Ancient Adjuchas and powerful Arrancar
  4. Upon victory, your Segunda will activate and u will be granted whenever u get 100% of your bar u can press g.
