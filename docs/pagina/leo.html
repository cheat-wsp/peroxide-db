Leo

Outros fonte original

Description

Leo is an Npc found in Hueco mundo, he can be used to reset your stats.

A common misconception is that he is a reskin of Hobo Monk, He is very similar but is not a reskin.

Location

Leo is sat down on the right side of The Alley.
