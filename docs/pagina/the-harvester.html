”Hahahahahahahaha! Back in the world of the living, am I? Yet here we are, surrounded by the dead still! Though, none of you are truly dead after all. You’ve yet to see what comes next! Say, say, bring me a few of those terrible (amazing) mushrooms growing around this city, and I’ll give you some stuff I found in the afterlife! Not very pleasant, it is.”

The harvester little shop can be found in the outskirt of karakura town near the canals. He offers some items for some wungus

Items

  • 150 Yen for 10 Wungus
  • A Clan reroll for 30 Wungus
  • An Element reroll for 30 Wungus
  • An Essence of Halloween for 120 Wungus
  • Bring forth Hell (Spawn Hell verse Ichigo raid boss) for 666 Wungus
  • Turn into a Wungus Man for 7777 Wungus

Cauldron

There is a big metal cauldron filled with a strange liquid right next to the Harvester NPC. Throwing a knocked out person inside rewards you with 10 wungus

Cooldown 12 mins, if you put people in it when it's on cooldown it gives 1 wungus
