Accessories

Outros fonte original


Description

Accessories are wearable items used for attributes, traits, or appearance. You can equip an accessory for stats, use another accessory as vanity, and hide an equipped accessory if you only want the effects.

You can access your Accessory Inventory by clicking the fourth button on the top left of your screen.
