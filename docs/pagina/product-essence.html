Product Essence

Itens fonte original

Description

Product essences (commonly abbreviated as PE) are used to buy rolls at Kisuke or just to reroll by opening the Robux icon and clicking "toggle PE usage".

One of the most valued Currency in the game.

General Tab Option

  • +1 Clan Reroll
  • +5 Name Rerolls.
  • +5 Eye Color Rerolls.
  • +5 Face Rerolls.
  • +5 Hair Color Rerolls.
  • +5 Marking Rerolls.
  • +5 Weapon Color Rerolls.
  • +4 Reiatsu Color Rerolls.
  • 1 Skill Reset for 5 Product Essence.

Path-Specific Tab (Different for all Races )

Soul Reaper :

  • +1 Shikai Element Reroll.
  • +4 Shikai Cosmetic Rerolls.
  • +3 Outfit Rerolls.
  • +3 Bankai Outfit Rerolls.
  • +3 Vizard Mask Rerolls.
  • Reset Kisuke Timer for 2 Product Essence.

Quincy :

  • +1 Schrift Reroll.
  • +3 Vollstandig Wings Rerolls.
  • +3 Vollstandig Wings Color Rerolls.
  • +3 Schrift Bow Rerolls.
  • Reset Kisuke Timer for 2 Product Essence.

Hollow , Adjuchas and Vasto Lorde :

  • +5 Hollow Body Rerolls.
  • +4 Mask Rerolls - Hollow Only.

Arrancar :

  • +1 Resurreccion Reroll.
  • +4 Resurreccion Cosmetic Rerolls.
  • +4 Mask Rerolls.
  • +3 Outfit Reroll Essences.
  • +3 Segunda Weapon Rerolls.
  • +3 Segunda Outfit Rerolls.
  • Reset Kisuke Timer for 2 Product Essence.

Fullbringer :

  • +1 Fullbring Reroll.
  • +3 Fullbring Name Rerolls.
  • Reset Kisuke timer for 2 Product Essence.

Obtainment

  • Afk world.
    • 1 PE every two hours.
    • 1 PE every hour with Roblox Premium.
    • 10 PE if you favorite the game. (One time only)
    • 10 PE if you purchase Roblox Premium within the game. (Monthly)
  • Invasions.
    • 1 PE (Common)
    • 2 PE (Rare)
    • 5 PE (Legendary)
