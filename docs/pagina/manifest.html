The pretty lie that is "equality" is nonsense spouted by weaklings who cannot look upon the darkness. Nothing but an excuse to cover up life's ugliness."

Description

Manifest is one of the Legendary Resurreccions that Arrancar can roll in Peroxide. Players receive a passive buff that spawn a shield, Knocking someone back shoots a Ak-47 that deals extra damage, Down slamming causes a anvil that slams down on them, and gain the ability to use any skill if you counter it in the domain.

(These only work in the Spiralling Abyss of Brilliance)
