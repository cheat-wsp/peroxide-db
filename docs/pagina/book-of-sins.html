Book of Sins

Itens fonte original

Description

The book of sins is a lore book which is 6 chapters long.

The book mentions Sousuke Aizen

It also talks about the Impure Hogyoku and it's effects towards a Vizard.

Chapter 1

After speaking to an odd fellow about the abnormalities in time; the so called “Time Bubbles”, I realized something far past what the man was chasing. For while he hunted this Temporal Reishi that seemed to spill out onto our world, I realized that if these were truly fragments of a world before ours, then greater treasures could be contained within.

Chapter 2

Hueco Mundo is covered in ruins of the old Las Noches, and though its original occupants have long since fled. I have reason to believe the new inhabitants may originate from a Time Bubble themselves, so far they have remained largely dormant… But I don’t believe that will last. Thus I have begun researching this legendary figure they call “Sousuke Aizen” and believe that his greatest creation, the Hogyoku, may too have persisted to this world.. It seems that his creation however has been corrupted through the twisting of time… As the Impure Hogyoku has been sighted within these abnormalities in time.

Chapter 3

It’s been dozens of nights since my last entry, I’ve delved into hundreds of these Time Bubbles.. And finally, I found what I was looking for. An Impure Hogyoku, it reacted oddly with one of those.. Masked Shinigami, however the ones around him did not feel a thing? Could it be that the Hogyoku only chooses those fated for it, that it can see the very threads of destiny?

Chapter 4

Nine nights have passed since I last slept, looking into the Unique masked shinigami and his reaction to the Impure Hogyoku, it seems to me that certain people are just born… More worthy than others, no.. More.. Cursed.. Like the world has a job for them, and they aren’t allowed to escape their duty…

Chapter 5

I found him, that special shinigami, I defeated him.. And took him, the hogyoku just merged within him, the last thing i saw was a hole emerge in his chest inside of my lab, everything, it was destroyed. I have lost everything… EVERYTHING…. AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAH!!!

Chapter 6

HEARTBEATS…. HEARTBEATS… ALL BLAND HAHAHAHAHAH ALL BLAND… ONLY THAT ONE… THAT ONE WAS NOT BLAND. IT WAS SO BEAUTIFUL… I WANT TO HEAR IT ONCE MORE…. BUT NOT EVEN THE STRONG HAVE IT? IS IT SOMETHING OF FATE TO AQUIRE?
