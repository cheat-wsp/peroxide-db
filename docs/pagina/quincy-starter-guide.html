Quincy Progression Guide: Part I [Becoming a Quincy]

The first step is to become a Quincy is by talking to Gunther NPC. By using your reiatsu compass, find the following symbol/icon (on the right); follow it to find a tunnel like area with maxwell and an NPC.[Gunther Location].

Quincy Progression Guide: Part II [Quincy Device]

Players can activate the device that Quincy uses by pressing the N key when they are not engaged in the battle to change their skill binds.

Quincy Progression Guide: Part III [Schrift]

The next step of the Quincy Progression obtaining your own Schrift. To obtain the Schrift as a Quincy, first reach level 15+ and talk to Yhwach (who can be found sitting on his throne in the Castle of the Wandenreich). After that, you need to finish the hunt by using the Bounty Medallion item and then talk to him again.

An alternative and easier way to get your schrift is by doing Invasions (any rank will do), and after each invasion, talk to Yhwach.

After completing your Bounty or Invasion, talk to Yhwach again, and you may obtain Schrift (there is a 1/4 chance of getting Schrift, which increases with each fail).

You change your schrift weapon into a greatsword, sword, claw, cutlass or a gun by talking to Johan and giving her a strange ore in Wandenreich.

Quincy Progression Guide: How To Get Bounty Medallion In Peroxide?

Players can buy Bounty Medallion from the general’s shop.  A shop that can be found in the Wandenreich. To find it, go left after entering the kingdom. You will need 150¥ to buy Bounty Medallion. 100¥ for Quincy Hat.

The Bounty Medallion is a peculiar contraption utilized by Quincies to initiate the Hunt, enabling them to track and pursue adversaries of the Wandenreich, be they Soul Reaper or Hollow PLAYERS. Engaging in this Hunt yields valuable experience points to the hunter. However, should the hunter’s endeavor prove unsuccessful, the intended target is instead granted the reward of experience points.

Furthermore, this item has a less-used purpose, as it can be used to obtain a Schrift, but this is NOT recommended as most targets will have noob protection.

Quincy Progression Guide: Part IV

Your next encounter with Yhwach is reserved for when you achieve the noteworthy milestone of reaching Level 90 or beyond as a Quincy. During this interaction, he imparts specific instructions designed to test and affirm your worthiness.

Upon receiving these instructions, your task entails ascending a sizable hill within the Wandenreich and entering the dwelling/house situated at its peak. There, an unforeseen and formidable challenge awaits[Vollstandig boss]. Should you emerge triumphant against these odds, your success will earn you the coveted ultimate power known as Vollstandig.

For Arrancars who have also reached Level 100 or above, engaging in a dialogue with Yhwach opens an alternate path. By doing so, you’re granted the opportunity to transition into a Quincy while retaining the affiliation of an Arrancar clan. This transformation, however, necessitates a positive reputation within the Wandenreich to accomplish.

Quincy Progression Guide: Part V [Vollstandig Guide]

To acquire your Vollstandig, you must prove your mettle to the Quincy King, Yhwach, by vanquishing his formidable minion. Be prepared, as his standards are exceptionally high. The prerequisites for engaging in combat with the Quincy Golem and obtaining Vollstandig are as follows:

1. Achieve Level 90.

2. Accumulate 420 “Points,” which become accessible only after reaching level 85.

3. Obtain points through various means: Successfully completing a Time Gate rewards 75 points. Completing an invasion (Stealing=5, Killing Spree=12, Captain=22). Time Bubbles (Bosses) grant 1 point for each percentage of boss health you damage. Defeating a Storm Vastocar awards 10 points if you contribute the majority of the damage (at least 60%).

Once you fulfill these requirements, initiate a conversation with Quincy King, Yhwach. He will then guide you to the location of his minion, signaling the start of your quest for Vollstandig.

Vollstandig Activation and Features:

By equipping your Schrift, activate your Vollstandig by pressing the ‘G’ key once the gauge on the right side is completely filled.

Vollstandig mode is subject to a time limit, beginning at 33 seconds and extending up to 190 seconds at its maximum. Enhancing this mode’s duration involves dedicated training, which includes employing it frequently or engaging in sparring sessions with the boss.

Upon emerging victorious against the Vollstandig Boss, you will promptly gain access to Vollstandig moves. Furthermore, you can refine and improve these moves by utilizing the mode extensively or repeatedly overcoming the Boss.

Vollstandig Mode Benefits:

While in Vollstandig mode, your character’s base stats receive substantial enhancements:

– Strength is augmented by 20% (1.2x).

– Spirit is heightened by 30% (1.3x).

– Vitality receives a 25% boost (1.25x).

Quincy Progression Guide: Part V [Vollstandig Boss]

Upon reaching level 90+ and following Yhwach’s directives, the Quincy player ventures into the enigmatic house, only to encounter a seemingly inert Stone Robot. As the player enters the room, the Ancient Automaton springs to life, its form infused with vibrant Reishi, setting the stage for an epic confrontation.

Phase 1 unfolds with unexpected challenges, as the Ancient Automaton proves more formidable than anticipated. It unleashes Reishi-fueled blasts that illuminate the entire arena, their destructive paths leaving nothing unscathed. You quickly deduce that conventional attacks are ineffective against the Automaton, realizing that the key to inflicting damage lies in parrying its energy blasts and countering with powerful strikes. After an arduous battle, the Ancient Automaton succumbs, and its supply of Reishi is finally depleted. Is this the conclusion?

TIP: If you are taking too much damage, reset your stats at Hobo Monk you'll need 8000¥ and put at least 160 on Vitality. You can also hide in a corner since the boss does move around quite alot, but when facing you in a corner, its movement will be VERY limited. It'll help you focus on parrying as the attack can only come from one direction.

Phase 2 marks the ultimate trial. An opened door ushers you into another chamber, where you confront another Stone Figure. Unlike its predecessor, this figure lacks flashy abilities, and conventional attacks can damage it. It refrains from saturating the arena with Reishi blasts. As you near victory, Yhwach himself intervenes, sacrificing imprisoned Soul Reapers to replenish the Stone Figure’s Reishi, triggering a transformation into Vollstandig. Empowered by the stolen Reishi, the Stone Figure heals and uses the player's Vollstandig ability.

This moment signifies the commencement of the true battle, a climactic clash that will determine the victor.

Side note: If your rank is S- or higher, there is a checkpoint after phase 1.
