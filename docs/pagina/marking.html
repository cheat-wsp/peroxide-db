Marking

Outros fonte original

In the game, players can customize their characters with markings, which are purely cosmetic. These markings do not affect gameplay or stats but serve as a way for players to personalize their appearance. To obtain different markings, players can use face marking rerolls in the reroll shop by using 50 Robux, or by using 0.5 Product Essence.
