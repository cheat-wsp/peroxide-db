Clans

Organizações fonte original

Clans in Peroxide act as a way to buff the players stats or even grant a special ability. These stat buffs vary depending on the clan you attain. Most Legendary clans grant the player an array of stat buffs along with a special clan skill.

If you didn't get the clan you desire you can reroll it for 100 or with 1 Product Essence by talking to Kisuke Urahara. You can also earn free rerolls from the Battle Tower.

Trivia

  • Some Clans change your appearance (male and female faces differ)
  • Aizen makes your eyes dark purple with a white iris.
  • Zaraki changes your face completely.
  • Jaegerjaquez changes your eyes and light blue eyeshadow markings.
  • Cifer changes your eyes and cyan tear markings.
  • Kurosaki makes you have ginger hair.
  • Yuji gives the face of popular anime character from Jujutsu Kaisen, Yuji Itadori.
  • Haschwalth gives the access to Yellow colored hair.
