The 2024 New year's event

Description

The NYE event had 2 parts:

- a special 'raid'

- present rain

Presents will rain in karakura town every 25-55 minutes. These usually are green presents, rarely being blue or red.

You can combine 10 presents of the same color to make a better one. Talk to saint peroxilas to do so (the new npc residing in the canal).

Unwrapping these presents may give you weapon skins, accessories and sometimes unlock a new variant for a skill!
