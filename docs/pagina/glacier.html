Description

Glacier is one of the Legendary Shikai elements that is based on a mix of the ice Shikai used by Tōshirō Hitsugaya the captain of 10th division and Jin Kisaragi from the Blazblue series

You can alter the appearance of your ice through rerolls by selecting the Robux icon and then selecting "Change Reroll Options" (0.25 Product Essence for each roll)

It is worth nothing that Glacier have very wordy skills, with lot of texts, so take the time to read them to understand them. This page attempts to simplify them, but a lot is still going on.
