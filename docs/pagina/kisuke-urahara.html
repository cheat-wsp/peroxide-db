Kisuke Urahara

Features

In Peroxide, Kisuke has a variety of functions that can benefit all players, even if they are not Soul Reapers.

You can become a Soul Reaper by interacting with Kisuke as a regular human or as a lost soul.

All players, Aside from hollows, etc: Soul Reaper, Arrancar, Fullbringer and Quincy players can fight Kisuke for the cost of 40. If you win, he will upgrade one of your stats. You can choose what stat do you want to upgrade before getting into the actual fight. The Kisuke NPC has a 30 minute timer everytime you defeat him. He gives 3 stars max. Each star makes the stat it applies to get a +33% experience boost.

He can also drop Kisuke's Hat, Kisuke's Cloak and Kisuke's Outfit if you defeat him.

Upon having a full Soul Gourd, you can exchange the souls it has inside for Yen, upgrading a stat or getting reputation for Soul Society.

Kisuke also has a series of chores for players under level 50, that will increase their level, potential and upgrade one of their stats once completed. Quests vary in difficulties and rewards, from killing all types of hollows to delivering packages and cleaning plastic.

Another feature the Kisuke NPC has is to exchange product essences for stuff like rerolling pretty much everything (mode element, name, clan, outfit, weapon skin, etc.) or restarting his fight timer. But he might scam you if you roll your element.

Location

Kisuke's location is in the Karakura map, specifically at Outer Karakura. If you go to the left after getting there, you should eventually find an odd-looking house. Trivia

  • Kisuke spamming Kido moves on the player is true to source material as in the anime/manga, Kisuke is a master in Kido, capable of unleashing Hado 91: Senju Koten Taiho and Hado 99: Goryu Tenmetsu, as well as perform a modified version of Byakurai.
  • Kisuke's supposed Shikai element is Benihime, capable of producing and manipulating a crimson-colored energy to perform a variety of techniques. Urahara can use these techniques by declaring the proper verbal command that corresponds to Benihime's different abilities.
    • Kisuke's Bankai is called Kannonbiraki Benihime Aratame, which possess the ability to "restructure anything it touches" both offensively and supportively. Offensively, it can split open whatever is within its area of effect as if cutting with a knife. This "splitting" is similar to the act of dissecting something by cutting it open. Supportively, it can restructure something by stitching it back together.
