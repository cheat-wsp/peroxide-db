Arrancar

Hollow fonte original

Description

The Arrancar types vary in power depending on when they remove their mask, with Menoscar being the weakest and Vastocar being the strongest. When you turn into an Arrancar, you will gain a Ressurección, later on you can unlock your Resurrección Weapon and Segunda Etapa.

Check out the Hollow Starter Guide to learn how to progress as a Hollow to become an Arrancar.

Menoscar

Obtainment

Obtained by pulling your mask off as a Menos by pressing G (pc) LSB (Xbox) and doing the arrow mini game.

Buffs

  • +13 HP bonus
  • +20% EXP buff
  • A sharp, cone-shaped nose which is said to posses a strong Hierro, according to the game's splash texts
  • Buffs are much worse than Adjuchar or Vastocar.

Debuff

  • You will be bullied.

Adjucar

You can also get the skill highspeed regen if you put stats into spirit

Obtainment

Obtained by pulling your mask off as an Adjuchas by pressing G (pc) LSB (Xbox) and doing the arrow mini game.

Buffs

  • 1.05x stat multiplier
  • +40% EXP buff
  • +25 HP bonus
  • +5 more levels to their Max Potential (196)
  • Tip: It is advised to stop at Adjuchar if you plan to go Quincycar, as the only buffs you will keep are the HP bonuses and extra levels, and is not worth the hassle. However, a Vastocar Quincy is technically the strongest type of Quincy, due to the fact which levels slightly boost stats and Vastocars have 10 extra levels.

Vastocar

Obtainment

Obtained by pulling your mask off as a Vasto Lorde by pressing G (pc) LSB (Xbox) and doing the arrow mini game.

Advice: It is recommended to obtain the Vasto Lorde exclusive skills (Cero Oscuras) before taking off your mask because you will keep them after becoming a Vastocar (upgrades on skills are also saved when taking off your mask & this will not prevent you from getting those skills after resetting your skills).

Buffs

  • 1.13x stat multiplier
  • +60% EXP boost
  • +50 HP bonus
  • +10 more levels to their Max Potential (201)
  • Access to the Vasto Lorde exclusive skills: Cero Oscuras and High-Speed Regeneration (previous arrancars may still access High-Speed-Regeneration).
  • Can obtain arankuda (Gain access to the Arrancar Hakuda skill set by winning Orochi in a fight)

Arrow Minigame Tips

  • If you are on mobile and find the arrow game challenging, Use the joystick to input directions—unlike on PC, mistakes don’t seem to be counted. By rotating the thumbstick in continuous circles, you can progress effortlessly.
