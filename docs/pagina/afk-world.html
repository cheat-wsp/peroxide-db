[]You can enter this through the Main menu or being AFK in any world.

The AFK World gives 1 Product Essence every two hours. It also prevents you from disconnecting from being afk.

Purchasing Roblox Premium through the game will instantly reward you with 10 Product Essence, aswell as shortening the timer to 1 Product Essence every one hour. if you have premium it will give more rewards.
