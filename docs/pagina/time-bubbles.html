Time Bubbles

Outros fonte original

Raids are bosses from the past coming back to challenge the players. Beware they are extremely strong opponents. Raids appear as a huge beams of energy coming from the sky.

The huge beams of light spawn in one of few location around the map every 30 - 45 min and despawn around 5 minutes later so beware, you can also summon one using Time Splitter, or a distorted raid using a Charged Time Splitter. Naturally spawned ones will cause a server message saying: An immense spiritual pressure etc. If a 'glass breaking' sound effect occurs during the message, it means an Aizen Raid has spawned, if mexican like music its Ulquiorra.

It is important to note that Time Bubbles only naturally appear in Karakura Town. Time Bubbles in Hueco Mondo and Soul Society will only appear via the usage of a (Charged) Time Splitter.

To join a Raid you need to be at least level 15 or above and level 80+ for Distorted Raids.(NOTE: YOU NEED TO HAVE NOOB PROTECTION OFF)

They give extra rewards if the player and his friends are able to beat the Raid in under 7 minutes.

Raids are also guaranteed to drop Time Relics, which can be traded for the magical Gems called Reiatsu Crystals at the Shady Shingami.

NOTE: Don't try to join in 2 separate raids at a time, doing at your own risks

Distorted Raids

Distorted Raids are raids that can only be triggered using a Charged Time Splitter, and are more difficult versions of regular raids.

Distorted Raids appear as a black and white wormhole floating in the sky.

Drop rates for these raids are also increased as well as Time Relics.

The current raids we have are:

KARAKURA TOWN/MENOS FOREST

  • Aizen Raid
  • Ulquiorra Raid

SOUL SOCIETY

  • Yamamoto Raid
  • Byakuya Raid

HUECO MUNDO

  • Nnoitra raid
  • Starrk Raid
