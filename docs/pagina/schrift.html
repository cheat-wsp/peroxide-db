How do I get my Peroxide Schrift?

To get your Peroxide Schrift you must be level 15 or higher after that you must talk to Yhwach in Wandenreich and go on the hunt for the soul reaper you are sent to kill in Karakura town. (you will need a bounty medallion for this you can buy it from the generals shop for 150 yen) If you don't speak with Ywach before your hunt you will be killed and sent back to Wandenreich After you kill the soul reaper you are sent back to Wandenreich, where you will talk to Yhwach again. You have a 30% chance to get Schrift first try, so if he says ¨pathetic¨, you will die and need to hunt another soul reaper. The chances of you getting schrift increase by 10% every time you die to Yhwach. If he says "..." that means you have obtained your schrift.

You can also do invasions and talk Yhwach with the same chance of getting Schrift

How do I use my Peroxide Schrift?

Once you gain your Schrift from Yhwach You activate your Schrift by pressing G after filling up the bar on the right to atleast 20%. But once you gain B rank you can use this transformation for as long as you please with 0% bar.
