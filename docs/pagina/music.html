Music Segunda

"TURN UP THE VOLUME! BECAUSE THIS IS A FUNERAL FOR THE LIVING!"

Description

Music is one of the common Spirit Resurrecciónes that Arrancar can roll. Players receive passive buffs in Segunda Etapa, that buff depends on the color of the notes under your feet.

Red: +50% to Strength and Spirit (Additive)

Blue: Constant Reiatsu regen.

Green: +100% to Health Regen rate (Additive) +50% to Vitality (Additive)

Purple: 10% chance to proc most crystal's effect on most sources

The color of the notes changes when you dash.
