Karakura

Karakura is the starter map where every player begins it's journey.

NPCs

Within the Karakura map, there is a variety of NPCs with different purposes, such as attacking you, giving you quests, boosting your stats, etc. These include:

·Kisuke ·Herupa ·Nurse Ari ·Hollows (Regular, Red-eye, Menos and Adjuchas) ·samsudin

·Shady Shinigami ·Hobo Monk ·Shady Arrancar ·The Trinigami ·Gym Bouncer ·Ippo ·Coach Kamogawa ·Gargan ·Shukuro Tsukishima ·Fluffy ·Gunther ·fusionfriends

Locations

Karakura has a few different locations that you can access by walking around such as the Bridge, Outer Karakura, Main City, etc. This map is decently large, and since lots of new people are there, it is the only place with a teleport feature to move around quicker. This feature is known as the Bus Stop.
