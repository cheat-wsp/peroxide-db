CampoValor
Bankai

"Thunder and lightning are my allies. It is in this rain that I can display the special powers that make me truly great!"

Description

Lightning is one of the Legendary Shikai elements that Soul Reapers can roll in Peroxide. Lightning Bankai's passive grants the user two customs counters and an [E] Ability. The first counter functions normally; if you counter an opponent while they are attacking your block, you will launch them into the air, suplexing them into the ground. The second counter, similar to Lightning's legendary counterpart, Glacier, activates when you counter while nobody is attacking you. In this state, you assume a defensive posture, and when someone strikes you during this stance, you will suplex them as before, but with much greater force, sending them soaring higher.

Pressing or holding down the [E] key in Bankai allows you to dash through opponents, inflicting damage upon them. Achieving full mastery of both your Shikai and Bankai abilities will transform your Lightning into a vivid shade of purple (your bankai form's lightning effects will also turn from yellow to purple after achieving max mastery).
