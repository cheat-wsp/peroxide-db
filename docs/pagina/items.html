Items

Itens fonte original

Description

Items are one of the main mechanics in Peroxide, much like any other Roblox game, allowing various gameplay changes, from cosmetics or keepsakes to flat upgrades to your character.

Unless stated otherwise, items are droppable, with a 5-second cooldown on dropping like all other items (items currently can not be dropped due to duping issues).
