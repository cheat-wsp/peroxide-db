Wandenreich

This is map is the home of Quincies, also the place where they can obtain Schrift and Volstanding. (Quincies can teleport here using their ability Schatten.)

It is also the place where Arrancars can become Quincycars by talking to Yhwach with positive reputation (100+) and Level 100.

The General's Shop

A shop found right after entering the walls of Wandenreich and taking a left, also the place where you can start invasions. Following things are sold here:

The Bounty Medallion for 150¥. (Used for hunts)

The Quincy Hat for 100¥. (Note: You must have a positive reputation to buy the Quincy Hat.)

A Cake for 20¥.

Cybernetics

You need to be a level 60 Quincy or a Quincycar and a cybernetic part to be able to use a Cybernetic. talk to Johan once you meet the requirements.


Wandenreich Dojo

The Wandenreich Dojo membership costs 250¥ and can train all of your stats up to 30, and can train star bonuses up to 5 stars.


Yhwach

Yhwach is a NPC that can be found in the wandenreich he serves multiple purposes.

Yhwach is used for gaining both Schrift and Vollstanding for Quincies as he gives the quest for both of them.

Yhwach is also the npc that turns arrancar into the hybrid race quincycar for more information please visit the Yhwach article.



Vollständig

A building that features a cannon turret on top of it with two Sternritters guarding it. By entering this place as a level 90+ Quincy with 420 points after talking to Yhwach, you are able to fight the Automation to unlock Vollständig. For more information check the main article.
