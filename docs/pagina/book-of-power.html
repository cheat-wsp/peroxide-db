Book of Power

Itens fonte original

Description

The Book of Power is a lore book which is 3 chapters long.

It seems to talk about a Gotei 13 captain's time as a captain and their findings.

it also mentions reiatsu crystals, and vizard

Small summary of each chapter

  • Chapter 1 is just a disclaimer saying that its not meant for people with the goal of power.
  • Chapter 2 talks about things that happen behind the scenes of the Gotei 13 and possibly mentions Reiatsu Crystals OR Ultimate Crystals as "cursed gems of power"
  • Chapter 3 talks about the writer seeing multiple shinigamis in the soul society's forest, referring to them as "masked ones" (possibly Vizards)

Location

To the left of the soul society entrance.

Chapter 1

An introduction, I want to give you a quick disclaimer this book is not meant for those with the goal of power, but those lost in the journey for it, I have noted my findings in my short time as a Gotei 13 Captain.

Chapter 2

Things that happen behind the scenes are disturbing to say the least, though we have been told to let higher ups know whenever we find a rare artifact from the time abnormalities, many shinigami tend to keep it to themselves, especially those cursed gems of power.

Chapter 3

I caught sighting of multiple shinigami within the forest, it was disturbing to say the least, they were masked ones…. But they had no spec of sanity, I could hear them talking about killing those of raw instinct and power, that was the last I saw of them, as they opened a senkaimon and left.
