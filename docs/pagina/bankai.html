Bankai

Shinigami fonte original

Bankai is an end-game mode for Soul Reapers and is the second mode available to them.

How to get Bankai

To unlock your Bankai form, you must once again defeat your Inner Spirit, you must once again defeat your Inner Spirit — a more difficult task than before. Here are the requirements for engaging in this battle and attaining Bankai:

  • 1. Attain Level 85.
  • 2. Accumulate 420 “Points”. - Obtain points through different means:
    • Time Gates: You receive 75 points if your team wins and 25 points if your team loses.
    • Time Bubbles (Bosses): You get 1 point for each percentage of the boss's health you damage.
    • Storm Vastocars: Defeating a Storm Vastocar gives 10 points if you contribute the majority of the damage (at least 60%)
    • Invasions:
      • 5 points for "Steal an Object of Interest"
      • 12 points for "Killing Spree"
      • 22 points for "Slay a Leader"


Once you meet all prerequisites, press ‘N’ to meditate and converse with your Inner Spirit. Afterward, you'll battle your Bankai Boss, and must win to obtain your Bankai.

NOTE: Your Bankai Boss copies your stats. A glass cannon will fight a glass cannon, a tank will fight a tank, etc.

Peroxide Bankai Guide: How To Activate Bankai?

After equipping your Shikai, press ‘G’ once the gauge on the left side is entirely filled. Bankai mode has a time limit starting at 33 seconds, which can be extended to 190 seconds through training to max your Bankai. This includes:

  • Frequent usage of Bankai
  • Sparring with your Bankai spirit in your Inner World

After defeating the Bankai Boss, you’ll immediately unlock Bankai Moves, which can also be upgraded through usage and sparring.

Bankai Benefits

When in Bankai, your base stats receive major buffs:

  • Strength: +20% (1.2x)
  • Spirit: +30% (1.3x)
  • Vitality: +25% (1.25x)

BAN-KAI!!

Bankai Boss

Phase 1:

Your sword is temporarily taken, and a bunch of swords fall from the sky. You must identify the real one by breaking seven decoy swords first.

Tip: Use a vitality-heavy build for this phase—it takes time.

If you're S- Rank or higher, a checkpoint activates after Phase 1.

Phase 2:

Now you must fight your Inner Spirit at full power, including its own Bankai. Expect a tough fight.

Unless you max Strength and rush it — then it becomes a “who hits first wins” battle. Using Wind is also recommended.

Bankai Mastery

To maximize your Bankai duration, you need to defeat your Bankai boss 13 times. To fight the boss and increase your mastery, meditate by pressing ‘N’ (you should know this by now smh), then talk to your Inner Spirit and select ‘I wish to spar’ and ‘I wish to hone my skill (XP & Mastery)’. You’ll know you have mastered your Bankai when the outline of your mastery bar turns purple. This might take some time.

For an easier fight, you can set your stats to 0 Vitality, 0 Spirit, and 490 Strength to defeat the boss quickly if you’re fast with a one-shotting combo. Alternatively, you can use 490 Vitality, 0 Spirit, and 0 Strength if you prefer an easier but lengthier fight.

The first option is WAY better for quickly defeating your Bankai boss, while the second is better for surviving without dying, at the cost of taking forever...

Also, using Bankai over 1000 times will apparently grant mastery, but this method has currently NOT been proven and is NOT recommended.

How to Easily Cheese Your Bankai Boss (Acquisition & Mastery)

Strength Build Strategy:

Requirements:

  • S- rank
  • All the points in strength (THAT MEANS NONE IN SPIRIT OR VITALITY! NONE! NADA!)

Moves: Slash barrage, any shikai or normal combo extender (such as fire's uppercut or cyclone), Hado 11 (the lightningpassive). You can also add more skills if you'd like, but those are the main ones.

Crystals:

  • Artificiality (PvE dmg)
  • Ichor (lifesteal)
  • Creep (basically reiatsu steal)

Accessories: Anything that gives you strength, PvE Damage or optionally vitality.

Execution:

To cheese the Bankai boss using a strength build with a light weapon, start by waiting near his spawn until he finishes his intro dialogue. Once he’s done, bait out his evasive move by hitting him with M1 attacks. After he uses his evasive, bait him into attacking you—either perfect block his hit or let him walk into your M1s. Continue M1-ing him until he enters Bankai. As soon as the I-frames from his transformation wear off, begin your combo: perform 4 M1s, use a combo extender (preferably Fire Uppercut), follow up with 4 more M1s, and then finish with Slash Barrage.

If executed correctly, this combo will deal around 50% to 80% of his health. While this method may take multiple attempts and some patience, it’s a reliable strategy for defeating the Bankai boss with a pure strength build.
