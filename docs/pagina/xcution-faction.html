Xcution

Xcution is one of Peroxides biggest factions having about 9.3k Roblox players in their group and 6.3k Players in their discord server.

Xcution is based off the group of Fullbringers from the anime Bleach meaning that most if not all members are going to be Fullbringers in Peroxide.

Floors are groups within Xcution, there are 8 floors:

  • Floor of Natural Sciences
  • Floor Of Art
  • Floor Of Technological Sciences
  • Floor Of Philosophy
  • Floor Of Religion
  • Floor Of History
  • Floor Of Literature
  • Floor Of Natural Sciences

There is a PVP based Floor you can join called Floor Of General Works (FOGW for short) that has ranked and skill requirements.

Similar to divisions Gotei Xcution has floors for each floor there is usually a Proxy, 2 Sephirah and an Index, if there isn't a Proxy than the Index would lead the floor, if there isn't an index a Sephirah could be promoted to fill the mission position, or another Hr (proxy+) can take over the floor until a new proxy is chosen.
