Urahara's Shop

Locais fonte original


Description

Urahara's Shop is located in Karakura Town, specifically at Outer Karakura and is an important location for all progressions alike. Kisuke Urahara resides here, with 2 Job Boards and Herupa standing outside. Gargan is also situated outside the shop by the wall, and Yoruichi also resides in the side room on the right of the shop, where you are able to buy a Soul Gourd for 25. You also able to buy Soul Drinks, which give a boost in exp, for 10.

Notes

  • The shop is where the player is able to fight the Dueler Kisuke as well as get jobs and small tasks from him till level 50, which is essential for progression starting, easy stat increases and limitbreaking.
  • You can also spend Product Essence in the Shop, and trading in your Soul Gourd for stat increases, reputation, and money.
