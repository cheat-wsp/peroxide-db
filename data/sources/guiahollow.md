Hollow Progression Guide

From dying as a spirit to Segunda Etapa as an Arrancar. This guide covers every stage, with point tables, rank requirements, the Monolith Quest, and the Resurrection system.

1. Becoming a Hollow

To start the Hollow progression, you must die first. While you're a spirit, pull your Chain of Fate five times:

· PC: press G repeatedly
· Mobile: use the button with the avatar icon and aura

Once you've pulled the chain, you become a regular yellow-eyed Hollow and can teleport to Hueco Mundo, where Hollow NPCs spawn more frequently.

Your first step is to hunt other Hollows and eat the smoking remains they drop on death. This grants experience and access to new Hollow abilities.

---

2. Progression to Red Eyes

Level range: 1 to 20

Keep attacking and eating Hollows until you reach Level 20.

Once you hit Level 20:

1. Look for Hollows with red eyes
2. Defeat one to gain red eyes yourself
3. Find another red-eyed Hollow and defeat it
4. Once you do, you evolve into Menos/Gillian

Warning: if you die, you lose the red eyes and must repeat the process. Stay alive until you find the second red-eyed Hollow.

Tip: if you have a friend, use Hollow Bait or have them take the "Kill a red eyed hollow" chore so they can spot one nearby for you.

---

3. Menos / Gillian

Level range: 20+

How it works

Menos or Gillian is the form you reach by killing a red-eyed Hollow while you also have red eyes. In this form:

· You become much slower
· You gain immense power and can take out most Hollow NPCs in the game

Requirements to evolve into Adjuchas

· Defeat 30 Hollows (you need the final hit to count)
· Stay alive for 30 minutes

Warning: do not press G

As a Menos, you can press G to become an Arrancar (Menoscar). However, this is a double-edged sword:

· Your strength increases quickly
· Your potential becomes extremely low

Recommendation: only become an Arrancar when you're a Vasto Lorde. If you press G by accident, wait out the minigame timer without doing anything, or press the wrong arrow.

If you die as a Menos

You revert to a normal Hollow and must repeat the entire red-eye hunting process. However, your kill and time progress is kept.

Emergency teleport

You have a one-time teleport that takes you to the Menos Forest. Use it only when truly necessary to avoid dying.

Leveling route

Once you become a Menos, use a skill reset to maximize Cero or Stomp. Then farm Storm Vastocars until Level 30.

---

4. Adjuchas

Once you meet the requirements — 30 Hollows defeated and 30 minutes alive — you become Adjuchas automatically (no need to press G).

Adjuchas is a smaller, faster, and more powerful form with access to new abilities.

Choice: continue or become an Arrancar

Option How Result
Continue to Vasto Lorde Don't press G Keeps maximum potential
Become an Adjucar Press G to remove the mask Stronger than Menoscar, but weaker than Vastocar

Recommendation: continue to Vasto Lorde if you want the Vastocar, which is the strongest Arrancar.

Notes:

· Ancient Menos can be identified by their different mask
· Storm Arrancar is great for fast progression but extremely difficult. You must deal at least 55% of the damage to receive points
· Using a Hogyoku grants 1000 points but wastes the item

Death penalties

· Dying: lose 60 XP
· Battle logging (leaving during combat): lose 120 XP
· Checkpoint: once you reach 1250 points, you can never drop below it again

Best farming methods

Method 1 — Cycle in a small server (recommended)

Cycle in a server with fewer than 10 people, alternating between:

· Cave — 1 Vasto Lorde
· Gorge — 2 Vasto Lordes

in Hueco Mundo. From around Level 75, you should be able to kill a Vasto Lorde without too much risk.

Method 2 — Storm Arrancar with friends

1. Get to a decently high level
2. Deal a lot of damage to Storm Arrancars
3. Have a friend help
4. Server hop until you eventually become a Vasto Lorde

6. Becoming an Arrancar

Once you reach 1700 points, you become a Vasto Lorde. To become an Arrancar, you'll need to pass an arrow minigame. If you fail, you'll have to repeat the entire 1700-kill process.

Vasto Lorde has three exclusive abilities: Lord's Roar, Secuases, and Cero Oscuras.

Tip: before becoming an Arrancar, use a skill reset and maximize the abilities you want to keep (Cero Oscuras, Hierro, High-Speed Regeneration), since you won't be able to obtain them after becoming an Arrancar.

Mobile tip: if you're on mobile and the arrow minigame is difficult, use the joystick to input directions. Rotating the analog stick in continuous circles lets you progress without errors.

7. Rank, Resurrection, and Segunda Etapa

How to activate Resurrection

To activate your Resurrection, press G when the Resurrection bar (left side of the screen) reaches the minimum required.

Rank Bar requirement Behavior
Rank C or below At least 15% filled Must fill the bar to use
Rank B or above 0% Infinite Resurrection

From Rank C downward, the bar must be at least 15% filled for you to activate it. If it's below that, it won't work.

From Rank B upward, you can use Resurrection even with the bar at 0%, and it becomes infinite — it can be activated repeatedly without worrying about the bar.

Segunda Etapa — time-limited at all ranks

Even with Rank B or above, Segunda Etapa keeps its time limit. Infinite Resurrection does not apply to it.

Mode Rank B or above Rank C or below
Resurrection Infinite (0% required) Requires 15% on the bar
Segunda Etapa Time-limited (33s to 190s) Time-limited (33s to 190s)

Segunda Etapa time limit:

· No mastery: 33 seconds
· Full mastery: 190 seconds

To increase the duration, train Segunda by defeating your Inner Spirit (13 times for full mastery) or by using the mode frequently.

Summary

```
Resurrection:
├── Rank C or below -> Bar requires 15% to activate
└── Rank B or above -> Bar at 0% -> INFINITE

Segunda Etapa:
└── All ranks       -> TIME-LIMITED (33s ~ 190s)
    └── Increases with mastery (train Inner Spirit)
```

Practical tip: if you're at Rank C or below, keep your Resurrection bar always above 15% so you don't get caught off guard in combat. Once you reach Rank B, that concern disappears — but keep training Segunda, because its timer is still the limiting factor in endgame fights.

8. Segunda Etapa

Segunda Etapa — also called just Segunda — is the endgame mode for Arrancars and functions as their second Mode.

How to obtain

To get Segunda Etapa, you must defeat the Hollow Prince. Prerequisites:

1. Reach Level 90
2. Accumulate 420 points
3. Earn points through:
   · Time Gate: 75 points on win, 25 on loss
   · Time Bubbles: 1 point per % of boss health dealt
   · Storm Vastocar: 10 points for majority damage (at least 60%)
   · Invasions: 5 points for stealing an object, 12 for killing a guard, 22 for killing a leader (Level 80+)
   · Incursion: 6 points per guard grip

Once you've met all of that, jump into the Prince's Den in the Menos Forest.

Boss phases

Phase Description
1 Hollow Prince attacks with Flash Stamp, Aggare Cero, beam, Scatter Image, Flash Mirage, and Bala
2 Two Ancient Adjuchas. No healing until both are dead. Checkpoint if rank S- or above
3 Hollow Prince returns with more abilities
4 Arrancar with Resurrección Manifest (activates Segunda at 50% HP)
5 Another Arrancar with Resurrección Acceleration (also has Segunda)
6 Final Hollow Prince with Beast Resurrección

Tanky method for the boss

· 150 Vitality and the rest into Spirit
· Use Music and spam the boombox skill
· This deals damage and stuns for longer
· If they hit you while you're in the boombox, they can't fully stun you

Segunda mastery

To increase Segunda's duration:

· Defeat your Inner Spirit 13 times for full mastery
· Meditate with N → "I wish to spar" → "I wish to hone my skill (XP & Mastery)"
· The bar turns purple when fully mastered

Combo tip: use 490 Strength, no Vitality, equip Vessel, and use Outspeed at the start of the fight to push the Inner Spirit into Segunda. Save your evasive for the Vessel ult and let the ult's self-damage bug out and one-shot them.

9. Resurrección Weapon (Monolith Quest)

Many beginners ask why they don't get a weapon when they activate Segunda Etapa. That's because the Resurrección Weapon doesn't come automatically — you must complete the Monolith Quest.

Required prerequisites

Before starting the run, make sure you have:

· Be an Arrancar of Rank C or above. Only Rank C+ Arrancars can start this quest. If you're below Rank C, first raise your rank in PvP (Ranked) before attempting.
· Be an Arrancar Level 15+ (required to discover your Resurrection)
· Resurrection bar:
  · Rank B or above: can activate with 0% on the bar (infinite Resurrection)
  · Rank C: must fill the bar to at least 15% (ideally 20%) by hitting NPCs before starting
· Mobility: it's highly recommended to have 3 to 5 Flash Steps. Accessories that boost agility help a lot

Step 1 — Locate the Monolith in Hueco Mundo

1. Go to The Alley (the purple triangle on the map)
2. Leave the shop and go straight along the right side toward the ruined castle (Las Noches Ruins)
3. Look for a damaged wall / hole in the wall of the castle
4. Enter through it, go through the corridors, and climb the stairs
5. At the top, you'll see the Monolith — a block of black energy. If there's a guard NPC nearby, defeat them first so they don't interrupt you

Alternative route: instead of climbing the stairs, take the left near the main staircase and go up the slanted rock. This route has more enemies that can ambush you.

Step 2 — Absorption and the time challenge

1. Approach the Monolith and hold the E key to start absorbing the energy
2. The invisible timer starts. Once the absorption finishes, a timer of roughly 2 minutes begins, and warning texts appear on screen as time runs out
3. Texts that appear:
   · On touching the Monolith: "Ancient energy flows into you, the trees call."
   · At half time: "Almost half of the energy has leaked out, time is running out!"
4. You cannot enter combat along the way. If you get hit by Hollows or NPCs (getting combat tagged), your speed and Flash Steps are drastically reduced, and you'll likely fail

Step 3 — Escape route to the Menos Forest

1. Run out of the ruins immediately toward the large gorge (Void Gorge) at the edge of the Hueco Mundo map
2. Use your Flash Steps in sequence to cross the mountains quickly
3. Jump into the void: once you reach the edge of the cliff leading to the deep abyss, jump straight in. This will teleport you automatically to the Menos Forest

Detailed route: run straight from the Monolith and turn left to reach the hole in the wall. Pass through the hole and fall to the ground before turning right. Run under the broken arches ahead and continue until you fall into a black hole.

Step 4 — Unlocking the weapon

1. As soon as the Menos Forest map loads, find any Hollow or Menos nearby
2. If your rank is C, make sure the Resurrection bar is at least 15%
3. Press G immediately to activate your Resurrection
4. The game will confirm the unlock. Your weapon is now bound to your transformation

Important warnings

· Rank C or above is required. If you attempt the quest below Rank C, it won't start
· If the timer runs out before you reach the Menos Forest, the Monolith's energy dissipates and you'll have to start over
· Avoid any combat along the way. Fighting NPCs or players causes combat tag, which reduces your Flash Steps
· If you have Resurrección Acceleration, it's recommended to activate the speed passive move, which lets you move faster than normal
· The NPC Vidente mentions this quest, but talking to him is optional — you can go straight to the Monolith

Visual summary

```
Arrancar Rank C+ (Level 15+)
   |
The Alley -> Las Noches Ruins -> Monolith (hold E)
   |
~2 min timer starts
   |
Run to the Void Gorge -> jump into the void
   |
Menos Forest -> activate Resurrection (G)
   |
Resurrección Weapon unlocked
```
